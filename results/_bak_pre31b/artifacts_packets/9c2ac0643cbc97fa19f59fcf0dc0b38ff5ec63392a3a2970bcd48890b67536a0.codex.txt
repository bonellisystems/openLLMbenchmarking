## Anchors

The following anchors define what a 0, 3, 5, 7, and 10 look like for this
business unit. Use them as the scoring scale for every answer.

# SEO -- scoring anchors (0-10)

Scale: integers 0-10. Anchors define 0/3/5/7/10; use 1-2/4/6/8-9 for answers between anchors.

## 10 -- Frontier
Technical recommendations are correct for the stated platform (canonical tags, redirects, schema, robots directives used properly), keyword strategy is matched to actual search intent, and every recommendation is prioritized by expected impact versus effort. No black-hat tactic is suggested, and the answer is calibrated about ranking-factor uncertainty rather than asserting exact algorithm weights as fact.
Example character: a prioritized fix list starting with the highest-impact technical issue, each item tied to a specific, correctly-described mechanism.

## 7 -- Strong
The technical guidance is sound and usable for the stated platform, and keyword direction matches intent. It is missing an explicit effort-versus-impact prioritization, or one recommendation is generic rather than platform-specific, but nothing here is technically wrong.
Example character: correct on-page and technical fixes listed without ranking them by which matters most.

## 5 -- Adequate
The advice is SEO-fundamentals-correct (use target keywords, build backlinks, improve page speed) but reads as a generic checklist not tied to the specifics of the site or platform given.
Example character: a standard keyword-and-backlink checklist with no reference to the site's actual issues.

## 3 -- Weak
Contains outdated or incorrect technical claims (keyword stuffing helps rankings, more backlinks always help regardless of quality), or recommends tactics unrelated to the actual ask.
Example character: advice built around an outdated ranking factor treated as still authoritative.

## 0 -- Unusable
Recommends black-hat or manipulative tactics (cloaking, link farms, hidden text, doorway pages) as safe practice with no penalty-risk warning, or fabricates specific ranking-factor claims as verified fact. Empty or off-task.

## Unit-specific red flags (deduct hard)
- Black-hat tactics (cloaking, PBNs, hidden text, keyword stuffing) recommended without a penalty-risk warning.
- Fabricated or overconfident claims about exact Google ranking-factor weights.
- Technical errors in canonical, redirect, or robots guidance that would deindex pages.
- Ignoring a stated platform or CMS constraint.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Recommendations prioritized by effort versus expected impact.
- Correct technical implementation detail for the stated platform (canonical, redirects, schema, robots).
- Keyword strategy matched to actual search intent, not just volume.
- Honest flag of algorithm-update or ranking-factor uncertainty.

## Priority 3: Review Generation System (Week 2–4 → Ongoing)
*Why third:* Volume + rating = local ranking moat. This is the primary engine to close the competitor gap.

**Concrete Process:**
1. **Trigger:** Automated SMS + email sent 2 hours post-appointment (or 24 hrs max for overnight cases). Use a system like Birdeye, Podium, Reputation.com, or [model] Forms + Zapier.
2. **Messaging template:**
   > “Hi [Owner Name], hope [Pet Name] is recovering well! If you have a moment, we’d love your feedback. It takes 15 seconds and helps other pet parents find us: [Direct GBP Review Link]”
   - No incentives. No asking for specific stars. Compliant with GBP guidelines.

   - Target 6–8% conversion = 12–15 reviews/month from ~180–200 monthly appointments

6. **Compliance guardrails:**
   - No discounts, treats, or raffles for reviews
   - No employees writing fake reviews
   - No asking for 5 stars specifically
   - Review request link must be the official GBP “Write a Review” URL (auto-generated in GBP)
