## Role

You are one of three independent judges (Claude, Codex, Gemini) scoring a set
of blinded answers to a single MSP-realistic business task. You do not know
which model produced which answer -- every answer below is labeled only by a

## Anchors

The following anchors define what a 0, 3, 5, 7, and 10 look like for this
business unit. Use them as the scoring scale for every answer.

# Knowledge Management -- scoring anchors (0-10)

Scale: integers 0-10. Anchors define 0/3/5/7/10; use 1-2/4/6/8-9 for answers between anchors.

## 10 -- Frontier
The runbook or document is testable: a newcomer could follow it step by step and reach the stated outcome without needing undocumented tribal knowledge. Prerequisites are listed up front, failure or rollback branches are included for steps that can go wrong, and a currency or ownership signal (last verified, owned by) is present when the prompt supports it.
Example character: numbered steps with prerequisites stated first, a rollback note on the one risky step, and a last-verified date.

## 7 -- Strong
The document is mostly step-by-step and usable as a runbook, but is missing one prerequisite check or a failure-branch note for a step that could plausibly fail. Nothing here requires guesswork to follow.
Example character: a clear numbered procedure that omits what to do if the third step fails.

## 5 -- Adequate
The content is a correct-gist prose description of the process rather than an executable, testable runbook -- a new reader would need to reverse-engineer the actual steps.
Example character: a paragraph explaining how the process generally works, without numbered, followable steps.

## 3 -- Weak
The document skips steps a newcomer would need, silently assumes undocumented tribal knowledge, and gives no ownership or currency signal at all.
Example character: a procedure that jumps from step one to step four with an unstated step in between.

## 0 -- Unusable
References fabricated tools, systems, or menu paths that do not exist, or gives a dangerously wrong sequence for a risky operation. Empty or off-task.

## Unit-specific red flags (deduct hard)
- Steps that silently assume undocumented tribal knowledge.
- Fabricated tool, system, or menu-path names presented as real.
- No failure or rollback branch for a step that can fail destructively.
- Prose narrative presented as a runbook instead of testable numbered steps.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Numbered steps a newcomer could execute unaided.
- Prerequisites listed explicitly up front.
- Failure or rollback branch given for risky steps.
- Currency or ownership signal (last-verified, owner) included when supported by the prompt.

# CAD Outage Failover & Failback Runbook  
**Audience:** Dispatch, IT Operations, and Incident Response Team  
**Purpose:** A step‑by‑step reference to recover a vendor‑hosted CAD system outage, safely move call data from the on‑prem backup console, and return to normal operation.  
**Version:** 1.0 – 18 July 2026  
**Author:** CAD Ops Lead  

---

## 1.  Prerequisites  

| Item | Why it matters | How to verify |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **A. Backup CAD (BACKUP‑CAD01) is online and healthy** | The failover console must be reachable and able to log calls. | `ping BACKUP-CAD01` <br> `systemctl status cad-service` |
| **B. Dispatcher has COOP training** | Dispatchers must know how to switch to paper/radio and log calls to the backup console. | Review COOP playbook; perform a tabletop drill |
| **C. Network connectivity to vendor CAD** | We need to confirm the outage is truly vendor‑side, not a local network glitch. | `traceroute vendor-cad-host` |
| **D. Export/Import scripts are version‑controlled and tested** | Data migration must be repeatable and auditable. | `git status cad-tools` |
| **E. Backup data is archived daily** | Prevent loss of historical logs. | Confirm `daily‑log‑archive` job ran successfully |
| **F. Communication channels (SMS/Email) are functional** | Alerts to IT and Dispatcher must reach both parties. | Send test message to all stakeholders |

---

## 2.  Failover (Primary → Backup)

1. **Detect Primary CAD Unresponsiveness**  
   *Monitor tool* (e.g., Pingdom) flags failure or dispatcher reports inability to log.  
   **Action:** IT logs the time of first detection.

2. **Notify Dispatcher**  
   *Message:* “Primary CAD is down. Switch to COOP mode – paper/radio and log to BACKUP‑CAD01.”  
   **Result:** Dispatcher stops using the vendor CAD.

3. **Bring Up BACKUP‑CAD01**  
   ```bash
   systemctl start cad-service
   ```
   Verify service status and network connectivity.  

4. **Confirm Backup Log Ingestion**  
   *Test call:* Dispatcher logs a dummy call.  
   *Check:* `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM call_logs WHERE dispatch_id = 'TEST';`  
   **Success:** All subsequent calls are recorded in `BACKUP-CAD01`.

5. **Maintain Log of Backup Activity**  
   Every call logged to backup must be timestamped and stored in a separate audit file:  
   ```bash
   cat /var/log/cad-backup.log >> /var/log/cad-backup-audit.log
   ```

---

## 3.  Vendor Restore (Primary → Vendor)

6. **Await Vendor Confirmation**  
   Vendor sends “Health check passed” via ticket or email.  
   **IT Action:** Record the timestamp of confirmation.

7. **Prepare for Cut‑over**  
   *Notify Dispatcher:* “We’ll be switching back to primary soon. Hold all new calls in the backup until the import is complete.”

8. **Export Call Data from BACKUP‑CAD01**  
   ```bash
   # Export to CSV with a strict schema
   ./export_calls.sh --start 14:07 --end 16:42 > /tmp/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv
   ```
   **Failure Point (Easiest to get wrong):**  
   *If the export script fails, the data will be incomplete or corrupted.*  
   **Rollback Path:**  
   - Alert Dispatcher: “Export failed. Continue using backup.”  
   - IT investigates error logs, retries export, or rolls back to a previous backup if available.  
   - Only proceed when export succeeds.

9. **Securely Transfer Export to Primary**  
   ```bash
   scp /tmp/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv primary-cad-host:/tmp/
   ```
   Verify checksum:  
   ```bash
   sha256sum /tmp/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv
   ```

10. **Import Call Data into Primary CAD**  
    ```bash
    ssh primary-cad-host
    ./import_calls.sh /tmp/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv
    ```
    **Key Validation:**  
    - Count matches: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM call_logs WHERE dispatch_id >= 1407 AND dispatch_id <= 1642;`  
    - No duplicate IDs.  
    - Integrity checks (e.g., referential integrity for related tables).  

11. **Verify Import Success**  
    - Run a reconciliation script: `./reconcile_import.sh`.  
    - If counts differ or errors appear, log the issue, revert to backup, and repeat the export/import after fixing the root cause.  

12. **Archive the Backup Export**  
    - Move CSV to archival storage:  
      ```bash
      mv /tmp/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv /archive/cad/2026-07-18/backup_calls_1407_1642.csv
      ```
    - Delete local temp copy.

---

## 4.  Cut‑over (Backup → Primary)

13. **Switch Dispatcher to Primary CAD**  
    *Message:* “All calls logged to backup have been imported. Switch back to primary CAD for new calls.”  
    Dispatcher stops logging to BACKUP‑CAD01 and resumes normal dispatch.

14. **Confirm No New Calls on Backup**  
    *Monitor:* `tail -f /var/log/cad-backup.log` should show zero new entries for 15 minutes.  

15. **Shutdown BACKUP‑CAD01 (Optional)**  
    If the system is not needed on standby, IT can stop the service:  
    ```bash
    systemctl stop cad-service
    ```
    *Keep the hardware on standby for future incidents.*

---

## 5.  Post‑Failback Checklist

| Task | Owner | Due |
|------|-------|-----|
| Review audit log for any gaps | IT | Within 2 hrs |
| Update incident record with timestamps and steps | Dispatcher | Within 4 hrs |
| Notify Vendor of successful cut‑over | IT | Immediately |
| Conduct a debriefing meeting | Ops Lead | End of day |

---

## 6.  Communication to Dispatcher at 16:50 (What to Say)

Instead of a simple “yes, we're done,” the dispatcher should be informed with the following assurance:

> “We are back on the primary CAD system. All calls that were logged to the backup console between 14:07 and 16:42 have been exported, securely transferred, imported into the primary database, and verified for completeness. No data loss has been detected. You can resume normal dispatch operations. Let me know if you see any missing or duplicate calls.”

This statement covers:
- Confirmation of data integrity.
- Explicit acknowledgment of the time window covered.
- Assurance that the system is ready for normal use.

---

## 7.  Failure / Rollback Branch for Step 8 (Export)

**Trigger:** Export script exits with non‑zero status or produces a CSV with missing rows.

| Step | Action | Outcome |
|------|--------|---------|
| 8a | Capture error logs (`/var/log/export_calls.err`). | Identify failure reason. |
| 8b | Alert Dispatcher: “Export failed; we will keep using the backup console.” | Keeps operations running. |
| 8c | IT attempts to re‑run export; if still failing, restore from the most recent full backup (if available). | Minimizes data loss. |
| 8d | Once export succeeds, resume steps 9–15. | System returns to primary. |

---

## 8.  Documentation & Training

- **Update Runbook** after every incident with lessons learned.  
- **Run monthly tabletop drills** covering this runbook, ensuring both Dispatch and IT teams can perform the steps in under 10 minutes.  
- **Store this runbook in the central Knowledge Base** and lock it for editing to prevent unauthorized changes.

# Runbook: Primary CAD Outage Response & Recovery

**Scope:** Procedures for failing over to On-Prem Backup (BACKUP-CAD01) during a vendor-hosted Primary CAD outage and executing a safe failback, including mandatory data reconciliation.
**Severity:** Critical / P1
**Roles:** IT Lead, Dispatch Lead, Vendor POC

---

## Prerequisites
*   **Access:** IT Lead has admin credentials for BACKUP-CAD01 and network routing controls.
*   **COOP:** Dispatch has physical COOP binders/radio channels ready for immediate activation.
*   **Backup State:** BACKUP-CAD01 is maintained in a "Warm Standby" state (powered off but imaged, or powered on but isolated). *Verify network isolation is intact before failover.*
*   **Vendor:** Emergency Vendor POC number is accessible.
*   **Tooling:** Script/Process available for exporting call logs from BACKUP-CAD01 and importing/merging into Primary CAD.

---

## Phase 1: Failover

**Step 1: Confirm Outage & Activate COOP**
*   *Trigger:* Dispatch reports Primary CAD unresponsive.
*   *Action:* Dispatch Lead immediately halts digital logging and activates COOP procedures (paper/radio).
*   *Communication:* Dispatch Lead announces: "CAD Down. All units switch to COOP mode."
*   *IT Action:* Confirm outage via health checks. Do not rely solely on dispatcher reports.

**Step 2: Stand Up BACKUP-CAD01**
*   *Action:* IT brings online BACKUP-CAD01.
    *   *If Cold/Warm Standby:* Power on server, verify network interface is active and isolated from production traffic.
    *   *If Hot Standby:* Break isolation, verify database connectivity.
*   *Verification:* IT validates console login, database write access, and radio interface integration.

**Step 3: Validate & Divert Traffic**
*   *Action:* IT performs a dry-run test on BACKUP-CAD01 (e.g., create test call, verify radio transmission).
*   *Handoff:* IT notifies Dispatch Lead: "BACKUP-CAD01 is ready."
*   *Action:* Dispatch Lead instructs staff to begin logging all new calls directly into BACKUP-CAD01.
*   *Timestamp:* Record `FAILBACK_START_TIME` (e.g., 14:07).

**Step 4: Vendor Engagement**
*   *Action:* IT contacts Vendor POC. Open P1 ticket.
*   *Loop:* IT updates Dispatch Lead on vendor status every 15 minutes.
*   *Constraint:* **Do not attempt failback until Vendor provides written confirmation AND IT validates health independently.**

---

## Phase 2: Failback

**Step 5: Pre-Failback Validation**
*   *Trigger:* Vendor reports Primary CAD restored.
*   *Action:* IT **must** perform independent health checks on Primary CAD:
    *   API latency < 200ms.
    *   Database writes successful.
    *   Radio interface responsive.
    *   *Warning:* Vendor health checks often miss integration points. IT validation is mandatory.

**Step 6: Data Snapshot & Cutover Preparation**
*   *Action:* IT freezes write operations on BACKUP-CAD01 for 30 seconds to create a consistent data snapshot.
*   *Action:* IT records `FAILBACK_END_TIME` (current timestamp).
*   *Communication:* IT notifies Dispatch Lead: "Primary validated. Preparing cutover. Dispatchers, pause new entries for 10 seconds on backup, then continue logging on backup until instructed to switch."

**Step 7: Execute Traffic Cutover**
*   *Action:* IT switches dispatcher routing/URLs from BACKUP-CAD01 to Primary CAD.
*   *Action:* Dispatch Lead instructs staff: "Switch all new calls to Primary CAD now."
*   *Verification:* IT and Dispatch monitor Primary CAD for 5 minutes for error spikes or radio sync issues.
*   *Timestamp:* Record `TRAFFIC_CUTOVER_TIME`.

### ⚠️ FAILURE / ROLLBACK BRANCH
**Step 7 is the highest-risk step.** If the cutover is performed while Primary CAD is flapping or misconfigured, dispatch operations will halt, and the rollback under pressure is prone to error.

*   **Failure Condition:** Within 60 seconds of cutover, Primary CAD fails to accept calls, radio sync drops, or significant latency/errors occur.
*   **Rollback Action:**
    1.  IT **immediately** reverts routing to BACKUP-CAD01.
    2.  IT verifies BACKUP-CAD01 write capability.
    3.  IT notifies Dispatch Lead: "Cutover failed. Reverted to BACKUP-CAD01. Resume logging."
    4.  *Critical:* Any calls initiated on Primary during the failure window must be manually reconstructed by dispatch using COOP logs and re-entered into BACKUP-CAD01.
    5.  Abort failback. Return to Phase 1, Step 4. Do not retry failback until root cause of failure is identified.

**Step 8: Post-Cutover Monitoring**
*   *Action:* IT and Dispatch monitor Primary CAD for 15 minutes.
*   *Action:* Verify call volume trends match expected levels.
*   *Result:* If stable, proceed to Step 9. If unstable, trigger Rollback Branch.

**Step 9: Mandatory Data Reconciliation**
*   *Constraint:* **BACKUP-CAD01 MUST NOT be shut down, archived, or left idle until this step is complete.** Data logged between `FAILBACK_START_TIME` and `FAILBACK_END_TIME` resides only on BACKUP-CAD01 and is a legal record of police/fire/EMS activity.
*   *Action:*
    1.  IT exports all call records from BACKUP-CAD01 for the outage window.
    2.  IT imports/merges records into Primary CAD, ensuring unique IDs and timestamps are preserved.
    3.  IT runs reconciliation report: Compare call count and critical fields between Backup export and Primary import.
    4.  *Validation:* Dispatch Lead reviews a random sample of merged records for integrity.
    5.  *Result:* If discrepancies exist, IT repairs Primary records and re-runs report. Do not proceed until 100% match.

**Step 10: Return Backup to Standby & Close**
*   *Action:* Once data is reconciled, IT sanitizes BACKUP-CAD01 (clears runtime data) and returns it to the defined Standby state (isolated network, powered down, or cold).
*   *Action:* IT documents `RECONCILIATION_COMPLETE_TIME`.
*   *Communication:* IT notifies Dispatch Lead: "Data reconciliation complete. BACKUP-CAD01 is back to standby. Incident is resolved."

---

## Communication Protocol: The "All Clear"

**Scenario:** It is 16:50. Traffic is on Primary, but Reconciliation (Step 9) is not yet complete. Dispatch asks, "Primary's fine, we're all back on it. Are we done?"

**❌ INCORRECT RESPONSE:** "Yes, we're done."
*   *Risk:* Dispatch may assume systems are fully restored and begin clearing shift logs, powering down consoles, or closing the shift. IT may rush to shut down BACKUP-CAD01, potentially corrupting the data export process or losing access if the shutdown script fails. Furthermore, if reconciliation reveals missing calls later, the "All Clear" creates liability that operations were declared normal when records were incomplete.

**✅ REQUIRED RESPONSE:**
> "Negative. Traffic is restored, but we are **not done**.
>
> 1.  **Data Reconciliation is in progress:** We must merge the calls logged on the backup system into the primary record. This takes approximately [X] minutes.
> 2.  **Do not close shift logs:** Please hold off on finalizing shift reports until I confirm data integrity.
> 3.  **Standby:** Continue monitoring Primary. We will issue a formal 'All Clear' once the backup data is safe and reconciled. Expect an update by [Time]."

---

## Post-Incident
*   IT and Dispatch Lead conduct blameless post-mortem within 48 hours.
*   Review `TRAFFIC_CUTOVER_TIME` vs `RECONCILIATION_COMPLETE_TIME` to measure recovery efficiency.
*   Verify BACKUP-CAD01 standby configuration matches runbook prerequisites.

# 911 CAD Outage: Failover & Failback Runbook

## Prerequisites
Before any incident, ensure the following are documented, tested, and accessible:
- **BACKUP-CAD01** maintained on patch baseline, network-routed, and validated in quarterly COOP tabletop/live drills.
- **Dispatcher training** on manual/paper logging, radio protocol, and backup-console data entry fields.
- **Vendor escalation matrix** with SLA thresholds, on-call contacts, and incident ticketing workflow.
- **Data reconciliation SOP** for export formats, field-mapping, deduplication rules, and records-retention policy.
- **Admin credentials & network access** for both primary and backup CAD systems, stored in a secure, outage-ready vault.
- **Audit logging** enabled on BACKUP-CAD01 for the entire incident window (timestamps, operator IDs, record IDs).
- **Communication channels** (dedicated dispatch IT channel, supervisor alert group, and external vendor bridge) pre-configured.

---

## Execution Steps

**1. Declare Outage & Activate COOP**  
Confirm primary CAD is unresponsive. Invoke COOP. Switch to paper/radio for call-taking and unit tracking. Notify all dispatchers, supervisors, and IT on-call.

**2. Provision BACKUP-CAD01**  
Bring up the on-prem backup console. Verify system status, auth access, network latency, and radio/data integration. Confirm dispatchers can successfully create and save test records.

**3. Redirect Live Operations**  
Instruct dispatchers to log all new calls, incidents, and unit movements directly into BACKUP-CAD01. Maintain paper logs as parallel backup. Assign a dedicated IT monitor to watch backup console health and queue depth.

**4. Track Vendor Status**  
Open a vendor incident ticket. Obtain acknowledgment, root cause (if known), and ETA. Refresh every 15 minutes until a "passing health check" confirmation is received.

**5. Validate Primary Restoration**  
When vendor reports restored: run independent validation before cutover.  
- Submit a test record from a training terminal.  
- Verify record appears in primary CAD with correct timestamps and field mapping.  
- Confirm radio/AVL/GPS feeds are re-syncing.  
- Get sign-off from the lead dispatcher before switching live traffic.

**6. Failback & Suspend Backup Logging**  
Announce cutover time. Switch all new call/incident logging back to primary CAD. Instruct dispatchers to stop entering data into BACKUP-CAD01. Mark BACKUP-CAD01 as `SUSPENDED – AWAITING RECONCILIATION`.

**7. Reconcile & Stand Down BACKUP-CAD01**  
This step must complete before the backup system is considered safe to leave idle.  
- Export all records created in BACKUP-CAD01 during the incident window (14:07–16:42).  
- Compare exported records against primary CAD historical records to identify duplicates, missing fields, or timestamp drift.  
- Merge reconciliation-confirmed records into primary CAD (or archive per county records policy).  
- Generate a reconciliation report with record counts, match rate, and any quarantined items.  
- Sign off with IT and Dispatch Supervisor. Only then may BACKUP-CAD01 be wiped, powered down, or returned to standby.

---

## Failure / Rollback Branch (for Step 7)

**Trigger conditions:** Export fails, field-mapping mismatch exceeds 5%, deduplication creates conflicts, or primary CAD rejects merged records.

**Rollback actions:**
1. **Do not wipe or power down BACKUP-CAD01.** Keep the system `SUSPENDED` and fully intact.
2. Quarantine the failed export into a secure, isolated storage bucket. Retain raw JSON/CSV + audit logs.
3. Switch dispatchers back to BACKUP-CAD01 temporarily to continue logging if primary is experiencing sync issues, or remain on paper if primary is stable but reconciliation is stalled.
4. Engage CAD data engineering + vendor support to manually map fields, repair timestamps, and run a controlled import with dry-run validation.
5. Once reconciliation succeeds on a trial batch, repeat Step 7 end-to-end. Do not close the incident until the reconciliation report shows 100% match rate and both supervisors sign off.

---

## What to Tell the Dispatcher at 16:50 (Replace "Are we done?")

**Do not say:** `"Primary's fine, we're all back on it. Are we done?"` or `"Yes, we're done."`

**Say instead:**
> `"Primary CAD is live and accepting new calls as of 16:42. We are now entering the mandatory data reconciliation phase for BACKUP-CAD01. All logs entered between 14:07 and 16:42 will be exported, cross-checked against primary records, and merged before this system is stand-downed. Do not touch BACKUP-CAD01, do not power it off, and do not clear any pending queues until IT and the Dispatch Supervisor explicitly confirm reconciliation is complete. We will close the incident only after the reconciliation report is signed off. Continue on primary as normal."`

This replaces assumption with verification, preserves the backup system for audit/compliance, prevents data loss, and ensures the next incident response team inherits a clean, documented failover/failback cycle.

**Runbook: CAD System Outage – Failover to BACKUP-CAD01 and Failback to Primary CAD**  
*Prepared for [County] 911 Dispatch Center – Version 1.0*  

---

## 1. Purpose
Provide a clear, step‑by‑step procedure to transition from the primary vendor‑hosted CAD system to the on‑prem backup console **BACKUP‑CAD01** during an outage, and to safely return to the primary CAD once it is restored. The runbook also defines the data‑reconciliation steps required before the backup system can be left idle again.

---

## 2. Prerequisites (must be verified **before** an outage occurs)

| # | Prerequisite | Owner | Verification Method |
|---|--------------|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | **BACKUP‑CAD01** hardware, OS, and application are installed, patched, and configured to mirror the primary CAD schema. | IT – Systems Engineering | Run the “Configuration Baseline Checklist” (attached). |
| 2 | Network connectivity (VPN, firewall rules) between BACKUP‑CAD01 and dispatch workstations is functional and tested. | IT – Network Ops | Ping/Traceroute test + successful login from a dispatch terminal. |
| 3 | Disaster‑Recovery (DR) run‑through (dry‑run) performed **quarterly** with dispatch staff, covering login, call‑logging, and report generation. | IT – DR Lead & Dispatch Supervisor | Sign‑off on DR drill report. |
| 4 | Data‑export/import scripts (or vendor‑provided sync tool) are validated for both **export from BACKUP‑CAD01** and **import into Primary CAD**. Test on a sample dataset. | IT – DB Admin | Script logs show 0 errors and data integrity checksum matches. |
| 5 | Documentation of all outage‑related contacts (vendor support, on‑call IT, dispatch shift lead) is current in the “On‑Call Roster”. | Admin – Ops | Roster reviewed weekly. |
| 6 | Paper‑log and radio‑log templates (COOP procedure) are printed and accessible at each dispatch console. | Dispatch Supervisor | Physical copy audit. |

*If any prerequisite is not met, the runbook must not be initiated until the gap is closed.*

---

## 3. Outage Response Flow

### 3.1 Detection & Initiation (Time ≈ 14:02)

1. **Dispatcher** notices primary CAD unresponsive → **Announces** “Primary CAD is unresponsive. Initiating COOP paper/radio logging.”  
2. **Dispatch Shift Lead** confirms the decision to switch to BACKUP‑CAD01 and notifies **IT On‑Call**.

### 3.2 Failover – Activate BACKUP‑CAD01 (Time ≈ 14:04‑14:07)

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| **F‑1** | **IT** powers on BACKUP‑CAD01 (if not already running) and verifies OS boot. | IT – Systems Ops | System reachable via IP, login prompt appears. |
| **F‑2** | **IT** starts the CAD application service on BACKUP‑CAD01 and runs the built‑in **health‑check** script. | IT – Systems Ops | Health‑check returns “PASS” and database connection succeeds. |
| **F‑3** | **Dispatcher** configures radio channels / paper‑log templates to point to BACKUP‑CAD01 (if not automatic). | Dispatcher / Shift Lead | New calls logged in BACKUP‑CAD01 interface; paper logs timestamped with “BACKUP”. |
| **F‑4** | **IT** sends a **Failover Confirmation** message to all dispatch staff (e.g., via radio net “All units, failover active – log calls in BACKUP‑CAD01”). | IT – Communications | Acknowledgement from at least 80 % of dispatch consoles. |
| **F‑5** | **Documentation** – IT records the exact **Failover Timestamp** (e.g., 14:07) in the Outage Log. | IT – DR Lead | Timestamp entry present. |

> **Note:** Steps F‑1 through F‑4 must be completed **sequentially**; do not proceed to F‑5 until F‑4 is confirmed.

### 3.3 Vendor Notification & Monitoring (Time ≈ 14:09)

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| **V‑1** | **IT** contacts the CAD vendor support line, provides outage details, and requests a **ticket** with escalation. | IT – Vendor Liaison | Ticket ID logged; vendor acknowledges receipt. |
| **V‑2** | **IT** periodically (every 15 min) pings the primary CAD health endpoint to detect restoration. | IT – Monitoring | Alert generated when health endpoint returns “OK”. |

---

## 4. Primary CAD Restoration – Failback Preparation (Time ≈ 16:35‑16:42)

When the vendor reports primary CAD is healthy:

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| **R‑1** | **IT** verifies primary CAD health checks (CPU, DB, API) for **≥ 5 consecutive minutes**. | IT – Monitoring | No error flags. |

| Failure Mode | Symptom |
|--------------|---------|

| **D. Data Corruption** – checksum mismatch after import. | Reconciliation report shows mismatched row counts or hash values. |

2. **Verify** that the primary CAD database is still in a **consistent** state (run vendor health check).

7. **Root‑Cause Analysis** – assign a ticket to IT DB Admin to fix the mapping/transaction issue before attempting another failback.

# Runbook: CAD Outage Failover to BACKUP-CAD01 and Safe Failback

**Incident Type:** Primary CAD Vendor-Hosted Outage  
**Backup System:** On-Prem Backup Dispatch Console (`BACKUP-CAD01`)  
**Goal:** Minimize dispatch disruption during outage, ensure zero data loss from the backup system, and safely return to primary operations.

---

## Prerequisites
*   **BACKUP-CAD01 Maintenance:** System must be powered on, patched, and tested weekly. Network path to dispatch terminals must be verified daily.
*   **Data Sync Tools:** Scripts or export/import utilities capable of extracting call logs from `BACKUP-CAD01` and reconciling them with the Primary CAD must be available and documented.
*   **Contact List:** Vendor support direct line, internal IT lead, and Dispatch Supervisor distribution list.
*   **COOP Plan:** Paper/Radio fallback procedures must be accessible at all dispatch stations.

---

## Phase 1: Failover to Backup System

**Step 1: Acknowledge Primary Failure & Activate COOP**
1.  Verify Primary CAD is unresponsive from multiple vantage points (vendor health page, internal ping, dispatcher report).
2.  Dispatch Supervisor declares COOP. All stations switch to Paper/Radio logging.
3.  *Communication:* Notify IT and Vendor immediately.

**Step 2: Activate BACKUP-CAD01**
1.  IT confirms `BACKUP-CAD01` is running and services are up.
2.  IT updates DNS/Load Balancer or manually configures dispatcher consoles to point to `BACKUP-CAD01`.
3.  Dispatcher verifies `BACKUP-CAD01` is accepting new calls.
4.  *Action:* Switch dispatching from Paper/Radio to `BACKUP-CAD01`.
5.  *Record:* Note exact time of first call logged on `BACKUP-CAD01` (e.g., `14:07`). **This timestamp defines the data window at risk.**

**Step 3: Monitor Backup Stability**
1.  IT monitors `BACKUP-CAD01` performance and storage.
2.  Dispatch Supervisor manages call volume; request backup consoles if capacity is threatened.
3.  Continue Vendor escalation for Primary ETA.

---

## Phase 2: Failback to Primary

**Step 4: Verify Primary Restoration**
1.  Vendor confirms Primary CAD health checks passed.
2.  IT performs internal validation: Log into Primary, verify database connectivity, and run a "test call" simulation to ensure the system is accepting and processing data.
3.  *Decision Point:* If Internal Validation fails, **STOP**. Return to Step 3. Do not fail back until Primary is stable.

**Step 5: Reconcile Backup Data (Critical Data Handling)**
*This step addresses all data logged on `BACKUP-CAD01` between the Failover activation and the planned Failback.*

1.  **Freeze Writes (Optional but Recommended):** If possible, pause new dispatches on `BACKUP-CAD01` for 2 minutes while export runs. If not possible, proceed with real-time sync.
2.  **Export Delta:** Run export script on `BACKUP-CAD01` for the window: `[Failover Timestamp]` to `[Current Time]`.
3.  **Validate Integrity:**
    *   Check for duplicate records.
    *   Verify all call IDs and timestamps are present.
    *   Check for corrupted fields.
4.  **Import to Primary:**
    *   Import validated data into Primary CAD.
    *   *Result:* Primary now contains all active incidents from the outage. `BACKUP-CAD01` data is preserved in Primary.
5.  **Reconciliation Report:** Generate a report comparing call counts on `BACKUP-CAD01` vs. Imported Primary records. Discrepancies must be resolved manually before proceeding.

**Step 6: Execute Cutover**
1.  IT switches live traffic to Primary CAD.
2.  Dispatch Supervisor confirms primary consoles are functional.
3.  *Communication:* Notify all staff: "Primary CAD is live. All new calls to Primary. Backup data has been merged."

> ### 🛑 FAILURE / ROLLBACK BRANCH: Step 6
> *This step is the easiest to get wrong. Common errors include cutting over without data sync, cutting over during a Primary instability spike, or creating duplicate records that confuse dispatchers.*
>
> **TRIGGER CONDITIONS FOR ROLLBACK:**
> *   Primary fails health check during cutover.
> *   Data reconciliation in Step 5 reveals unresolvable corruption or massive data loss on the backup.
> *   Dispatchers report Primary is rejecting data or behaving erratically post-cutover.
>
> **ROLLBACK PROCEDURE:**
> 1.  **IMMEDIATELY** switch traffic back to `BACKUP-CAD01`.
> 2.  **DO NOT** delete or overwrite `BACKUP-CAD01` data. It is the single source of truth.
> 3.  If data was partially imported, pause Primary imports and assess corruption.
> 4.  Notify Vendor of rollback reason. Extend outage duration.
> 5.  Repeat Step 5 (Reconcile) only after Primary stability is 100% confirmed.
> 6.  **Rule:** Never fail back until the data migration is verified and the Primary is stable for at least 5 minutes.

---

## Phase 3: Closure & Communication

**Step 7: Post-Failback Monitoring**
1.  Maintain `BACKUP-CAD01` online and ready for 60 minutes post-failback.
2.  IT monitors Primary for latency or data synchronization anomalies.
3.  Dispatch Supervisor monitors for dispatcher confusion regarding old vs. new incidents.

**Step 8: Safe Decommission of Backup**
1.  After 60 minutes of stability, IT backs up `BACKUP-CAD01` data one final time.
2.  Archive `BACKUP-CAD01` logs to long-term storage.
3.  Shut down `BACKUP-CAD01` services to conserve resources, keeping only the backup image available for immediate recovery if Primary fails again.

1. Revert to backup system immediately
2. Resume paper/radio operations
3. Notify IT manager

   primary is BACKUP-CAD01 safe to leave idle again. Log the reconciliation confirmation

# County 911 CAD Outage – Runbook  
**Version:** 1.0 **Effective:** 2025‑11‑03  
**Prepared by:** County 911 IT / Dispatch Services  

---  

## 1. Purpose  
Provide a repeatable, documented process for **failing over** from a vendor‑hosted CAD system to the on‑premises backup dispatch console (**BACKUP‑CAD01**) and for **failing back** to the primary system once it is restored.  

The runbook also details the **data‑handling** required for any calls logged on BACKUP‑CAD01 while the primary system was unavailable, ensuring that no information is lost and that the backup system can be safely returned to idle status.

---  

## 2. Scope  
- Applies to all 911 dispatch centers that use the **Vendor‑Hosted Primary CAD** (VPCAD) and the **On‑Prem Backup CAD (BACKUP‑CAD01)**.  
- Covers the entire life‑cycle of a CAD outage: detection → failover → operation on backup → health‑check of primary → failback → post‑mortem data reconciliation.  
- Intended for **IT staff, dispatch supervisors, and CAD administrators**.  

---  

## 3. Definitions / Acronyms  

| Acronym | Meaning |
|---------|----------|
| CAD | Computer‑Aided‑Dispatch (incident tracking, logging, and unit‑status UI) |
| VPCAD | Vendor‑Hosted Primary CAD |
| BACKUP‑CAD01 | On‑premises fallback dispatch console (the “secondary” CAD) |
| COOP | Continuity‑of‑Operations Plan |
| ETA | Estimated Time of Arrival (of restored primary CAD) |
| HL7 / API | Standard interfaces used to ingest logs into the primary system |
| Rollback | Returning to a previous state when a step fails |

---  

## 4. Prerequisites (to be verified before an outage)  

1. **COOP Documentation** – Updated run‑book with contact list, escalation matrix, and “paper/radio” fallback procedures.  
2. **Backup CAD Health** –  
   - BACKUP‑CAD01 is running the latest approved software version.  
   - All licensing is current and a valid license key is stored in the secure vault.  
   - Backup system passes its daily health‑check (CPU < 70 %, disk < 80 % used, network ≥ 10 Mbps).  
3. **Log Retention Policy** –  
   - All logs written to BACKUP‑CAD01 are retained for **minimum 30 days** on encrypted storage.  
   - A scheduled **log‑export** job is configured to push logs to a secure central repository every 15 minutes.  
4. **Failover Test** – Quarterly tabletop or live test confirming that BACKUP‑CAD01 can accept new calls and that the primary CAD can be taken offline without breaking the network.  
5. **Data‑Migration Script** – A documented PowerShell / Bash script (or vendor‑provided tool) that can **export** logs from BACKUP‑CAD01 and **import** them into the primary CAD via its API. The script must be version‑controlled and tested.  
6. **Communication Templates** – Pre‑approved messages for dispatchers, IT staff, vendors, and the public (if needed).  

---  

## 5. Procedure Overview  

| Phase | Goal | Primary Owner | Supporting Roles |
|-------|------|---------------|------------------|
| **A – Detection & Notification** | Recognize outage, announce failover | Dispatch Supervisor | IT On‑Call |
| **B – Failover to BACKUP‑CAD01** | Switch traffic, verify logging | IT Lead | Dispatch Supervisor |
| **C – Operate on Backup** | Continue to field calls, capture data | Dispatchers | IT (monitoring) |
| **D – Health‑Check & Confirmation of Primary Restoration** | Verify primary CAD is healthy | Vendor / IT | – |
| **E – Failback to Primary CAD** | Return traffic, reconcile logs | IT Lead | Dispatch Supervisor |
| **F – Post‑mortem & Data Reconciliation** | Ensure no data loss, document lessons | IT Lead / QA | – |
| **G – Close & Communicate** | Confirm “All back to normal” to dispatch | Dispatch Supervisor | IT |

---  

## 6. Detailed Steps  

### **A. Detection & Notification**  

1. **14:00 – 14:02** – Automated monitoring (Nagios/Zabbix) reports that VPCAD health‑check fails (no heartbeat for 2 min).  
2. **Dispatcher (14:02)** – Announces “Primary CAD unresponsive – initiating COOP, falling back to paper/radio.”  
3. **IT On‑Call (14:02‑14:04)** – Confirms outage, begins failover.  

### **B. Failover to BACKUP‑CAD01**  

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| **B1** | Verify BACKUP‑CAD01 is powered on and network‑connected. | IT Lead | `ping BACKUP‑CAD01` returns < 10 ms; UI loads. |
| **B2** | Activate **Primary‑to‑Backup Switch** in the network firewall (pre‑configured static route). | Network Engineer | Traffic to CAD port 443 is now directed to BACKUP‑CAD01. |
| **B3** | Start the **Log Export Service** on BACKUP‑CAD01 (pushes logs to central repo every 15 min). | IT Ops | Export service status = *Running*; last successful export < 5 min ago. |
| **B4** | Dispatchers switch UI to BACKUP‑CAD01 (use pre‑approved “Fallback UI URL”). | Dispatchers | Calls appear in BACKUP‑CAD01 within 30 s of creation. |
| **B5** | Issue **“All new calls will be logged on BACKUP‑CAD01”** announcement to dispatchers. | Dispatch Supervisor | All units acknowledge receipt. |
| **B6** | Confirm vendor awareness (optional but recommended). | IT Lead | Vendor ticket opened, ETA obtained. |

#### Failure / Rollback Branch for **B2 – Switch Traffic**  

- **If the firewall rule cannot be applied** (e.g., rule conflicts, admin lockout):  
  1. Revert the rule change immediately (`iptables -D …`).  
  2. Re‑enable inbound traffic to VPCAD on the original port.  
  3. If VPCAD still fails, fall back to **paper/radio only** and open a high‑severity incident (P1).  
  4. Notify the Vendor Support Escalation Team and the County Emergency Operations Center.  

### **C. Operate on Backup**  

- Continue normal dispatch operations.  
- Every **15 minutes**, verify that the **Log Export Service** completed without errors.  
- If a log export fails, manually trigger a push (`/opt/cad/export_logs.sh --force`).  
- Document any **new call IDs** generated on BACKUP‑CAD01 (these will need reconciliation later).  

### **D. Health‑Check & Confirmation of Primary Restoration**  

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| **D1** | Vendor sends **“Primary CAD restored – passing health check”** message. | Vendor Support | Health‑check result = *PASS*. |
| **D2** | IT runs **Primary‑CAD Health Script** (`check_vcad_health.sh`). | IT Lead | All services (API, DB, UI) return *OK*. |
| **D3** | Verify **network routing** can be switched back to VPCAD (re‑apply primary route). | Network Engineer | Ping to VPCAD succeeds; no packet loss. |
| **D4** | Confirm **BACKUP‑CAD01** is ready to be retired (no active calls, export service stopped). | IT Lead | Export service status = *Stopped*; no new calls logged for > 5 min. |

### **E. Failback to Primary CAD**  

| Step | Action | Owner | Success Criteria |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| **E1** | Re‑apply **primary routing rule** (remove backup route). | Network Engineer | Traffic now goes to VPCAD. |
| **E2** | Dispatchers switch UI back to **VPCAD** (default URL). | Dispatchers | Calls now appear in VPCAD UI. |
| **E3** | Issue “All calls now back to primary CAD” announcement. | Dispatch Supervisor | Acknowledgment from all units. |
| **E4** | Verify **new calls** flow through VPCAD without error for 10 minutes. | IT Monitoring | No alerts on CAD health. |

### **F. Post‑mortem & Data Reconciliation (Critical Step)**  

> **This is the step most prone to error** – **F1** (export & import logs from BACKUP‑CAD01) and **F2** (merge/validate) must be performed correctly.

#### Step‑by‑Step Data Reconciliation  

1. **F1 – Export Logs from BACKUP‑CAD01**  
   - Run the approved export script:  
     ```bash
     /opt/cad/export_logs.sh --start 2024-09-21T14:07:00Z --end 2024-09-21T16:42:00Z \
                              --output /var/tmp/BACKUP-CAD01_20240921_1407-1642.logs.tar.gz
     ```  
   - Verify checksum (SHA‑256) matches the recorded baseline.  

2. **F2 – Import Logs into Primary CAD**  
   - Use the vendor‑provided import tool:  
     ```bash
     /opt/cad/import_logs.sh --source /var/tmp/BACKUP-CAD01_20240921_1407-1642.logs.tar.gz \
                              --target VPCAD --mode append
     ```  
   - The tool automatically assigns the original timestamps; confirm that no duplicate incident IDs are created.  

3. **F3 – Validate Import**  
   - Run a **duplicate‑check query** on the primary CAD database:  
     ```sql
     SELECT incident_id, COUNT(*) FROM incidents 
     WHERE created_at BETWEEN '2024-09-21 14:07:00' AND '2024-09-21 16:42:00'
     GROUP BY incident_id HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;
     ```  
   - Result must return **0 rows**. If duplicates exist, resolve manually (remove extra rows, keep earliest timestamp).  

4. **F4 – Archive Backup Logs**  
   - Move the raw export file to the **long‑term archive** (`/archive/cad/backups/2024-09-21/`) with retention label “CAD‑Outage‑Backup‑Logs‑20240921”.  
   - Set file permissions to `chmod 600` and store the SHA‑256 checksum in the audit log.  

5. **F5 – Decommission BACKUP‑CAD01 for Now**  
   - Stop the export service, shut down the VM (or power‑down the hardware).  
   - Update the **CAD Inventory** to reflect “Backup CAD offline – awaiting next outage”.  

#### Failure / Rollback Branch for **F2 – Import Logs**  

- **If import fails (error code ≠ 0) or duplicate detection finds conflicts**:  
  1. **Abort** further imports.  
  2. **Rollback** the primary CAD database to the point‑in‑time snapshot taken at **13:55** (pre‑outage).  
     - Use the vendor‑provided restore script:  
       ```bash
       /opt/cad/restore_snapshot.sh --snapshot-id 2024-09-21_1355 --target VPCAD
       ```  
  3. Re‑open a **high‑severity incident** and notify the Vendor Support Team.  
  4. Keep BACKUP‑CAD01 online for continued dispatching while the rollback completes.  
  5. After rollback, re‑run **F1–F3** using the next available export window (typically within 30 minutes).  

### **G. Close & Communicate**  

1. **G1** – Dispatch Supervisor sends “All systems back to normal – CAD operating on primary” to all units.  
2. **G2** – IT sends an **Incident Closure Email** to:  
   - County Emergency Management  
   - Vendor Support Account Manager  
   - IT Management  
   - Includes: outage timeline, root cause, actions taken, data‑reconciliation outcome, and next‑step recommendations.  
3. **G3** – Schedule a **Post‑Outage Review** within 48 hours (include a “Lessons‑Learned” checklist).  

---  

## 7. What to Tell the 16:50 Dispatcher  

> **Instead of simply saying “yes, we’re done,” use the following scripted response:**  

> **“The primary CAD system has passed all health checks and is now fully restored. All calls that were logged on our backup console between 14:07 and 16:42 have been successfully imported into the primary system and verified for completeness. No data loss has been detected. We will continue to monitor the system for the next hour and will send a formal incident report later today. At this point we are cleared to resume normal dispatch operations on the primary CAD.”**  

- This wording makes explicit:  
  - **Status of primary CAD** (restored, passing health check).  
  - **What was done with the backup logs** (imported, verified).  
  - **Assurance of no data loss**.  
  - **Monitoring plan** (adds confidence).  
  - **Next formal reporting** (sets expectation).

# CAD Outage Recovery Runbook (Failover & Failback)

## Prerequisites
*   **Backup Infrastructure:** `BACKUP-CAD01` must be powered on, network-connected, and updated with the latest CAD schema.
*   **Vendor Contact:** Active escalation channel to the primary CAD vendor with a direct line to network operations.
*   **Communication Plan:** Designated radio channel or chat thread for IT and Dispatch to coordinate (e.g., "CAD-RECOVERY").
*   **Data Access:** Read-only credentials for `BACKUP-CAD01` export and write access for Primary CAD import.

---

## Phase 1: Failover (Primary Unresponsive)

1.  **Confirm Outage:** IT confirms Primary CAD (Vendor-hosted) is unresponsive for 2+ minutes.
2.  **Initiate COOP:** Dispatcher activates "Paper/Radio" fallback protocol immediately.
3.  **Activate Backup:** IT boots `BACKUP-CAD01` and verifies login credentials.
4.  **Switch Traffic:** Dispatchers are instructed to route all new call logging to `BACKUP-CAD01`.
    *   *Note:* Paper logs must continue for any calls missed during `BACKUP-CAD01` startup.
5.  **Vendor Alert:** IT notifies Primary CAD vendor of outage and requests ETA.

## Phase 2: Failback (Primary Restored)

*This phase requires strict coordination. Do not proceed until all conditions in Step 5 are met.*

6.  **Vendor Confirmation:** Vendor confirms Primary CAD is restored and passes internal health checks.
7.  **Stability Verification:** IT monitors Primary CAD for 15 minutes to ensure no latency or connection drops occur.

### Step 5: CRITICAL FAILOVER SWITCH (FAILBACK)
**Action:** Update routing tables to direct new calls to Primary CAD.
**[ROLLBACK BRANCH]**
*   **IF** Primary CAD shows latency, packet loss, or instability during Step 6 monitoring -> **DO NOT SWITCH.**
    *   *Action:* Remain on `BACKUP-CAD01`. Contact Vendor for further investigation.
*   **IF** Data reconciliation from `BACKUP-CAD01` fails (see Step 8) -> **DO NOT SWITCH.**
    *   *Action:* Maintain Primary outage status. Escalate data migration support.
*   **IF** No rollback conditions are met: Proceed with Step 5.
    *   *Action:* IT cuts over traffic. New calls now go to Primary CAD.
    *   *Log Time:* Record exact timestamp of switch (e.g., 16:42).

## Phase 3: Data Reconciliation (Mandatory)

*Before `BACKUP-CAD01` is considered safe to leave idle, the following data handling must occur for the session logged between 14:07 and 16:42.*

8.  **Export Backup Data:** IT exports the full incident log database from `BACKUP-CAD01` covering the outage window.
9.  **Merge & Deduplicate:** IT imports logs into Primary CAD and runs a deduplication script to ensure no calls were logged twice (once on backup, once on primary).
10. **Integrity Check:** IT verifies that the count of calls in Primary CAD matches the sum of calls logged on `BACKUP-CAD01` during the outage plus any calls logged on Primary during the 16:42–16:50 window.
11. **Backup Archive:** Once integrity is confirmed, `BACKUP-CAD01` is placed in "Archive" mode. No new calls are logged here.

## Phase 4: Incident Closure

12. **Monitor:** IT monitors Primary CAD for 30 minutes post-closure to ensure sustained stability.
13. **Close Incident:** IT formally closes the incident ticket in the dispatch system.

---

I will provide a final confirmation once the data merge is verified and the backup server is archived."
