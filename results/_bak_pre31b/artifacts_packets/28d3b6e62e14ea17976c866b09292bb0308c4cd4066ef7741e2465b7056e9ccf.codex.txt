2. **Duty to Explore Reasonable Accommodations** –  
   An accommodation is “any modification or adjustment” that enables an employee to perform the essential functions of the job, **unless it would cause undue hardship**. The only concrete barrier you identified is a **security‑related access requirement** (VPN + hardware token). **Bottom line:** A denial must be based on a **thorough assessment** of the request, the employee’s functional limitations, and the feasibility of any proposed accommodation—not on a gut feeling that “the job requires being in the office.”
