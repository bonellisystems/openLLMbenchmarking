# Knowledge Management -- scoring anchors (0-10)

## 10 -- Frontier
The runbook or document is testable: a newcomer could follow it step by step and reach the stated outcome without needing undocumented tribal knowledge. Prerequisites are listed up front, failure or rollback branches are included for steps that can go wrong, and a currency or ownership signal (last verified, owned by) is present when the prompt supports it.
Example character: numbered steps with prerequisites stated first, a rollback note on the one risky step, and a last-verified date.

## 7 -- Strong
The document is mostly step-by-step and usable as a runbook, but is missing one prerequisite check or a failure-branch note for a step that could plausibly fail. Nothing here requires guesswork to follow.
Example character: a clear numbered procedure that omits what to do if the third step fails.

## 5 -- Adequate
The content is a correct-gist prose description of the process rather than an executable, testable runbook -- a new reader would need to reverse-engineer the actual steps.
Example character: a paragraph explaining how the process generally works, without numbered, followable steps.

## 3 -- Weak
The document skips steps a newcomer would need, silently assumes undocumented tribal knowledge, and gives no ownership or currency signal at all.
Example character: a procedure that jumps from step one to step four with an unstated step in between.

## 0 -- Unusable
References fabricated tools, systems, or menu paths that do not exist, or gives a dangerously wrong sequence for a risky operation. Empty or off-task.

- Prose narrative presented as a runbook instead of testable numbered steps.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Numbered steps a newcomer could execute unaided.
- Prerequisites listed explicitly up front.
- Failure or rollback branch given for risky steps.
- Currency or ownership signal (last-verified, owner) included when supported by the prompt.

### **Overview**
Traders at this client utilize physical FIDO2 hardware security keys to authenticate into the Order Management System (OMS). If a key is lost or stolen, the trader is immediately locked out of all trading activities. 

Due to SEC/FINRA recordkeeping regulations, any modification to OMS access credentials is considered an auditable event. Furthermore, because an interrupted OMS session can result in "orphaned" or inconsistent order states, reissuance is strictly governed by market hours and order status.

---

### **Prerequisites**
Before initiating the reissuance process, the Helpdesk Technician **must** verify the following:

1.  **Dual-Factor Identity Verification (Compliance Requirement):**
    *   **Verification A:** Confirmation from the trader’s direct Desk Supervisor (via recorded phone call or authenticated email).
    *   **Verification B:** Internal IT identity verification (via approved video call or secondary MFA).
    *   *Note: No credential can be reissued with only one party's approval.*
2.  **Order Status Check:**
    *   Log into the OMS Admin Console and verify the trader’s current session status and order book.
    *   Confirm if the trader has any **Open Orders** (unfilled, partially filled, or pending cancel/replace).

---

### **Resolution Steps**

#### **Scenario A: The "Happy Path" (No Open Orders / Outside Market Hours)**
*Use these steps if the trader has zero open orders OR if the request is made outside of 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET.*

1.  **Document Verification:** Open a high-priority ticket. Log the timestamp and method of verification for both the Desk Supervisor and the IT technician.
2.  **Revoke Old Key:** Access the Identity Provider (IdP) and revoke the lost FIDO2 security key immediately to prevent unauthorized OMS access.
3.  **Provision New Key:** Prepare a new hardware security key.
4.  **Register Key:** 
    *   Access the user's profile in the Identity Provider.
    *   Initiate the "Register New FIDO2 Device" workflow.
    *   *Note: If the trader is locked out, this must be done via an administrative bypass/temporary bypass code if permitted by the client's specific policy.*
5.  **Deliver Key:** 
    *   **On-site staff:** Hand-deliver the key to the trader’s desk after verifying their physical ID.
    *   **Remote staff:** Send via overnight courier; do not use standard mail.
6.  **Final Audit Entry:** Close the ticket, explicitly stating: *"Dual-person verification completed (Supervisor [Name] and IT [Name]). Order status verified: No open orders. Key reissued."*

### **Failure Branches & Exceptions**

#### **Branch 1: Verification Failure (Compliance Risk)**
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor is unreachable, refuses to verify, or the trader cannot provide secondary IT verification.
*   **Action:** **DO NOT proceed with reissuance.**

*   **Action:** Escalate the ticket to the **Client Compliance Officer** and the **MSP Account Manager**.

#### **Branch 2: Market Hours / Open Order Risk (Operational Risk)**
**Condition:** The trader has active/open orders in the OMS AND the current time is between **9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET**.
*   **Action:** **DO NOT proceed with reissuance.**
*   **Action:** Inform the trader: *"Reissuance is prohibited during active market hours while orders are open to prevent order inconsistency. Your request is queued for [Time/Date]."*
*   **Action:** Set a follow-up task/reminder for the Helpdesk to execute the reissuance immediately after the market close (4:00 PM ET) or before the next market open (9:30 AM ET).
*   **Reasoning:** Forcing a credential reset while orders are active can drop the session mid-transaction, leaving orders in an "inconsistent state" (e.g., an order is sent to the exchange but the OMS loses the ability to track its fill status), creating significant financial and legal risk.

# Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

**Article ID:** KB-OMS-FIDO2-014  
**Audience:** MSP Helpdesk & NOC Teams  
**Client Environment:** Broker-Dealer Order Management System (OMS)  
**Priority Handling:** Critical (Trading Floor)  
**Last Updated:** [Current Date]

---

## Overview
This procedure guides helpdesk technicians through the secure, compliant replacement of a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for broker-dealer trading-floor staff. Loss of the key results in complete OMS authentication lockout. Strict SEC/FINRA recordkeeping and market-integrity controls apply to all credential reissuance events.

---

## Prerequisites
Before initiating this procedure, verify all conditions below. **Do not proceed if any prerequisite is unmet.**

- [ ] **Trader Presence:** Trader is physically on the trading floor or connected via a verified corporate channel.
- [ ] **Dual Verifiers Available:** The trader’s desk supervisor AND an IT representative (or compliance-cleared helpdesk senior) are available to participate in identity verification.
- [ ] **Zero Open Orders:** OMS query confirms the trader currently has `0` open, pending, or leg orders across all sessions.
- [ ] **Market Window Confirmed:** Current time is outside continuous market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET) **OR** the zero-open-orders condition is definitively met.
- [ ] **Hardware Staged:** A provisioned, out-of-the-box FIDO2 key is logged in inventory and ready for issue.
- [ ] **Compliance Template Ready:** SEC/FINRA Access Change & Identity Verification form is open and attached to the ticket.
- [ ] **Admin Console Access:** IT verifier has active session in the OMS Admin Console / Identity Provider to revoke old credential and register new key.

---

## Resolution Steps

1. **Triage & Ticket Initialization**
   - Set ticket priority to `Critical` and route to `Trading_Floor_Support`.
   - Confirm the key is irrecoverably lost and the trader is fully locked out of OMS.
   - Attach the compliance verification template to the ticket.

2. **Validate OMS Order State**
   - Run the standard OMS session query for the trader’s User ID.
   - Confirm `Active_Orders = 0`, `Pending_Fills = 0`, and `Session_Locks = None`.
   - Document the query timestamp, result, and script/output hash in the ticket.

3. **Execute Two-Person Identity Verification**
   - Contact the desk supervisor via verified corporate phone or in-person. Ensure an IT representative is physically present or on a secure, recorded bridge call.
   - Both parties must independently verify the trader’s identity using two approved methods (e.g., firm badge + government ID, or badge + live biometric match via secure camera).
   - Record: names, titles, verification methods, timestamp, and terminal location.
   - Both verifiers must electronically sign or provide recorded verbal confirmation logged in the ticket. **This step is non-negotiable per SEC/FINRA audit requirements.**

4. **Revoke Lost Credential & Push Registration Flow**
   - In the OMS Admin Console, locate the trader’s registered FIDO2 credential ID.
   - Mark the lost key as `Revoked/Lost` and note the revocation timestamp.
   - Use the `Emergency_Key_Provisioning` workflow to generate a time-bound registration token and push it to the trader’s OMS terminal.

5. **Pair New FIDO2 Key**
   - Hand the staged key to the trader in the presence of both verifiers.
   - Guide the trader to apply the registration token in OMS.
   - Trader taps the new key, approves the on-device FIDO2 prompt, and confirms successful registration.
   - Verify OMS returns `Key_Paired_Success` and `Auth_Method_Updated`.

6. **Validate Access & Finalize Documentation**
   - Have the trader perform a test authentication and confirm full OMS functionality (no restricted menus, full order routing visibility).
   - Complete and attach the two-person verification form. Log: new key serial/ID, revocation timestamp, registration timestamp, and verifier signatures.
   - Route ticket to `Compliance_Archive` for 6-year retention.
   - Close ticket with resolution code: `HARDWARE_KEY_REPLACEMENT_SUCCESS`.

## Compliance & Post-Implementation Notes
- **Audit Trail:** OMS credential changes are treated as auditable events. All verification logs, revocation records, and key serials must be retained for a minimum of 6 years per FINRA Rule 3110/3120 and SEC Rule 17a-4.
- **No Bypasses:** Never circumvent the two-person rule or market-window restriction, even under execution pressure. Use break-glass admin accounts only for session termination if required, never for credential issuance.
- **Key Policy:** FIDO2 keys are cryptographically bound to the OMS tenant and cannot be recovered or reset remotely. Only replacement is permitted.
- **Escalation Path:** If OMS registration fails or the admin console shows conflicting credential states, immediately escalate to `Client_SRE_Trading` and `Compliance_Officer` before touching the terminal again.

## Overview
This article outlines the standard operating procedure for reissuing a FIDO2 hardware security key to a trader at the [Broker-Dealer Client]. Traders utilize physical hardware keys to authenticate to the OMS. Loss of this key results in a complete lockout from the OMS.

**WARNING:** Reissuing credentials is a regulated event. Failure to follow the verification and timing protocols below constitutes a compliance violation (SEC/FINRA) and risks data integrity within the OMS. **Do not bypass these steps.**

## Prerequisites
Before initiating the reissuance process, the following conditions must be verified and met simultaneously:

1.  **Two-Person Identity Verification:** You must obtain verbal and written confirmation from **both** of the following:
    *   The Trader’s Desk Supervisor.
    *   The IT Security Manager (or designated IT approver).
2.  **OMS Order Status:** The trader must have **zero open orders** currently working in the OMS.
3.  **Market Hours Check:** The current time must be **outside of active market hours** (before 9:30 AM ET or after 4:00 PM ET) if the trader possesses any open orders.

## Failure Branches (Stop Conditions)
**DO NOT PROCEED** with the reissuance if any of the following scenarios occur. Document the interaction in the ticket and follow the escalation path.

### Branch A: Supervisor Unreachable
*   **Condition:** You cannot reach the Trader’s Desk Supervisor within 5 minutes of initial contact attempts.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key.
    2.  Log the attempt in the ticket notes (timestamps and phone numbers called).
    3.  Escalate to the **MSP Client Account Manager**.
    4.  **Reason:** Compliance requires documented two-person verification. Without the Supervisor, the event is not auditable.

### Branch B: Open Orders During Market Hours
*   **Condition:** The trader has open orders **AND** the current time is between **9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET**.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** revoke the key or provision a new one.
    2.  Inform the Trader and Supervisor that the process is on hold.
    3.  Set a reminder to revisit the request immediately after **4:00 PM ET** (Market Close) or before **9:30 AM ET** (Market Open).
    4.  **Reason:** Interrupting an OMS session with open orders mid-session risks orders landing in an inconsistent state, potentially causing financial loss or compliance breaches.

---

## Resolution Steps
Once all Prerequisites are met and no Failure Branches are triggered, proceed with the following steps:

### Step 1: Verify Authorization
1.  Contact the **Desk Supervisor** via phone.
2.  Confirm the loss of the hardware key and request authorization for reissuance.
3.  Contact **IT Security** to confirm approval.
4.  **Crucial:** Record the names of the approvers and the time of the call in the ticket. This constitutes the audit trail required for SEC/FINRA obligations.
    *   *Ticket Note Format:* `APPROVED BY: [Supervisor Name] + [IT Name] at [Time]. Reason: Lost Hardware Key.`

### Step 2: Confirm Market Status (If Applicable)
1.  If the request is made during market hours, double-check with the trader that they have no pending orders.
2.  If there are no orders, proceed to Step 3.
3.  If there are orders, enforce **Branch B** above and wait for market close/open.

### Step 3: Revoke Compromised Key
1.  Log into the OMS Identity Provider (IdP) console.
2.  Navigate to **Users > [Trader User ID]**.
3.  Select **MFA/FIDO2 Keys**.
4.  Locate the lost key (usually identifiable by the last 4 digits of the serial number or registration date).
5.  Click **Revoke** to invalidate the lost key immediately. This prevents unauthorized access if the key is found or compromised.

### Step 4: Provision New Hardware Key
1.  Issue a new FIDO2 hardware key to the trader.
2.  Instruct the trader to register the new key:
    *   Insert the key into the USB port.
    *   Navigate to the OMS login page.
    *   Select "Use Security Key" and follow the on-screen prompts to register the new token.
3.  Verify the new key works by attempting a test login (if permitted by client policy) or observing the trader log in successfully.

### Step 5: Close Ticket
1.  Document the new key serial number in the ticket.
2.  Update the **Hardware Inventory** database.
3.  Mark the ticket as **Resolved** with the following note: `Key Replaced. Audit Log Attached. Pre-market/Closed Market Hours Verified.`

---

## Escalation Path
If you encounter technical issues with the OMS IdP console or the new key fails to register:

1.  **Do Not** escalate to Tier 3 without first confirming the two-person approval is documented.
2.  Contact the **MSP Client Account Manager** to coordinate with the Client’s Internal IT/Compliance team.
3.  **Note:** Internal IT may need to reset the specific OMS session to clear any locked state caused by the lost key.

# KB Article: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

**Article ID:** KB-OMS-0092  
**Applies To:** Broker-Dealer Client | Trading Desk Staff  
**System:** FIDO2 Hardware Key / OMS Authentication  
**Last Updated:** [Current Date]  
**Owner:** MSP Security & Identity Team  

---

### Purpose
This article defines the compliant, auditable process for replacing a lost FIDO2 hardware security key used to authenticate traders to the Order Management System (OMS). Loss of this key locks the trader out of OMS entirely. Because OMS access changes are treated as auditable events under SEC and FINRA recordkeeping obligations, strict procedural controls must be followed to prevent unauthorized access, audit failures, or trading session disruption.

---

### Prerequisites (Must Verify Before Proceeding)
Complete all items below before initiating any reissuance action:

1. **Trader Identity Confirmed**  
   Verify the requester’s identity via pre-approved channels (e.g., internal directory, two-factor call verification, or existing support ticket with PII).

2. **Two-Person Authorization Documented**  
   Obtain and log approvals from:
   - The trader’s assigned **Desk Supervisor**
   - An authorized **IT/Security Provisioning Engineer**  
   Both approvals must be recorded with timestamps in the ticketing system prior to any system modification.

3. **OMS Open Order Status Verified**  
   Confirm the trader currently has **zero open orders** in the OMS. Check via OMS Admin Dashboard, pre-trade risk monitor, or session audit log.

4. **Market Window Compliance**  
   If open orders exist, verify current ET time against trading windows (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET). Reissuance cannot occur during active trading sessions.

5. **Provisioning Assets Ready**  
   Ensure access to the FIDO2 Provisioning Portal and OMS Access Control Console. A replacement key must be packaged, logged in the asset tracking system, and ready for delivery.

---

### Resolution Steps
1. **Open & Validate Ticket**  
   Create a Level-2 Security ticket. Log the exact time of report, requestor details, and last known key serial number.

2. **Verify Two-Person Authorization**  
   - Contact the desk supervisor via secure internal channel. Record their verbal/digital approval, full name, and timestamp in the ticket.
   - Notify the IT Provisioning team. Obtain their explicit approval and log it.
   - If either approval cannot be obtained, proceed to **Failure Branch A**.

3. **Check Open Order Status**  
   - Log into the OMS Admin Console or pre-trade monitoring dashboard.
   - Query the trader’s session for active/open orders.
   - If open orders = 0, proceed to Step 4.
   - If open orders > 0, proceed to **Failure Branch B**.

4. **Initiate Key Reissuance**  
   - Navigate to the FIDO2 Provisioning Portal.
   - Enter the trader’s OMS Principal ID.
   - Select `Reissue FIDO2 Device` and attach the two-party authorization documentation.
   - Generate and provision the replacement key. The system will automatically invalidate the lost key’s credentials and update OMS access logs.

5. **Verify & Test Authentication**  
   - Confirm the new key appears in the trader’s authorized device list.
   - Advise the trader to log out of all OMS sessions (if feasible) and attempt authentication with the new key.
   - Verify successful login and confirm permission level matches their role.

6. **Document & Archive**  
   - Attach supervisor approval, IT approval, open-order check screenshot, and replacement key serial number to the ticket.
   - Tag ticket with `#SECFINRA-AUDIT` and `#HARDWARE_KEY_REISSUANCE`.
   - Notify the desk supervisor of completion and close the ticket.

---

### Failure Branches

#### 🔹 Branch A: Supervisor or IT Approval Unavailable
- **Do not proceed** with reissuance. Bypassing two-party control violates SEC Rule 4541 and FINRA Rule 4414.
- If the trader reports urgent access needs during trading hours:
  1. Notify the Trading Desk Manager and Compliance Officer via out-of-band secure channel.
  2. Document the delay, reason, and escalation in the ticket.
  3. Reschedule reissuance for the next available window with confirmed approvals.
  4. Do not provision emergency access tokens or alternative credentials without explicit Compliance sign-off.

#### 🔹 Branch B: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET)
- **Do not initiate reissuance.** Interrupting an active OMS session with open orders mid-session risks order state corruption, failed executions, or inconsistent risk positions.
- Take the following actions:
  1. Log the open order count, session ID, and current ET time in the ticket.
  2. Inform the trader that reissuance must wait until after **4:00 PM ET** or before **9:30 AM ET**.
  3. If the trader requires immediate alternative access for emergency risk control (e.g., system halt request or compliance stop-trade), escalate to the Trading Desk Manager and Compliance. Follow the firm’s `Trading Session Emergency Override` SOP (KB-OMS-0128).
  4. Schedule reissuance for the next approved window. Re-verify that open orders = 0 before proceeding.

---

### Compliance & Audit Notes
- All OMS credential changes are auditable under SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations. Treat every key reissuance as a material access control event.
- Retain supervisor/IT approvals, timestamps, system logs, and key serial numbers for a minimum of 6 years.
- Physical lost keys must be reported to the Asset Tracking team within 24 hours for chain-of-custody destruction logging.
- Never cache, pre-provision, or share replacement keys outside of documented, two-party authorized workflows.

| Field | Details |

| **Service** | Order Management System (OMS) |
| **Severity** | **P1 / Critical** (Trader cannot execute orders) |
| **SLA Target** | Immediate response; Resolution time depends on Market Hours/Compliance checks. |

---

### ⚠️ Critical Warnings

*   **NO SOLO REISSUANCE:** You **cannot** reissue a key alone. SEC/FINRA recordkeeping requirements mandate **documented two-person verification** (Desk Supervisor + IT) for all OMS credential changes.
*   **MARKET HOURS LOCKOUT:** If a trader has open orders during market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET), you **must not** reissue the key until after market close or before market open. Interrupting a session with open orders risks orders landing in an inconsistent state, which is a trade-break event.
*   **LOST KEY IS COMPROMISED:** Treat the lost key as compromised. Revoke the associated credential **immediately** upon successful verification to prevent unauthorized access if the key is found by a third party.

---

### Prerequisites

Before executing any resolution step, verify **all** of the following. If any prerequisite fails, follow the associated **Failure Branch** below.

1.  **Trader Identity Confirmed:** You have positively identified the affected trader (e.g., via desk location, badge, or manager confirmation).
2.  **Desk Supervisor Reachable:** The trader's named desk supervisor is available, reachable by phone, and authorized to approve credential reissuance.
3.  **Second IT Verifier Available:** A second IT team member (not the agent performing the reissuance) is available to act as the independent witness/verifier.
4.  **Open Orders & Market Time Check:**
    *   *Scenario A:* Trader has **zero** open orders **OR** current time is **outside** Market Hours (before 9:30 AM ET or after 4:00 PM ET). → **Proceed.**
    *   *Scenario B:* Trader has **open orders** **AND** current time is **inside** Market Hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET). → **DEFER. Do not proceed.**
5.  **Replacement Inventory:** A spare, unprovisioned FIDO2 hardware key is available in the on-site IT inventory for this client.

---

### Resolution Steps

#### 1. Triage and Open Orders Verification
*   Log the ticket as P1. Notify the Trading Floor Manager via the designated escalation channel.
*   Access the **OMS Admin Console** and navigate to the Trader's active session profile.
*   Check the **"Open Orders"** count for this trader.
*   **Decision Point:**
    *   *Zero Open Orders:* Proceed to Step 2.
    *   *Open Orders Present:* Check current time.
        *   *Outside Market Hours:* Proceed to Step 2.
        *   *Inside Market Hours:* Execute **Failure Branch 1** below.

#### 2. Initiate Two-Person Verification
*   **Action:** Call the Trader's Desk Supervisor immediately.
*   **Script:** *"This is [MSP Name] IT. Trader [Name] has reported a lost FIDO2 key. Due to compliance requirements, I need your verbal authorization to reissue their OMS credential. Please confirm you authorize this action."*
*   **Verification:**
    *   Supervisor must verbally confirm authorization.
    *   Second IT Verifier must witness this call (or join a conference bridge) and confirm they heard the authorization.
    *   Record: Supervisor Name, Verification Time, and Second Verifier Name.

#### 3. Document Audit Trail
*   Before touching the OMS, create a **Compliance Audit Log Entry** in the ticket notes. This is an auditable event under SEC/FINRA recordkeeping.
*   **Required Fields:**
    *   Trader Name / ID.
    *   Reason: Lost Hardware Key.
    *   Authorization: Desk Supervisor [Name] authorized at [Timestamp].
    *   IT Verifier: [Name] witnessed at [Timestamp].
    *   Open Orders Status: [Count] at [Timestamp].
    *   Market Hours Context: [In Session / Closed].

#### 4. Revoke Lost Key Credential
*   In the **OMS Admin Console** or **Identity Provider (IdP)**, locate the trader's FIDO2 credential associated with the lost key.
*   **Revoke/Disable** the credential immediately.
*   *Rationale:* This prevents the trader from authenticating with the lost key if it is used by an unauthorized party and locks them out of any active OMS session safely.
*   *Note:* If the trader has an active session, this will terminate the session. Ensure this is done only after Steps 2 and 3 are complete.

#### 5. Provision New Hardware Key
*   Retrieve the spare FIDO2 key from inventory.
*   Register the new key against the trader's profile in the OMS/IdP.
*   Perform a **FIDO2 Attestation** to ensure the key is authentic and unmodified.
*   Map the new key to the trader's OMS role and permissions.

#### 6. Handoff and Validation
*   Physically hand the new key to the trader.
*   Have the trader perform a **live test authentication** to the OMS from their trading desk.
*   Confirm the trader can access the OMS and that no orders are stuck in the credential chain (if applicable).
*   Request the trader sign the **Hardware Key Receipt Form** (or digital acknowledgment) confirming receipt of the replacement.

#### 7. Ticket Closure
*   Update the ticket with:
    *   New Key Serial Number.
    *   Handoff confirmation.
    *   Test success timestamp.
*   Mark ticket as Resolved.
*   If the reissuance was deferred due to open orders, create a **Follow-Up Task** scheduled for the next market open/close window to complete the reissuance.

---

### Failure Branches

#### Failure Branch 1: Open Orders During Market Hours
*   **Condition:** Trader has open orders AND time is 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key.
    2.  Inform the trader: *"You are locked out due to compliance. Because you have open orders, we cannot reissue the key until market close at 4:00 PM ET to protect order integrity."*
    3.  Update the ticket with the Open Order count and the scheduled reissuance time (e.g., "Reissuance scheduled for 4:05 PM ET").
    4.  Notify the Desk Supervisor of the delay reason.
    5.  Set a reminder/escalation for 4:00 PM ET to immediately execute Steps 2–7 once the system allows.
    6.  *Post-Market Procedure:* After 4:00 PM, verify orders are settled/closed in OMS, then proceed with the full reissuance workflow.

#### Failure Branch 2: Desk Supervisor Unreachable
*   **Condition:** Supervisor cannot be reached after 2 attempts, or declines authorization.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key.
    2.  Do **not** escalate to a peer supervisor or the Chief Compliance Officer without explicit client policy allowing it. The client requires the **Trader's Desk Supervisor** specifically.
    3.  Notify the Trader and the MSP Account Manager.
    4.  Update ticket: *"Blocked. Desk Supervisor unreachable. Awaiting contact."*
    5.  Re-attempt contact every 15 minutes. If unresolved, escalate to the designated alternate approver **only if** the Client's Risk/Compliance policy document explicitly names a backup (check Client KB `KB-COMPLIANCE-005`). If no backup is named, hold the ticket.

#### Failure Branch 3: Second IT Verifier Unavailable
*   **Condition:** No second IT team member available to witness the verification.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** proceed with reissuance. One-person reissuance violates the two-person control.
    2.  Request coverage from the MSP Shift Lead or adjacent MSP team.
    3.  If no coverage is possible, defer reissuance until the next shift or on-site engineer arrival.
    4.  Document the verifier shortage in the ticket.

#### Failure Branch 4: Replacement Key Inventory Depleted
*   **Condition:** No spare FIDO2 keys available on-site.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** reissue.
    2.  Check with neighboring MSP sites or Client IT if they have a loaner key (temporary measure).
    3.  If no loaner available, initiate emergency procurement via MSP inventory portal.
    4.  Inform the Trader: *"Reissuance is pending hardware delivery. ETA [Time]."*
    5.  Log the inventory failure for replenishment planning.

# KB Article: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

## Overview
This article provides step-by-step guidance for replacing lost FIDO2 hardware security keys used by trading-floor staff to authenticate to the order-management system (OMS). Lost keys result in complete OMS access loss and require specific compliance procedures before reissuance can occur.

## Prerequisites
Before beginning the replacement process, verify the following conditions:

1. **Traded has a confirmed lost hardware security key** - The trader must confirm they have lost their physical FIDO2 security key
2. **Trader's desk supervisor is available for verification** - Both the trader's supervisor and IT must be available for two-person identity verification
3. **Open orders status verified** - Confirm trader has zero open orders in the OMS (refer to timing requirements)
4. **Compliance documentation ready** - Prepare for audit trail documentation of the credential replacement

## Resolution Steps

1. **Initial Contact and Verification**
   - Contact the trader to confirm the lost hardware key situation
   - Verify the trader's identity using the existing access control procedures

2. **Supervisor and IT Coordination**
   - Contact the trader's desk supervisor to verify the loss and confirm their identity
   - Both the supervisor and IT representative must confirm the credential replacement is authorized

3. **Open Orders Verification**
   - Check the trader's current order status in the OMS
   - Verify there are zero open orders working in the system

4. **Reissuance Timing Confirmation**
   - If no open orders exist, proceed with immediate reissuance
   - If open orders exist, schedule reissuance for after market close (4:00 PM ET) or before market open (9:30 AM ET)

5. **Hardware Key Reissuance**
   - Generate new hardware security key through approved vendor process
   - Configure the new key with the trader's account credentials
   - Provide new key to trader and document in compliance system

6. **Access Verification**
   - Have trader verify successful authentication to OMS
   - Confirm all trading functionality works properly

## Failure Branch: Supervisor Unavailable

**Scenario**: The trader's desk supervisor cannot be reached for verification within 30 minutes

**Resolution**:
1. Document the failed verification attempt in the ticketing system
2. Contact the supervisor's direct manager or backup contact
3. If supervisor cannot be reached within 2 hours, escalate to the Compliance Department
4. Per compliance policy, do not proceed with credential replacement until proper verification is completed
5. Schedule the replacement process for the next available time slot when supervisor is available

## Failure Branch: Open Orders During Market Hours

**Scenario**: Trader has open orders in the OMS during normal business hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)

**Resolution**:
1. Inform the trader that immediate reissuance is not permitted due to order state risk
2. Document the order status and time in the ticketing system
3. Schedule reissuance for the next available window:
   - After market close (4:00 PM ET)
   - Before market open (9:30 AM ET)
4. Notify trader of the scheduled reissuance time
5. Monitor OMS status until scheduled window and verify orders are no longer active before proceeding

## Compliance Notes

All credential replacement actions are auditable events and must be documented in accordance with SEC/FINRA recordkeeping requirements. The two-person verification process must be completed and recorded before any new security key is issued.

2.1 Log in to the OMS admin console with your admin credentials.

4.2 Click **“Revoke / De‑activate”** – confirm the action.  
4.3 The system will generate a **Revocation ID**; copy it to the ticket.  

> This step creates an audit trail required by SEC/FINRA for “OMS access changes”.

### Step 5 – Provision the New Key  
5.1 In the same module, click **“Add New FIDO2 Key”**.  
5.2 Insert the replacement key into your workstation’s USB port.  
5.3 Follow the on‑screen enrollment wizard (usually: Touch the key → Confirm).  
5.4 Assign the key to the trader’s username and set the **“Primary”** flag.  
5.5 Save; the system will return a **Provisioning ID** – log it in the ticket.

6.1 Ask the trader (or their desk) to **log into the OMS** using the new key.  
6.2 Verify that the trader can access the OMS dashboard without errors.  
6.3 Record the **login timestamp** in the ticket.

7.1 Attach the following to the ticket:  
- Supervisor approval email  
- IT‑security approval email  
- Revocation ID & Provisioning ID screenshots  
- Vault inventory log entry (key removal)  
7.2 Change ticket status to **“Resolved – Closed”**.  
7.3 Notify the trader’s supervisor that the process is complete.

  - If IT cannot respond within 20 minutes, escalate to MSP Security Lead and notify client compliance contact via secure channel.

### 🔴 Failure Branch D: FIDO2/OMS Admin Console Error
- **Trigger:** Key deactivation fails, new key registration returns error code, or OMS sync fails.
- **Action:**
  - Capture full error logs and screenshots.
  - Do not retry more than twice to prevent session lockouts.
  - Escalate to Tier 3/Systems Engineering with priority `P2-OMS_INTEGRITY`.
  - Provide trader with temporary manual override access (if client policy permits) while engineering investigates.

## Compliance & Audit Documentation
- **SEC/FINRA Requirement:** All OMS access changes are treated as auditable events. The `Two-Person_Verification_Checklist.xlsx` must be signed (electronically or physically) by both the desk supervisor and IT representative.
- **Retention:** Attach the completed audit form, ticket notes, and admin console logs to the ticket. Retain for minimum 7 years per client recordkeeping policy.
- **Prohibited Actions:** Never bypass two-person verification, never force-logout a session with open orders during market hours, and never share FIDO2 provisioning credentials outside the IT security team.

# KB Article  
**Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading‑Floor Staff**  
*Target audience: Help‑Desk (Level‑1/2) agents supporting broker‑dealer traders*  

---  

## 1. Purpose  
Provide a step‑by‑step procedure for issuing a **replacement FIDO2 hardware security key** to a trader who has lost their primary key. The process must satisfy the firm’s **SEC/FINRA record‑keeping** and **two‑person identity verification** requirements and avoid any interruption of open OMS orders.

---

## 2. Prerequisites  *(must be verified before any action)*  

| # | Prerequisite | How to verify | Acceptance criteria |
|---|--------------|---------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Trader identity is confirmed** – name, employee ID, and photo‑ID match the OMS account. | Pull the trader’s profile from the **Active Directory / IAM** console and compare with the presented ID. | Exact match on all fields. |
| 2 | **Supervisor approval** – the trader’s desk‑supervisor has signed off on the replacement. | Obtain a written (email or ticket) approval from the supervisor **and** capture the supervisor’s internal approval code in the ticket. | Supervisor’s name, approval timestamp, and approval code present. |
| 3 | **IT verification** – IT security has performed the dual‑person verification and logged the event. | Confirm that the IT security analyst has entered a **two‑person verification code** in the ticket and that the code matches the one recorded by the supervisor. | Both verification codes present and identical. |
| 4 | **No open OMS orders** – the trader must have **zero open orders** in the OMS at the time of re‑issue. | Query the OMS for any active orders belonging to the trader’s user‑ID. | Order count = 0. |
| 5 | **Appropriate time window** – if open orders exist, the re‑issue must be scheduled **after 4:00 PM ET** (market close) **or before 9:30 AM ET** (pre‑market). | Check the current system time against the trader’s order status. | If orders exist, current time must be ≤ 9:30 AM ET **or** ≥ 4:00 PM ET. |

> **Failure branch** – If any prerequisite above cannot be satisfied, **do not proceed** with key replacement. Follow the “Failure‑Branch Handling” section (see § 7).

---

## 3. Resolution Steps  

> **All actions must be recorded in the ticket (Ticket‑ID: \<NUM\>)** with timestamps, verification codes, and any supporting screenshots.

1. **Create a new ticket** titled “Replacement – Lost FIDO2 Key – \<Trader‑Name\>”.  
2. **Log prerequisite verification** – use the checklist in § 2 to confirm each item. Add the verification codes from the supervisor and IT security.  
3. **Confirm zero open orders**:  
   - Open the OMS “Order Dashboard”.  
   - Search for the trader’s user‑ID.  
   - Verify the result shows **0 open orders**.  
   - Record the order‑count screenshot.  
4. **Validate time window** (if open orders existed):  
   - If the current time falls within an allowed window, note “Time window validated”.  
   - If not, schedule the replacement for the next permissible window and close the ticket as “Deferred – scheduled for \<date‑time\>”.  
5. **Submit re‑issuance request** to the **Hardware Security Key Management System (HSM‑KMS)**:  
   - Select “Generate New Key → Assign to User \<User‑ID\>”.  
   - Record the newly generated **Key Serial Number** and **QR‑code** in the ticket.  
6. **Notify the trader**:  
   - Send an encrypted email (or secure messaging channel) with the new key’s enrollment instructions and a link to the “Key Enrollment Guide”.  
   - Include a **copy of the two‑person verification logs** for audit purposes.  
7. **Close the ticket** once the trader confirms successful enrollment (they will be prompted to register the new key on first login).  
8. **Audit trail**: Ensure the ticket includes:  
   - Supervisor approval code.  
   - IT verification code.  
   - Screenshot of zero open orders.  
   - Timestamp of re‑issue.  
   - Final “Completed” status.  

---

## 4. Failure‑Branch Handling  

If **any** prerequisite cannot be satisfied, **immediately halt** the replacement process and follow the appropriate branch below.

### 4.1 Supervisor Unreachable  

| Condition | Action | Documentation |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Supervisor cannot be contacted within 30 minutes (phone, email, Teams) | 1. Escalate to the **Supervisor’s Manager** or the **Trading Desk Lead**. <br>2. Obtain written approval via a **manager‑signed email** (attach to ticket). | Save the escalated email as an attachment; note the escalation time. |
| No approval can be obtained after escalation (e.g., manager also unavailable) | **Do not proceed**. Close the ticket with status **“Pending Supervisor Approval – Re‑try later”** and schedule a follow‑up. | Record the closure reason and expected re‑try date. |

### 4.2 Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours  

| Condition | Action | Documentation |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Trader possesses ≥1 open order **and** current time is between 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET | 1. **Do not issue** a new key. <br>2. Notify the trader: “Your replacement key request is on hold until all open orders are closed or the market is closed. Please contact your desk supervisor to discuss.” <br>3. Schedule the replacement for the next permissible window (after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET). | Add a note with the scheduled time and a copy of the open‑order query results. |
| Trader has open orders **but** it is already after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET | Proceed with steps 1‑7, as the time window is valid. | No special action needed; just confirm the time check. |

### 4.3 IT Verification Code Missing or Mismatched  

| Condition | Action | Documentation |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| IT verification code is absent or does not match the supervisor’s code | 1. Contact the **IT Security Analyst** to retrieve the missing code. <br>2. If the analyst is unavailable, **escalate to the IT Security Manager**. <br>3. Once a valid code is received, continue with step 1‑7. | Capture the retrieved code and the timestamp of receipt. |
| Analyst refuses to provide code (e.g., pending investigation) | **Do not proceed**. Close the ticket as **“On Hold – IT verification pending”** and set a follow‑up reminder. | Log the reason for hold. |

## 6. Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them  

| Pitfall | Symptom | Prevention |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Skipping the open‑order check | Attempting re‑issue while orders are active → risk of inconsistent state | Always query OMS **before** any key‑generation step; screenshot the result. |
| Issuing key outside approved time window | Re‑issue during active trading hours | Use the system clock; if time is not within the window, defer. |
| Forgetting to capture verification codes | Audit trail incomplete → compliance breach | Mandatory fields in ticket must be filled; enforce via ticket‑template validation. |
| Not documenting escalation | Supervisor/manager unavailable but no record | Log every escalation with timestamp and recipient. |
| Enrolling the key before supervisor/IT approval | Two‑person rule violated | Ticket status must be **“Approved – Supervisor & IT”** before proceeding. |
