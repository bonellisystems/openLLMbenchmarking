## Role

You are one of three independent judges (Claude, Codex, Gemini) scoring a set
of blinded answers to a single MSP-realistic business task. You do not know
which model produced which answer -- every answer below is labeled only by a
letter. Do not try to guess model identity from writing style. Score only
what is on the page against the anchors below.

## Anchors

The following anchors define what a 0, 3, 5, 7, and 10 look like for this
business unit. Use them as the scoring scale for every answer.

# Knowledge Management -- scoring anchors (0-10)

Scale: integers 0-10. Anchors define 0/3/5/7/10; use 1-2/4/6/8-9 for answers between anchors.

## 10 -- Frontier
The runbook or document is testable: a newcomer could follow it step by step and reach the stated outcome without needing undocumented tribal knowledge. Prerequisites are listed up front, failure or rollback branches are included for steps that can go wrong, and a currency or ownership signal (last verified, owned by) is present when the prompt supports it.
Example character: numbered steps with prerequisites stated first, a rollback note on the one risky step, and a last-verified date.

## 7 -- Strong
The document is mostly step-by-step and usable as a runbook, but is missing one prerequisite check or a failure-branch note for a step that could plausibly fail. Nothing here requires guesswork to follow.
Example character: a clear numbered procedure that omits what to do if the third step fails.

## 5 -- Adequate
The content is a correct-gist prose description of the process rather than an executable, testable runbook -- a new reader would need to reverse-engineer the actual steps.
Example character: a paragraph explaining how the process generally works, without numbered, followable steps.

## 3 -- Weak
The document skips steps a newcomer would need, silently assumes undocumented tribal knowledge, and gives no ownership or currency signal at all.
Example character: a procedure that jumps from step one to step four with an unstated step in between.

## 0 -- Unusable
References fabricated tools, systems, or menu paths that do not exist, or gives a dangerously wrong sequence for a risky operation. Empty or off-task.

## Unit-specific red flags (deduct hard)
- Steps that silently assume undocumented tribal knowledge.
- Fabricated tool, system, or menu-path names presented as real.
- No failure or rollback branch for a step that can fail destructively.
- Prose narrative presented as a runbook instead of testable numbered steps.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Numbered steps a newcomer could execute unaided.
- Prerequisites listed explicitly up front.
- Failure or rollback branch given for risky steps.
- Currency or ownership signal (last-verified, owner) included when supported by the prompt.

#### **Prerequisites**  
1. **Backup System Readiness**:  
   - `BACKUP-CAD01` (on-prem backup) must be:

2. **Vendor Coordination**:  
   - CAD vendor must provide a clear health-check process and restoration timeline.  
   - Designated IT contact must have direct communication with the vendor.  

3. **Personnel Roles**:  
   - **Dispatcher**: Manages radio/paper fallback; logs calls into `BACKUP-CAD01`.  
   - **IT**: Activates `BACKUP-CAD01`; coordinates with vendor; handles data reconciliation.  

---

### **Failover Steps (Primary CAD Down)**  
1. **Declare Outage**:  
   - Detect primary CAD unresponsiveness (e.g., >5-minutes latency or failure to load).  
   - **Action**: Dispatch initiates COOP (paper/radio procedures).  

2. **Activate Backup System**:  
   - **IT Action**: Bring `BACKUP-CAD01` online.  
   - **Verification**: Confirm system stability (e.g., logs, dashboard).  

3. **Redirect Call Logging**:  
   - **Dispatcher Action**: Log all new calls into `BACKUP-CAD01`.  
   - **IT Action**: Validate real-time logging functionality.  

4. **Monitor Primary CAD**:  
   - **IT Action**: Track vendor updates and restoration status.  

---

### **Failback Steps (Primary CAD Restored)**  
5. **Confirm Primary Health**:  
   - **Vendor Action**: Confirm primary CAD is operational and passing health checks.  
   - **IT Action**: Validate via internal tests (e.g., load test, user access).  

6. **Export/Archive Backup Data**:  
   - **IT Action**: Export all calls logged in `BACKUP-CAD01` during the outage (start time: failover initiation; end time: before failback).  
     - **Destination**: Archive to a secure, immutable location (e.g., encrypted file in cloud storage).  
   - **Verification**: Ensure 100% data integrity (checksums, record counts).  

7. **Synchronize Data to Primary CAD**:  
   - **Action**: Use the sync protocol (manual/API) to import archived data into the primary CAD.  
   - **Verification**: Confirm no duplicates, missing records, or corruption.  

8. **Instruct Switchback**:  
   - **IT Action**: Notify dispatch to resume logging on the primary CAD.  
   - **Timing**: Only after Step 7 is fully verified.  

9. **Post-Switchback Validation**:  
   - **IT Action**: Monitor primary CAD for 30+ minutes.  
   - **Dispatcher Action**: Confirm real-time call logging is uninterrupted.  

10. **Archive Backup System**:  
    - **IT Action**: Deactivate `BACKUP-CAD01` and archive its raw logs per retention policy.

**Why This Step is Prone to Failure**:  
- Manual data entry/API sync risks duplicates, missing records, or corruption if not meticulously validated.  

**Failure Scenarios & Rollback**:  
- **Scenario 1**: Sync fails (e.g., API error, import corruption).  
  - **Rollback**:  
    1. **Revert**: Delete corrupted data from primary CAD.  
    2. **Re-export**: Re-download archived data from Step 6.  
    3. **Retry Sync**: Use alternative method (e.g., manual entry if API fails).  
    4. **Escalate**: Notify CAD vendor for API support if needed.  

- **Scenario 2**: Data integrity issues (e.g., mismatched records).  
  1. **Halt Sync**: Flag discrepancies immediately.  
  2. **Reconcile**: Manually compare archived logs with primary CAD.  
  3. **Correct**: Fix errors in primary CAD or re-export corrected data.  

---

> **"Not yet. We’ve restored primary CAD, but we must finalize data reconciliation. We’ve archived all backup logs and are now syncing them to the primary system. Once verified (expected in 15 mins), we’ll archive the backup system and confirm full closure."**  

#### **Closure Checklist**:  
- [ ] Data sync (Step 7) verified.  
- [ ] `BACKUP-CAD01` deactivated and archived.  
- [ ] All logs from outage period exist in primary CAD.  
- [ ] Vendor incident formally closed.  

--- 

**Key Takeaways**:  
- **Data Sync is Critical**: Never skip Step 7. Unreconciled backup data compromises audit trails and emergency response records.  
- **Backup System Lifespan**: Keep `BACKUP-CAD01` active until data sync is complete (per Step 6–7).  
- **Vendor Accountability**: Hold vendors accountable for restoration timelines and API support.

# Runbook: 911 CAD Failover & Failback Protocol
**System:** County 911 Dispatch Center  
**Primary:** Vendor-Hosted CAD  
**Backup:** On-Premise `BACKUP-CAD01`  
**Version:** 1.0 (Post-Incident Update)  
**Status:** Active

---

## Prerequisites

Before initiating any failover or failback procedure, the following must be confirmed:
1.  **Hardware Readiness:** `BACKUP-CAD01` console is powered on, networked, and verified as bootable *before* a primary outage occurs (not on-demand).
2.  **Vendor Contact:** Primary vendor emergency escalation number is verified and accessible.
3.  **Witness:** A second operator or IT lead must be present to validate data integrity during the reconciliation window.
4.  **Paper/Radio COOP:** Physical COOP materials (paper pads, radios) are accessible immediately for the transition gap.

---

## Phase 1: Failover (Primary Unresponsive)

1.  **Verify Primary Failure.** Confirm the vendor-hosted CAD is unresponsive via at least two methods (e.g., UI check + phone to vendor). Do not declare failure based on a single screen freeze.
2.  **Activate COOP.** Switch all active dispatchers to Paper/Radio immediately. Acknowledge the outage to the Public Information Officer.
3.  **Stand Up `BACKUP-CAD01`.** Power on and boot the on-premise backup console. Verify it accepts network traffic and begins writing logs to the local disk.
4.  **Confirm Traffic Flow.** Direct all new call intake to `BACKUP-CAD01`. Verify that new call records are appearing in real-time on the backup screen.
5.  **Notify Vendor.** Contact the primary CAD vendor to report the outage. Establish a timeline or ETA for restoration.

---

## Phase 2: Data Reconciliation (Required)

**The Data Window:** From the moment `BACKUP-CAD01` accepts traffic (Step 4 above) until `BACKUP-CAD01` is cleared of data for the next cycle (see Failure Branch below), all call records logged during this period are **active and fragile**.

1.  **Export Backup Logs.** Once the primary CAD is confirmed healthy and traffic is switching back, export the full log file from `BACKUP-CAD01` covering the outage window (e.g., 14:07–16:42).
2.  **Validate Completeness.** Cross-reference the exported backup logs against the primary CAD's record of the same time window. Ensure no calls were double-entered, truncated, or dropped during the handoff.
3.  **Archive & Purge.** Move the exported backup logs to the county's official cold storage/archive system. Only *after* archival is complete should `BACKUP-CAD01` be considered safe to leave idle. Do not leave `BACKUP-CAD01` running with unarchived data.

---

## Phase 3: Failback (Primary Restored)

1.  **Confirm Primary Health.** Verify the primary vendor CAD is passing health checks and accepting traffic for a minimum of 5 minutes before declaring it stable.
2.  **Switch Traffic.** Direct all new call intake back to the primary CAD.
3.  **Monitor.** Confirm that all dispatchers are successfully logging to the primary system for a minimum of 15 minutes.
4.  **Declare Resolution.** Confirm resolution with the Public Information Officer.

---

## ⚠️ Critical Failure/Rollback Branch
**Step:** Failback (Phase 3, Step 2)  
**Why it is the hardest step:** The transcript shows IT switching traffic back at 16:42 and declaring "done" at 16:50 without reconciling the 2-hour window of data logged on `BACKUP-CAD01`. This creates duplicate records, orphaned data, or a permanent gap in the county's dispatch history.

**Failure Mode:** Traffic is switched back to Primary, but `BACKUP-CAD01` data is not reconciled, archived, or verified.

**Rollback Procedure:**

1.  **Halt Failback.** If any discrepancy is detected between `BACKUP-CAD01` logs and the Primary records during the reconciliation check, immediately revert traffic back to `BACKUP-CAD01`.
2.  **Isolate Data.** Do not allow new calls to flow to either system until the mismatch is resolved.
3.  **Fix.** Coordinate with IT to either merge the records, re-enter missing data, or purge duplicates from the Primary system.
4.  **Re-Verify.** Once the data is reconciled, run a second integrity check on `BACKUP-CAD01` to ensure no further issues exist.
5.  **Re-Attempt Failback.** Only attempt Phase 3, Step 2 again after the data integrity check passes.

---

## Correct Dispatcher Communication

**Scenario:** 16:50 — Dispatcher asks, "Primary's fine, we're all back on it. Are we done?"

**Do NOT say:** "Yes, we're done."

**Say instead:**

> "Primary is live and traffic is switched. However, we are currently running a data reconciliation check on the backup console logs to ensure no call records were lost or duplicated during the outage window. I will confirm full operational status once that verification is complete."

**Why:** This explicitly tells the dispatcher that the system is *functionally* operational but not yet *data-integrity* compliant. It prevents the assumption that the incident is fully closed until the data gap is resolved.

### Runbook: CAD Outage Response – Failover, Failback, and Data Reconciliation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Trigger:** Primary Vendor-Hosted CAD is unresponsive or degraded.  
**Objective:** Maintain call logging continuity via BACKUP-CAD01, restore Primary CAD operations, and ensure all data captured in the backup is reconciled into the primary system before declaring resolution.

---

### Prerequisites
1.  **BACKUP-CAD01 Maintenance:** BACKUP-CAD01 (on-prem) must be kept in a "hot standby" state with recent software patches and configuration matching Primary CAD.
2.  **Network Path:** Dedicated, tested network path to BACKUP-CAD01 must be functional independent of the vendor-hosted Primary CAD link.
3.  **Reconciliation Procedure:** Documented export/import format between BACKUP-CAD01 and Primary CAD (e.g., XML/CSV schema mapping, API endpoint, or database merge protocol) must be available and tested quarterly.
4.  **Access Control:** IT and Dispatcher personnel must have current credentials for both BACKUP-CAD01 and Primary CAD administrative functions.
5.  **COOP Awareness:** Dispatchers trained on the manual fallback (Paper/Radio) protocol in addition to BACKUP-CAD01.

---

### Phase 1: Failover
*Goal: Establish logging continuity when Primary CAD is down.*

1.  **Verify Primary Outage:** Confirm Primary CAD is unresponsive via automated health checks or vendor status page.
2.  **Activate Manual COOP (Immediate):**
    *   *Action:* Dispatchers transition to Paper/Radio logging immediately per COOP. This ensures zero gap in call capture while IT prepares the digital fallback.
    *   *Owner:* Dispatcher Supervisor.
3.  **Stand Up BACKUP-CAD01:**
    *   *Action:* IT provisions and brings up BACKUP-CAD01 as the active logging system. Verify service status, database connectivity, and network reachability.
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations.
4.  **Validate Backup Logging:**
    *   *Action:* Dispatcher validates that BACKUP-CAD01 is accepting inputs and creating records. Perform a test call entry to confirm record persistence.
    *   *Owner:* Dispatcher.
5.  **Transition Active Logging:**
    *   *Action:* Dispatchers cease Paper/Radio logging for new calls and switch active logging to BACKUP-CAD01.
    *   *Confirmation:* Dispatcher announces "BACKUP-CAD01 active, logging all new calls."
    *   *Owner:* Dispatcher.

---

### Phase 2: Failback
*Goal: Restore Primary CAD as the active system while preserving all data recorded in BACKUP-CAD01.*

6.  **Confirm Primary Restoration:**
    *   *Action:* IT receives confirmation from Vendor that Primary CAD is restored and passing internal health checks.
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations.
7.  **⚠️ HIGH-RISK STEP: Data Reconciliation & Merge**
    *   *Context:* **This is the step most prone to failure.** Simply switching calls to Primary without reconciling data leaves records stranded in BACKUP-CAD01. If BACKUP-CAD01 is decommissioned, restarted, or fails over again, the data is lost or orphaned.
    *   *Action:*
        1.  Identify the timestamp of the Failover completion (Step 5). All records created in BACKUP-CAD01 from that timestamp forward must be accounted for.
        2.  Execute the **Reconciliation Procedure** (Prerequisite #3). Export records from BACKUP-CAD01 and import/merge them into Primary CAD.
        3.  **Validate Match:** Compare record counts and spot-check critical records (call timestamps, officer assignments, unit IDs) between BACKUP-CAD01 and Primary CAD.
        4.  *Success Criteria:* 100% of BACKUP-CAD01 records exist in Primary CAD with identical integrity.
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations (with Dispatcher spot-check).
8.  **Switch Traffic to Primary:**
    *   *Action:* Once reconciliation is verified, IT routes active call logging to Primary CAD. New calls are logged in Primary.
    *   *Confirmation:* IT announces "Traffic switched to Primary as of [Time]."
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations.
9.  **Confirm Dispatcher Readiness:**
    *   *Action:* Dispatcher confirms Primary CAD is responsive and all call streams are normal.
    *   *Owner:* Dispatcher.

---

### Phase 3: Closure
*Goal: Decommission BACKUP-CAD01 safely and close the incident.*

10. **Declare Resolution:**
    *   *Action:* Incident is marked resolved in CAD/Incident Management.
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations.
11. **Post-Incident Documentation:**
    *   *Action:* Log duration of outage, reconciliation time, and any anomalies during the merge.
    *   *Owner:* IT Operations.

---

### ❌ Failure / Rollback Branch: Step 7 (Data Reconciliation)

**Trigger Conditions:**
*   Reconciliation procedure fails (error during export/import).
*   Record counts do not match after merge attempt.
*   Imported records in Primary CAD show corruption or missing fields.
*   Primary CAD rejects the merge payload.

**Rollback Actions:**
1.  **STOP Failback:** Do **NOT** execute Step 8. Do not switch traffic to Primary until reconciliation succeeds.
2.  **Maintain BACKUP-CAD01:** Keep BACKUP-CAD01 active and logging. Dispatchers continue logging to BACKUP-CAD01.
3.  **Triage:** IT opens a war room ticket with the Vendor. Determine if the issue is a schema mismatch, a Primary CAD configuration issue, or a backup corruption issue.
4.  **Alternative Path:** If automated reconciliation is blocked, evaluate manual data entry of critical records into Primary via API or admin override, prioritizing active/open calls.
5.  **Communicate:** Notify Dispatcher Supervisor that failback is delayed due to data integrity risks. Update stakeholders on ETA.

---

### Response to the 16:50 Dispatcher Query

**Transcript Context:** At 16:50, the Dispatcher asks, *"Are we done?"*  
**Incorrect Response (from transcript):** Implied "Yes" based on Primary being fine.  
**Correct Response:**

> "No, we are not done yet. Primary is live, but we still have calls between 14:07 and 16:42 that are only sitting in BACKUP-CAD01. We have to finish the data reconciliation now to merge those records into Primary. Once IT confirms the merge is complete and the counts match, then we can consider BACKUP-CAD01 safe to leave idle and we'll declare this fully resolved."

**Why this matters:**
If the dispatcher accepts "We're done" at 16:50, the team may prematurely power down or lose access to BACKUP-CAD01. Any records remaining in BACKUP-CAD01 would be lost, creating permanent gaps in the 911 record of calls handled during the outage. The system is only safe to leave idle once its data has been successfully transferred to the primary system.

**Runbook: CAD Outage Response and Recovery**

**Prerequisites:**

* The county 911 dispatch center's CAD system has a vendor-hosted primary instance and an on-prem backup dispatch console, BACKUP-CAD01.
* The backup console, BACKUP-CAD01, is configured and tested to handle logging and dispatch operations.
* The COOP (Continuity of Operations Plan) for the dispatch center is in place and understood by all personnel.

**Step 1: Failover to Backup Console (BACKUP-CAD01)**

1.1. In the event of a primary CAD system outage, dispatchers will switch to paper/radio operations per COOP.
1.2. IT will bring up the on-prem backup dispatch console, BACKUP-CAD01, as the fallback logging system.
1.3. Dispatchers will log all new calls directly to BACKUP-CAD01 until the primary system is restored.

**Step 2: Primary System Restoration and Data Synchronization**

2.1. Once the primary CAD system is restored and passes the vendor's health check, IT will confirm with the dispatcher that the primary system is ready for failback.
2.2. **Critical Step:** Before cutting back to the primary system, ensure that all data logged on BACKUP-CAD01 between the time of failover (14:07) and failback (16:42) is synchronized with the primary system. This includes:
	* Manually entering all calls logged on BACKUP-CAD01 into the primary system.
	* Verifying that all call information, including timestamps, caller details, and incident descriptions, is accurately transferred.
	* **Failure/Rollback Branch:** If data synchronization fails or is incomplete, do not proceed with failback. Instead:
		+ Continue using BACKUP-CAD01 as the primary logging system.
		+ Escalate the issue to senior IT personnel for assistance with data synchronization.

**Step 3: Failback to Primary System**

3.1. Once data synchronization is complete and verified, IT will cut back to the primary system, and new calls will be logged there again.
3.2. Dispatchers will verify that the primary system is functioning correctly and that all data is up-to-date.

**Step 4: Post-Failback Verification and BACKUP-CAD01 Data Cleanup**

4.1. After failback, dispatchers should not consider the incident fully resolved until:
	* All data logged on BACKUP-CAD01 has been verified to be accurately reflected in the primary system.
	* BACKUP-CAD01's database has been cleared of temporary data logged during the outage.
4.2. Instead of simply saying "yes, we're done," the IT personnel should inform the dispatcher that:
	* The primary system has been restored and verified.
	* Data synchronization with BACKUP-CAD01 has been completed.
	* BACKUP-CAD01's data has been cleared, and the system is ready for future use as a backup.

By following this runbook, the county 911 dispatch center can ensure a smooth failover and failback process, minimizing disruptions to emergency services and maintaining data integrity.

**Runbook: CAD System Outage – Failover to BACKUP-CAD01 and Failback to Primary CAD**

**Purpose:**  
Provide a clear, step‑by‑step procedure for dispatch operations to switch from the primary, vendor‑hosted CAD system to the on‑premises backup console (BACKUP‑CAD01) during an outage, and to safely revert (failback) once the primary system is restored. The runbook also defines data‑reconciliation steps required before the backup system can be left idle again.

---

### Prerequisites (must be verified **before** an outage occurs)

1. **Backup System Readiness**  
   - BACKUP‑CAD01 is installed, licensed, and fully patched.  
   - Network connectivity (VPN, firewall rules) between dispatch workstations and BACKUP‑CAD01 is tested and documented.  
   - All dispatchers have been trained on the manual logging workflow for BACKUP‑CAD01 (paper‑to‑digital entry, radio read‑backs, etc.).  

2. **Data Synchronization Baseline**  
   - At least once per shift, perform a **checksum/record‑count comparison** between primary CAD and BACKUP‑CAD01 to confirm they are in sync.  
   - Document the last successful sync timestamp (e.g., “Sync verified 13:55 on 2024‑07‑15”).  

3. **Communication Plan**  
   - Designate a **CAD Outage Liaison** (usually the IT lead) who will coordinate status updates with dispatch supervisors.  
   - Ensure the liaison has direct contact (radio channel, secure chat) with all shift supervisors.  

4. **Documentation & Audit Trail**  
   - Maintain a **CAD Outage Log** (electronic or paper) that records: time of outage detection, actions taken, responsible personnel, and timestamps for each step.  

---

### 1. Detection & Initiation of Failover (Primary CAD Unresponsive)

| Time | Action | Owner | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| **T₀** | Dispatcher observes primary CAD unresponsive (no login, no response to API calls). | Dispatcher | Trigger COOP (Continuity of Operations Plan). |
| **T₀ + ≤2 min** | Announce “Primary CAD down – initiating fallback per COOP.” on the dispatch radio channel. | Dispatcher | All dispatchers stop using primary CAD. |
| **T₀ + ≤2 min** | IT Lead confirms receipt and begins failover procedure. | IT Lead | |

---

### 2. Failover – Bring UP BACKUP‑CAD01

**Step 2.1 – Power‑On / Service Start**  
1. Power on BACKUP‑CAD01 if not already running (follow hardware power‑on checklist).  
2. Verify OS boot and network services (ping, SSH/remote desktop).  

**Step 2.2 – Application Initialization**  
1. Start the CAD backup application (e.g., `sudo systemctl start backup-cad`).  
2. Monitor startup logs for errors (look for “Server listening on port 443” or equivalent).  

**Step 2.3 – Connectivity Test**  
1. From a dispatcher workstation, attempt to log a **test incident** (e.g., “Test – Failover Verification”).  
2. Confirm the test record appears in BACKUP‑CAD01’s UI and database.  

**Step 2.4 – Announcement**  
- IT Lead broadcasts: “BACKUP‑CAD01 is operational. All new calls must be logged here until primary CAD is restored.”  

**Rollback Condition (Step 2.2 Failure):**  
- If the application fails to start **or** critical services (database, API) are unavailable, **do not** proceed to Step 3.  
- **Rollback:**  
  1. Abort any partial configuration changes.  
  2. Document the failure in the CAD Outage Log.  
  3. Re‑attempt failover after addressing the root cause (e.g., missing patch, corrupted config).  
  4. If repeated attempts fail, escalate to vendor support with a **“Backup CAD Unusable”** ticket.

| Action | Owner | Timing |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Log all incoming 911 calls directly into BACKUP‑CAD01. | Dispatchers | Continuous until failback. |
| Maintain paper log (if required by policy) for each call, noting the BACKUP‑CAD01 incident ID. | Dispatchers | Continuous. |

### 4. Vendor Confirms Primary CAD Restoration

**Step 4.1 – Receive Confirmation**  
- IT Lead obtains written (email/secure chat) confirmation from vendor that primary CAD has passed all health checks and is accepting traffic.  

**Step 4.2 – Pre‑Failback Verification**

2. **Data Integrity Check** – Verify that the test record is persisted (viewable in the primary CAD UI and backend database).

### 5. Failback – Switch New Calls Back to Primary CAD

| Time | Action | Owner | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-------|

### 6. Post‑Failback – Secure BACKUP‑CAD01 for Idle State

**6.1 Data Archive (mandatory before idle)**  
1. **Create a read‑only archive** of the BACKUP‑CAD01 database (e.g., `pg_dump -Fc -f cad_backup_20240715_1407-1642.dump`).

**6.2 System Quiescence**

  - Archive details.

# County 911 Dispatch Center – CAD Outage Runbook  
**Purpose** – Provide a repeatable, auditable process for handling a loss of the primary (vendor‑hosted) Computer‑Aided‑Dispatch (CAD) system, switching to the on‑premises backup console (BACKUP‑CAD01), and safely returning to the primary once it is restored.

> **Scope** – This runbook covers the *fail‑over* (primary → backup) and *fail‑back* (backup → primary) phases, including data‑integrity verification, communication with dispatchers, and a rollback plan for the step most prone to error (synchronizing call‑log data from BACKUP‑CAD01 to the primary system).

---  

## 1. Prerequisites  *(must be satisfied before the runbook is initiated)*  

| # | Item | Detail / Owner |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | **COOP (Continuity of Operations Plan) activation checklist** | Verify that paper/radio COOP procedures are ready and that all dispatchers have the COOP packet. |
| 2 | **Backup CAD server (BACKUP‑CAD01) status** | Confirm hardware health, network connectivity, and that the latest nightly backup of the primary CAD schema has been restored to BACKUP‑CAD01 (within the last 24 h). |
| 3 | **Access credentials** | Ensure the on‑call IT technician has local admin rights on BACKUP‑CAD01 and VPN/remote console access to the vendor portal. |
| 4 | **Logging & audit tools** | Enable syslog capture on both primary and backup consoles and verify that the “CAD‑Sync” script (see Section 4) is present on the IT admin workstation. |
| 5 | **Communication list** | Up‑to‑date contact list for: Dispatch supervisors, on‑call IT, vendor support, and senior leadership. |
| 6 | **Time synchronization** | NTP must be confirmed on both CAD servers; all timestamps must be consistent to the second. |
| 7 | **Tested rollback procedure** | A documented “rollback to backup only” procedure must be in the incident response folder (Section 7). |

*If any prerequisite is missing, abort the runbook, notify leadership, and follow the “Escalation” path in the COOP.*

| Acronym | Meaning |

## 3. Fail‑over Procedure (Primary → Backup)

| **3** | **Activate COOP** – Switch to paper/radio logging per COOP SOP. | Dispatch Supervisor | Dispatchers begin using COOP forms. |

    *   Attempt merge again after root cause analysis.
    *   If retry fails after two attempts, maintain operation on BACKUP-CAD01 and request Vendor support for manual data recovery.

### Runbook: CAD Primary Failure – Failover and Failback

**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Primary CAD outage, utilization of BACKUP-CAD01, data integrity preservation, and return to service.  
**Trigger:** Primary CAD unresponsive or failing health checks.

---

#### Prerequisites
*   **Access:** IT must have administrative credentials for BACKUP-CAD01 and Primary CAD.
*   **Contacts:** Vendor escalation path active; COOP activation list ready.
*   **Verification Tool:** CAD Log Reconciliation Utility (or equivalent) available for data comparison.
*   **Backup State:** BACKUP-CAD01 must be in "Cold Standby" status with database synchronization paused prior to this incident.

---

#### Procedure

**1. Detection & Failover Declaration**
*   **Dispatcher:** Confirm Primary CAD unresponsiveness. Immediately invoke COOP protocol; begin paper/radio logging.
*   **IT:** Acknowledge failure. Notify vendor of outage.
*   **Action:** Declare **Cad_Failover_Active** in status dashboard.

**2. Backup System Activation**
*   **IT:** Boot BACKUP-CAD01. Verify network connectivity and database write permissions.
*   **IT:** Configure BACKUP-CAD01 to accept traffic; disable sync to Primary to prevent corruption.
*   **Dispatcher:** Verify BACKUP-CAD01 interface is responsive.
*   **Action:** Log a **Test Call** on BACKUP-CAD01 to confirm data persistence. Verify test call appears in local backup database.

**3. Backup Operational Status**
*   **Dispatcher:** Begin logging all new calls on BACKUP-CAD01.
*   **IT:** Monitor BACKUP-CAD01 CPU/DB utilization. Ensure no "Write Fail" errors.
*   **Action:** Maintain COOP protocol for any calls missed during the transition window.

**4. Primary Recovery Monitoring**
*   **IT:** Monitor vendor updates. Verify Primary CAD returns to `Healthy` state and passes vendor health check.
*   **Action:** Mark Primary as `Restored_Awaiting_Reconciliation`. **DO NOT** switch traffic yet.

**5. Data Reconciliation & Validation (CRITICAL STEP)**
*   *Note: This step is the most common point of failure. Dispatchers may pressure for immediate failback; data loss occurs if this is skipped or rushed.*
*   **IT:** Pause new call logging on BACKUP-CAD01.
*   **IT:** Initiate **Log Export** from BACKUP-CAD01 for all records between `Failover_Time` and `Now`.
*   **IT:** Run **Reconciliation Utility** to compare BACKUP-CAD01 exports against Primary CAD.
    *   *Check:* Verify all BACKUP-CAD01 calls exist in Primary.
    *   *Check:* Verify no duplicates (Primary may have cached partial writes).
    *   *Check:* Verify timestamps and call data integrity.
*   **IT:** If reconciliation fails, execute **Branch A: Reconciliation Failure**.
*   **Action:** If reconciliation is successful, execute **Ingestion/Commit**. Mark Primary as `Ready_Failback`.

**6. Traffic Failback**
*   **IT:** Switch CAD load balancer/DNS to route new calls to Primary.
*   **IT:** Verify Primary is accepting traffic (test call).
*   **Action:** Mark status as `Failback_Completed`.

**7. Post-Failback Verification & Decommission**
*   **Dispatcher:** Resume normal operations on Primary. Confirm no call drops or UI errors.
*   **IT:** Archive BACKUP-CAD01 session data.
*   **IT:** Notify Dispatcher that BACKUP-CAD01 data has been preserved and is now safe to leave idle/decommission.
*   **Action:** Transition status to `Normal_Ops`. Close COOP protocol.

---

#### Handling BACKUP-CAD01 Data & Idle State
**Before BACKUP-CAD01 is safe to leave idle/decommission, the following must occur:**
1.  **Immutability:** The BACKUP-CAD01 session must be locked in read-only mode immediately after Step 5 begins to prevent accidental new writes during reconciliation.
2.  **Export & Merge:** All call records (metadata, voice file references, officer notes) must be exported and successfully ingested into the Primary CAD database.
3.  **Audit Sign-Off:** IT must confirm the record count and hash checks match between the export and the Primary database.
4.  **Retention:** BACKUP-CAD01 data must be archived to long-term cold storage before the system is powered down or returned to standby, ensuring no single point of data loss for the outage window.
5.  **Verification:** The Dispatcher must verify that the **Test Call** logged during Step 3 is visible in Primary CAD and matches the backup record.

---

#### Failure / Rollback Branch
**Target Step:** Step 5 (Data Reconciliation & Validation).  
**Risk:** Skipping or failing this step results in loss of all calls logged during the outage or duplicate records that corrupt officer history.

**Scenario A: Reconciliation Utility Reports Mismatch or Error**
1.  **Abort Failback:** Do not switch traffic to Primary.
2.  **Resume Backup:** IT commands Dispatcher to resume logging on BACKUP-CAD01 immediately.
3.  **Diagnose:** IT isolates cause (e.g., schema drift, corruption, network packet loss).
4.  **Mitigate:**
    *   If fixable: Retry reconciliation.
    *   If unfixable: Engage Vendor L3 Support immediately.
    *   *Decision:* If data cannot be reconciled within SLA, BACKUP-CAD01 remains the **Source of Truth**. Primary is left offline or used read-only.

**Scenario B: Primary Fails Health Check During Reconciliation**
1.  **Abort:** Stop reconciliation.
2.  **Resume Backup:** Return all traffic to BACKUP-CAD01.

* **Action:** IT notifies Dispatch Lead: *"BACKUP-CAD01 is online. You may resume digital logging on backup consoles."*

### Step 5: Confirm Vendor Restoration

* **Validation:** IT verifies record counts match.
  * *Check:* `Count(Backup_Export) == Count(Primary_Import)`
  * *Check:* Audit timestamps for the incident window are preserved.
* **Result:** Primary CAD now contains all data, including the "orphaned" records from the backup window.

> 4. **INVESTIGATE:** IT engages Vendor to debug sync tool or database state.
> 5. **RESUME:** Continue operating on BACKUP-CAD01 until sync issue is resolved and a retry succeeds.

### Step 7: Cutover Traffic
* **Pre-requisite:** Step 6 completed successfully.
* **Action:** IT switches dispatch routing/load balancers to point to Primary CAD.
* **Communication:** IT notifies Dispatch Lead: *"Cutover complete. Primary CAD is accepting new calls. BACKUP-CAD01 is now Read-Only."*

* **Action:** Dispatchers log a "Test Call" on Primary.
* **Action:** IT confirms Test Call appears in Primary database and syncs correctly.
* **Action:** Dispatch Lead confirms all posts are writing to Primary.
* **Validation:** IT and Dispatch Lead sign off on "Primary CAD Fully Operational."

### Step 9: Archive and Reset BACKUP-CAD01
* **Action:** IT creates a forensic snapshot of BACKUP-CAD01 for audit purposes.
* **Action:** IT exports the full incident log (covering the entire backup window) to secure archival storage.
* **Action:** IT wipes BACKUP-CAD01 state and restores to the clean base snapshot.

*All items must be verified and signed‑off before an outage occurs.*

## 3. Glossary  

- **Primary CAD** – Vendor‑hosted, cloud‑based dispatch console.  
- **Backup CAD (BACKUP‑CAD01)** – On‑prem, self‑hosted dispatch console used during outages.  
- **COOP Fallback** – Paper/radio dispatch method activated when both CADs are unavailable.  
- **Merge / Re‑sync** – Process of moving logs captured on BACKUP‑CAD01 back into Primary CAD before it is re‑enabled.  
- **Cut‑Back** – Switching dispatch traffic from the backup console back to the primary console.

## 4. Run‑Book Overview  

| Phase | Trigger | Primary Owner | Key Deliverables |
|-------|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **A – Detect & Declare Failure** | Primary CAD unresponsive or health‑check fails. | Dispatcher Lead | Formal outage declaration, activate COOP fallback. |
| **B – Switch to Backup CAD** | Primary CAD declared down. | IT – CAD Ops | Start BACKUP‑CAD01, redirect all new calls, confirm logging. |
| **C – Preserve & Validate Backup Logs** | While backup is in use (14:07‑16:42). | IT – Log Management | Continuous export of inbound logs to a secure, immutable store; schedule merge window. |

## 5. Detailed Step‑by‑Step Procedure  

### **Phase A – Detect & Declare Failure**

1. **A1 – Monitor Health**  
   - Primary CAD health‑check (via vendor API or internal ping) returns **“DOWN”** or no response for **> 2 minutes**.  
   - Dispatcher sees **“Primary CAD unresponsive”** in CAD status panel.

2. **A2 – Announce Outage**  
   - Dispatcher Lead broadcasts:  
     > *“Primary CAD has failed. Activating COOP paper/radio fallback now. All units, continue using radio until further notice.”*  

3. **A3 – Activate COOP Fallback** (Paper/Radio) – reference COOP SOP.  

> **Prerequisite**: COOP plan must be signed‑off and rehearsed quarterly.

---

### **Phase B – Switch to Backup CAD**

4. **B1 – Provision Backup Console**  
   - IT Ops runs:  
     ```bash
     ./start_backup_cad.sh --instance BACKUP-CAD01
     ```  
   - Verify services: `systemctl status cad-daemon`, `netstat -lnp | grep 8443`.

5. **B2 – Redirect Incoming Calls**  
   - Update DNS / load‑balancer rule (or firewall NAT) to route new 911 call sessions to **BACKUP‑CAD01** IP.  
   - Confirm traffic flow: `tcpdump -i eth0 port 8443 -c 5`.

6. **B3 – Verify Logging**  
   - Dispatcher confirms logs appear in **Backup CAD UI** under “Incoming Calls”.  
   - IT confirms **log collection agent** is running and writing to `/var/log/cad/backup/`.

7. **B4 – Communicate to Units**  
   - Dispatcher announces:  
     > *“All new 911 calls are now entering BACKUP‑CAD01. Continue to dispatch as usual; all actions will be logged here.”*

8. **B5 – Begin Continuous Log Export**  
   - Every 5 minutes, run:  
     ```bash
     ./export_logs.sh --src BACKUP-CAD01 --dest s3://cad-backup-logs/$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M)/ --batch 5m
     ```  
   - Store logs in an **immutable S3 bucket** (or on‑prem write‑once storage).  
   - Keep a **checksum file** (`sha256sum`) for each batch.

> **Easiest step to get wrong:** **B5 – Log Export**. Missing or incomplete batches will cause gaps in the final merge and can lead to lost calls.  
> **Failure/Rollback Branch** (see Section 6.3).

---

### **Phase C – Preserve & Validate Backup Logs (During Outage)**  

9. **C1 – Continuous Validation**  
   - Every hour, run `./log‑checksum‑verify.sh` to ensure exported batches match source checksums.  
   - Flag any **“checksum mismatch”** immediately to IT Ops.

10. **C2 – Archive Integrity**  
    - At the end of the outage (or when primary is restored), retain the entire export directory in a **read‑only snapshot** for audit.

11. **C3 – Prepare Merge Queue**  
    - Create a **merge‑job manifest** (`merge_job.txt`) listing each exported batch in chronological order.  
    - Example entry: `2025-10-28T14:10-14:15.log → merge_queue/14_10.log`.

> **Note:** No manual editing of log files is performed. All processing must be done via the **merge tool** described in Section 6.

---

### **Phase D – Validate Primary Restoration**

12. **D1 – Vendor Confirmation**  
    - IT contacts CAD vendor (via dedicated outage line). Vendor replies with:  
      > “Primary CAD is restored. Health‑check status: **GREEN**. Ready for traffic.”

13. **D2 – Internal Health‑Check**  
    - Run vendor‑provided health‑check script (`cad‑healthcheck --instance primary`).  
    - Confirm **All components = UP** and **Response Time < 2 s**.

14. **D3 – Document Read‑iness**  
    - IT logs the confirmation timestamp (e.g., `2025‑10‑28 16:35:12 UTC – Primary CAD restored, health‑check PASS`).

---

### **Phase E – Merge & Re‑sync Logs**  

> **Goal:** Transfer *all* logs captured on BACKUP‑CAD01 (14:07 → 16:42) into Primary CAD **without duplication, loss, or corruption**.  

15. **E1 – Pre‑Merge Clean‑Up**  
    - Verify no duplicate timestamps exist within the export directory (`dedupe.py`).  
    - Ensure **timezone alignment** (UTC) across all sources.

16. **E2 – Execute Merge Job**  
    - Run the validated merge script:  
      ```bash
      ./cad-merge-logs.sh \
        --src-dir /mnt/backup-logs/ \
        --dest-db "postgresql://cad_user:pwd@primary-cad-db/cad" \
        --manifest merge_job.txt \
        --log-file /var/log/cad/merge_$(date +%Y%m%d%H%M).log \
        --dedup true
      ```  

    - The script:  
      1. Reads each file in order.  
      2. Inserts records into Primary CAD’s event store using bulk API.  
      3. Performs **idempotency check** (record hash + call‑ID).  
      4. Writes a **merge‑audit record** (`merge_audit.csv`) per batch.

17. **E3 – Post‑Merge Validation**  
    - Run `./cad‑merge‑validate.sh --report /var/log/cad/merge_validation_$(date +%Y%m%d).pdf`.  
    - Validation checks:  
      - Record count matches sum of all exported batches.  
      - No **duplicate Call‑ID** errors.  
      - Latency < 5 s for each ingested batch.  
    - If any validation fails → go to **Phase H (Rollback)**.

18. **E4 – Finalize Merge**  
    - Archive the export directory to immutable storage with a **“MERGE‑COMPLETE”** tag.  
    - Delete temporary working copies (after audit sign‑off).  

> **Result:** Primary CAD now contains a complete, deduplicated record of every call that occurred while it was down.

---

### **Phase F – Cut‑Back to Primary**

19. **F1 – Traffic Switch**  
    - Re‑apply DNS / firewall rule to route new 911 call sessions back to the **Primary CAD** IP.  
    - Confirm traffic flow with `tcpdump` (`port 8443 -c 5`).  

20. **F2 – Announce Cut‑Back Completion**  
    - Dispatcher Lead announces:  
      > *“Primary CAD is back online. All units may return to normal dispatch procedures. All missed calls have been logged and merged into Primary CAD.”*  

21. **F3 – Post‑Cut‑Back Monitoring (30 min)**  
    - IT monitors:  
      - CAD error rate (< 0.1 %).  
      - Log ingestion latency (< 5 s).  
      - No duplicate entries in audit logs.  

22. **F4 – Close Incident**  
    - Incident Commander signs off the **“Outage Closed”** status.  

---

### **Phase G – Post‑Outage Review (AAR)**  

23. **G1 – Gather Metrics**  
    - Outage start/end timestamps, duration, number of calls handled via backup, merge‑job duration, validation errors (if any).  

24. **G2 – Document Lessons Learned**  
    - Update COOP SOP if any procedural gaps were identified.  
    - Revise merge‑tool configuration or schedule if validation failures occurred.  

25. **G3 – Communicate to Stakeholders**  
    - Send **Incident Summary** to County Board, Vendor, and all emergency services.

## 6. Failure / Rollback Branches  

### 6.1. **Primary CAD Restoration Fails**  
| Symptom | Immediate Action | Owner | Rollback |
|--------|-------------------|-------|----------|
| Vendor health‑check stays **RED** after 5 min | Cancel cut‑back, keep traffic on BACKUP‑CAD01. | IT – CAD Ops | Continue using backup; re‑attempt restoration later. |
| Primary CAD becomes **unresponsive again** after cut‑back | Re‑activate COOP fallback; revert traffic to backup. | Dispatcher Lead | Notify Vendor for deeper investigation. |

### 6.2. **Merge Validation Errors**  
| Error | Diagnosis | Remediation |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Duplicate Call‑ID detected | Two batches contain same call (e.g., retransmission). | Re‑run merge with `--skip-duplicates` flag; manually review offending batch. |
| Record count mismatch | Export missed some logs or export duplicated them. | Re‑export missing batches; verify checksums. |
| Ingestion latency > 30 s | Primary CAD overloaded. | Pause further ingestion, scale DB connection pool, retry after 2 min. |

If any validation fails **after** Phase E, execute **Phase H** (Rollback).

### 6.3. **Rollback Procedure (When Merge Fails)**  

1. **H1 – Preserve Current State**  
   - Tag the current Primary CAD DB snapshot (`cad-db-snapshot-$(date +%Y%m%d).sql.gz`).  
   - Keep the export directory **read‑only** (`chmod 500`).  

2. **H2 – Re‑activate Backup Console**  
   - Restore firewall/DNS rule to point **all new calls** back to BACKUP‑CAD01.  
   - Verify that logs continue to flow (`tail -f /var/log/cad/backup/app.log`).  

3. **H3 – Re‑import Logs to Backup** (if needed)  
   - Use `./export_logs.sh` to re‑export any **un‑merged** batches to a fresh archive (`/mnt/backup-undo/`).  

4. **H4 – Notify Stakeholders**  
   - Dispatcher announces:  
     > *“Primary CAD cannot accept merged logs at this time. All calls will continue to be processed via BACKUP‑CAD01. We are investigating and will re‑attempt merge once stability is confirmed.”*  

5. **H5 – Attempt Alternate Merge**  
   - If the issue is **temporary overload**, wait 5 minutes, then retry merge with `--throttle 50`.  
   - If still failing, **escalate** to Vendor and consider **partial manual entry** (only high‑priority calls).  

6. **H6 – Document & Close**  
   - Record root cause, time taken, and lessons learned.  
   - Schedule a **post‑mortem** within 48 hours.

# **Runbook: CAD System Outage – Failover & Failback Procedure**  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Applicable Systems:** Primary Vendor-Hosted CAD, On-Prem Backup CAD (BACKUP-CAD01)  
**Purpose:** Standardize response to CAD system outages, including failover to backup system and safe failback to primary, with data integrity and continuity assurance.  

---

## **Prerequisites**

Before executing this runbook, ensure the following:

1. **Personnel Roles Assigned:**
   - **Dispatcher Lead:** Oversees communication, logs, and operational continuity.
   - **IT Incident Lead:** Coordinates technical response, system checks, and data transfer.
   - **Backup CAD Admin:** Manages BACKUP-CAD01 configuration and data export.

2. **Required Tools & Access:**
   - Access to BACKUP-CAD01 (local console or secure remote).
   - Secure file transfer mechanism (e.g., SFTP, encrypted USB).
   - Primary CAD vendor support contact (on-call).
   - Paper logging forms (COOP kits) available at all dispatch positions.
   - Change control/rollback plan approved by IT and Operations.

3. **Pre-Configured Systems:**
   - BACKUP-CAD01 is maintained weekly (patches, backups, test boots).
   - Data export templates/format mappings exist between BACKUP-CAD01 and primary CAD.
   - Audit log review process documented.

---

## **Section 1: Failover Procedure (Primary CAD Down)**

**Trigger:** Primary CAD is unresponsive or declared down by vendor or internal monitoring.

| Step | Action | Owner | Verification |
|------|-------|-------|--------------|
| 1.01 | Confirm outage: Attempt to access primary CAD from multiple workstations. | Dispatcher / IT | No login or response after 2 minutes. |
| 1.02 | Notify IT Incident Lead and Dispatcher Lead via secure channel (e.g., secondary radio or cell). | Dispatcher | Acknowledged by both leads. |
| 1.03 | Declare COOP activation. Begin using paper logging and radio for all calls. | Dispatcher Lead | All units notified; paper logs initiated. |
| 1.04 | IT powers on and authenticates to BACKUP-CAD01. Verify local network connectivity and database status. | IT | BACKUP-CAD01 boots; login successful. |
| 1.05 | Validate BACKUP-CAD01 functionality: Create test incident, save, and retrieve. | IT | Test incident persists. |
| 1.06 | Notify dispatch team that BACKUP-CAD01 is operational. All new calls to be logged here. | IT → Dispatcher Lead | Confirmed receipt by dispatch team. |
| 1.07 | Dispatcher transitions to BACKUP-CAD01. Continue using paper logs until transition complete. | Dispatchers | All new entries in BACKUP-CAD01 post-activation. |
| 1.08 | IT contacts primary CAD vendor to report outage and request status/ETA. | IT | Ticket opened; vendor acknowledgment received. |

> ✅ **Failover Complete when:** All new incidents are being logged in BACKUP-CAD01 or paper (if backup unavailable).

---

## **Section 2: Failback Procedure (Primary CAD Restored)**

**Trigger:** Primary CAD vendor confirms system restoration and passes health checks.

| Step | Action | Owner | Verification |
|------|-------|-------|--------------|
| 2.01 | IT verifies primary CAD is accessible, responsive, and stable across multiple sessions. | IT | System responds within 5 seconds; no errors. |
| 2.02 | IT notifies Dispatcher Lead that failback is possible. Do **not** cut over yet. | IT | Dispatcher Lead acknowledges. |
| 2.03 | **PAUSE – Data Export Required (Critical Step):**  
Export all records from BACKUP-CAD01 created between failover start and current time.  
- Filter logs from **[Failover Start Time]** to **[Now]**  
- Export in `.CSV` or vendor-compatible format (e.g., XML)  
- Include call details, timestamps, units dispatched, status changes. | IT / Backup CAD Admin | File generated and checksum verified. |
| 2.04 | Securely transfer export file to IT staging server for import prep. | IT | Transfer logged; file integrity confirmed. |
| 2.05 | Validate export data:  
- Check for missing or corrupted entries  
- Match count against paper logs (if used)  
- Confirm time range accuracy | IT + Dispatcher Lead | Report signed off by both. |
| 2.06 | Schedule data import during low-call volume (if not immediate). | IT | Approval obtained from Operations. |
| 2.07 | Import validated data into primary CAD using vendor-approved method. | IT | Import log shows 100% success; no duplicates. |
| 2.08 | Cross-check 3–5 random imported incidents with original BACKUP-CAD01 and paper logs. | Dispatcher Lead + IT | All match; no discrepancies. |
| 2.09 | Announce cut-over: All **new** calls now resume in primary CAD as of `[Time]`. | IT → Dispatcher Lead | Dispatchers confirm switch. |
| 2.10 | Update incident log: Document outage duration, actions taken, data migration. | Dispatcher Lead | Log filed in incident management system. |

> ✅ **Failback Complete when:** New calls are logged in primary CAD **and** all backup data is successfully imported and verified.

---

## **Section 3: Failure Handling & Rollback Branch**

> ⚠️ **Highest-Risk Step:** **2.07 – Data Import into Primary CAD**  
> This step is most likely to fail due to format mismatches, duplicates, or system errors.

### **Failure Condition:**
- Import fails or partial success (e.g., 80% imported, errors in log).
- Duplicates created in primary CAD.
- Data corruption or missing fields post-import.

### **Rollback Procedure:**

| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|-------|-------|
| F.01 | **Halt import immediately.** Do not retry without analysis. | IT |
| F.02 | Revert primary CAD to pre-import state using last known good backup (if available). | IT |
| F.03 | Notify Dispatcher Lead of delay; continue logging in primary CAD **only new calls**. | IT |
| F.04 | Analyze error logs and export file. Validate mapping rules and data cleanliness. | IT + Vendor Support |
| F.05 | Correct issues (e.g., deduplicate, reformat) and re-export. | IT |
| F.06 | Re-attempt import in test environment first (if available). | IT |
| F.07 | Upon success, proceed with production import and re-validate. | IT |
| F.08 | Document root cause and update runbook accordingly. | IT + Ops |

> ❌ **If rollback fails or primary CAD becomes unstable:**  
> Return to BACKUP-CAD01 for **all** logging (new and historical gap-filling) until resolved.

Do not power down BACKUP-CAD01 yet. We will notify you when the data migration is confirmed and the system is safe to idle."**

### **Post-Outage Steps:**

| Step | Action | Owner | Deadline |
|------|-------|-------|---------|

| 4.02 | Power down BACKUP-CAD01 **only after** data is confirmed migrated. | IT | After Step 4.01. |
| 4.03 | Conduct post-mortem: Document cause, timeline, gaps, and improvements. | IT + Ops | Within 72 hours. |
| 4.04 | Update runbook based on lessons learned. | IT + Training Lead | Within 1 week. |
| 4.05 | Train dispatch and IT teams on updates. | Training Lead | Within 2 weeks. |

Runbook: Primary CAD Outage -- Failover to BACKUP-CAD01 and Failback

Prerequisites: BACKUP-CAD01 must be kept patched and ready to bring up on short notice; on-call

Failover:
1. Once dispatch confirms primary CAD is unresponsive, immediately have dispatchers begin the
   COOP paper/radio fallback for any calls in flight during the transition.
2. IT brings up BACKUP-CAD01 and confirms it is accepting new call entries.
3. Once BACKUP-CAD01 is confirmed up, dispatchers log ALL new calls there directly -- record
   the exact time BACKUP-CAD01 logging starts; you will need this timestamp for reconciliation
   later.
4. IT contacts the primary CAD vendor to report the outage and get an ETA; check back
   periodically rather than waiting for the vendor to proactively update.

Failback (do not skip -- this is the step most likely to be treated as "done" prematurely):
5. Once the vendor confirms primary CAD is restored AND passing its own health check, do NOT
   immediately declare the incident over. Before cutting dispatch back to primary, note the
   exact time BACKUP-CAD01 logging will stop.
6. Cut new call intake back over to primary CAD.
7. Reconcile: export every call record logged on BACKUP-CAD01 between the time logging started
   (step 3) and the time it stopped (step 6), and import/merge them into primary CAD so the

   number successfully imported into primary. If they don't match, do not decommission or
   idle BACKUP-CAD01 -- keep it live and treat the discrepancy as its own incident until every
   call is accounted for.
9. Only once record counts are verified and BACKUP-CAD01's data is fully reconciled into
   primary is BACKUP-CAD01 safe to leave idle again. Log the reconciliation confirmation
   before closing the incident.

What to tell a dispatcher asking "are we done?" right after cutback: no -- cutting back to
primary only restores live call intake; the incident isn't closed until the calls logged on
BACKUP-CAD01 during the outage have been reconciled into primary and verified complete.
