Your client is a regional credit union. On July 1, IT discovered that a misconfigured export
job had exposed a database extract to an unauthorized third party. The extract contained names,

The credit union's outside counsel has summarized the applicable state breach-notification law

discovery; the state Attorney General must also be notified if the incident affects 500 or more
residents; and there is a safe harbor from notification if ALL of the personal information
involved was encrypted using a method meeting an industry-standard threshold.

General background, not case-specific: credit unions are also generally subject to federal
safeguarding-of-customer-information obligations under GLBA, separate from any state breach-
notification analysis.

Triage this incident: does it trigger notification, who must be notified, by what date (working
from the July 1 discovery date), and how does the mixed encrypted/unencrypted nature of the
extract affect whether the safe harbor applies?
