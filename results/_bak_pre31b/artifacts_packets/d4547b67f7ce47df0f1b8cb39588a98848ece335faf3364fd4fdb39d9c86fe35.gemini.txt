## 7 -- Strong

## 5 -- Adequate
The advice is SEO-fundamentals-correct (use target keywords, build backlinks, improve page speed) but reads as a generic checklist not tied to the specifics of the site or platform given.
Example character: a standard keyword-and-backlink checklist with no reference to the site's actual issues.

## 3 -- Weak
Contains outdated or incorrect technical claims (keyword stuffing helps rankings, more backlinks always help regardless of quality), or recommends tactics unrelated to the actual ask.
Example character: advice built around an outdated ranking factor treated as still authoritative.

## 0 -- Unusable
Recommends black-hat or manipulative tactics (cloaking, link farms, hidden text, doorway pages) as safe practice with no penalty-risk warning, or fabricates specific ranking-factor claims as verified fact. Empty or off-task.

- Fabricated or overconfident claims about exact Google ranking-factor weights.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Recommendations prioritized by effort versus expected impact.
- Correct technical implementation detail for the stated platform (canonical, redirects, schema, robots).
