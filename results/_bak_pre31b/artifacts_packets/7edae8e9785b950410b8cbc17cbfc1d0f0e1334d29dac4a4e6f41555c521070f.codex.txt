To maintain employer brand reputation and mitigate the risk of wrongful termination claims, the package should be comprehensive.

**A. Severance Pay**
*   **Formula:** A standard approach is 1–2 weeks of base pay for every year of completed service.
*   **Structure:** Offer the severance as a lump sum or in scheduled installments (e.g., monthly over the 45-day period) in exchange for a signed **Separation Agreement and General Release of Claims**.

**B. Benefits Continuation**
*   **Health Insurance (COBRA):** Provide clear, written instructions on how to elect COBRA. As a gesture of goodwill, many companies pay the employer-portion of the COBRA premiums for the duration of the severance period.
