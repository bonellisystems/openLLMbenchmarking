## 10 -- Frontier
Numbers reconcile and are internally consistent, and the correct concept is applied to the work shape at hand -- accurate AP/AR aging or reconciliation logic for transactional asks, or sound forecast/valuation methodology for strategic asks. Assumptions behind any calculation are stated explicitly, work is shown rather than a bare final number, and the answer is calibrated about when a licensed CPA or controller must sign off.
Example character: a reconciliation or forecast with the formula and inputs shown, an explicit assumption flagged, and a concrete next action (a specific entry, adjustment, or budget line).

## 7 -- Strong
The methodology is correct and the numbers are usable, but one assumption is left unstated, or a reconciliation step is skipped without affecting the bottom line. The work shape (transactional versus strategic) is correctly matched to the ask.
Example character: a correct forecast or reconciliation that does not show its underlying assumptions explicitly.

## 5 -- Adequate
The direction is correct but the numbers are rounded or generic without shown work, or don't fully reconcile against the figures given in the prompt.
Example character: a plausible-looking total that cannot be traced back to the specific inputs provided.

## 3 -- Weak
The answer applies the wrong treatment (conflates cash and accrual basis, misapplies an AP/AR concept), contains a math error, or ignores the specific figures given in the prompt in favor of generic placeholders.
Example character: a calculation that silently mixes cash and accrual figures without flagging the inconsistency.

## 0 -- Unusable
States fabricated financial figures as if sourced or verified, is internally inconsistent to the point of being unusable (totals do not sum, signs are wrong), or gives advice that would misstate a regulated filing with no caveat. Empty or off-task.

- Fabricated financial statistics or rates presented as real sourced data.
- Conflating cash and accrual basis without flagging it.

## Unit-specific excellence markers

| Component | Formula (simplified) | Dollar effect |
