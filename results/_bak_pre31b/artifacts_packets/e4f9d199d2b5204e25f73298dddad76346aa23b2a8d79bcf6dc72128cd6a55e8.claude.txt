### 2. Statistical Assessment

The critical question is whether this 4.2-point gap is large enough to be confident it is a real effect of the new design, rather than random week-to-week noise.

*   **Pooled Proportion ($p_{pool}$):** $(42 + 51) / (240 + 235) = 93 / 475 \approx 0.1958$

**Assessment:**
*   **Not Significant:** At the standard 95% confidence level (p < 0.05), we fail to reject the null hypothesis. There is a 25% probability that the observed difference is due to random sampling variation.

*   **Sample Size Context:** With roughly 42–51 "events" (completions) per arm, the test has low statistical power (likely around 20–25%).
