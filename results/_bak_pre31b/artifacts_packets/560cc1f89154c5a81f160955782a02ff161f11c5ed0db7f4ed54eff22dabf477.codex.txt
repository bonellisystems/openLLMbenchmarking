# Article ID: KB-TRD-042
# Title: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff
# Audience: MSP Helpdesk (L1/L2) & Broker-Dealer Client Support
# Last Reviewed: October 26, 2023

## Overview
This procedure outlines the secure replacement of a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for trading staff accessing the Order-Management System (OMS). Due to the firm's SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations, OMS access changes are treated as high-risk auditable events. 

**Critical Warning:** Improper handling of this request can result in compliance violations or order execution inconsistencies. Strict adherence to the **Two-Person Verification** and **Open Order Status** protocols is mandatory.

---

## Prerequisites
Before initiating any credential reset, the following conditions must be verified and documented:

1.  **Trader Identity Confirmation:** The requester must be the affected trader. Verify their unique employee ID and trading floor location.
2.  **Two-Person Verification (Supervisor + IT):** 
    *   The trader's desk supervisor must explicitly authorize the reissuance.
    *   You (IT Support) must act as the second verifying party.
    *   **Compliance Note:** Both parties must agree to the reissuance. This creates the required audit trail for SEC/FINRA.
3.  **OMS Order Status Check:** Query the OMS to determine if the trader has any **open orders** currently working.
4.  **Market Timing Check:** Determine if the current time is within market hours (9:30 AM ET – 4:00 PM ET).

---

## Resolution Steps

1.  **Create/Update Ticket**
    *   Log the request with the subject: `[URGENT] Lost Key - [Trader Name] - [Desk ID]`.
    *   Tag the ticket as `COMPLIANCE_CRITICAL` and `OMS_ACCESS`.

2.  **Initiate Two-Person Verification**
    *   Contact the Trader's Desk Supervisor via secure channel (phone or encrypted chat).
    *   State: *"I am verifying the replacement of the hardware security key for [Trader Name] due to loss. Do you authorize this action?"*
    *   **Action:** Record the Supervisor's verbal authorization in the ticket notes (Timestamp, Supervisor Name, Explicit "Yes").
    *   *You are the second verifying party.*

3.  **Validate Open Order Status**
    *   Log into the OMS administration console (with appropriate privileges).
    *   Query the trader's active order book.
    *   **Decision Point:**
        *   **If Open Orders = 0:** Proceed to Step 4 (Immediate Reissuance).
        *   **If Open Orders > 0:** Proceed to Step 5 (Wait for Market Close/Open).

4.  **Execute Immediate Reissuance (Zero Open Orders)**
    *   **Revoke:** Disable the lost hardware key in the OMS identity provider.
    *   **Issue:** Assign a new hardware key to the trader's profile.
    *   **Test:** Have the trader confirm successful login to the OMS.
    *   **Log:** Record the new key ID and the time of reissuance in the ticket for audit purposes.

5.  **Execute Delayed Reissuance (Open Orders Present)**
    *   **Do Not Revoke:** Do **not** disable the key while orders are open. Interrupting the session risks orders landing in an inconsistent state.
    *   **Inform Trader:** Notify the trader that the key replacement must wait until market hours have ended (after 4:00 PM ET) or market has not yet opened (before 9:30 AM ET).
    *   **Schedule:** Set a reminder in your ticket to revisit the request during the next safe window.
    *   **Re-Verify:** Once the safe window is reached, repeat **Step 2** (Two-Person Verification) before proceeding to Step 4.

---

## Failure Branches
If any prerequisite is not met, you must **STOP** the process. Do not attempt to bypass security controls.

### Branch A: Supervisor Unreachable
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be contacted, refuses to authorize, or is unavailable during the ticket creation window.
**Action:**
1.  **Do Not Proceed.** Reissuing the key without Supervisor authorization violates the two-person compliance requirement.
2.  **Escalate:** Immediately notify your IT Manager or the Client's Compliance Officer.
3.  **Ticket Note:** Document the attempt to contact the supervisor, the time of attempts, and the refusal/absence.
4.  **Hold:** Close the ticket pending escalation or higher-level approval.

### Branch B: Open Orders During Market Hours
**Condition:** The trader has active open orders AND the current time is between 9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET.
**Action:**
1.  **Do Not Proceed.** Reissuing the key during market hours will break the OMS session, risking inconsistent order states (e.g., orders remaining open but unexecutable, or duplicate entries).
2.  **Notify:** Inform the trader and the Desk Supervisor that the replacement is on hold until the market closes or opens.
3.  **Ticket Note:** Explicitly state: *"Reissuance delayed due to active open orders during market hours. Scheduled for [Time] after [Market Event]."*
4.  **Wait:** Monitor the market status. Once the market is closed (after 4:00 PM ET) or open (before 9:30 AM ET), verify **Zero Open Orders** again before proceeding to Step 4.

---

## Compliance & Audit Notes
*   **Recordkeeping:** All authorization logs (timestamps, names, and explicit consent) must be retained for the minimum retention period required by the client (typically 3 to 6 years).
*   **Key Chain of Custody:** Document the physical handover of the new security key to the trader.
*   **Incident Reporting:** If the loss of the key is suspected to be a security incident (e.g., theft), follow the client's Incident Response Plan prior to reissuance.

# Knowledge Base Article
## Title: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

### Overview
This article outlines the procedure for replacing a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for trading-floor staff at our broker-dealer client. Due to the critical nature of Order Management System (OMS) access and regulatory compliance, strict adherence to the outlined steps is mandatory.

### Prerequisites
Before initiating the replacement process, the following must be verified:

1. **Identity Verification**: Confirm that both the trader's desk supervisor **and** an IT representative are available for the two-person identity verification process, as required by SEC/FINRA regulations.
2. **Open Orders Check**: Ensure the trader has **zero open orders** in the OMS. If the trader has open orders, the reissuance must be scheduled for either after market close (4:00 PM ET) or before market open (9:30 AM ET) to prevent inconsistent order states.
3. **Documentation Ready**: Have all necessary documentation for audit purposes readily available, including the trader's identification, supervisor's verification, and IT approval records.

### Resolution Steps

1. **Initiate Verification Process**
   - The trader must contact their desk supervisor to begin the two-person identity verification process. The supervisor will verify the trader's identity and intent to replace the lost hardware security key.

2. **IT Department Notification**
   - Simultaneously, the trader or their supervisor should notify the IT department of the lost key and the intent to replace it. An IT representative must join the verification call or meeting to fulfill the two-person verification requirement.

3. **Complete Two-Person Verification**
   - Both the desk supervisor and an IT representative must confirm the trader's identity and the legitimacy of the request. This step is crucial for audit trails and compliance with regulatory obligations.

4. **Check Open Orders Status**
   - IT will verify the trader's current open orders in the OMS.
     - **If there are zero open orders**: Proceed to step 5.
     - **If there are open orders**: Proceed to the Failure Branch section below.

5. **Hardware Key Reissuance**
   - Upon successful verification and confirmation of zero open orders, the IT department will:
     - Revoke the lost hardware security key from the OMS.
     - Issue a new FIDO2 hardware security key to the trader.
     - Update the OMS records to reflect the change, ensuring all actions are logged for audit purposes.

6. **Trader Re-Authentication**
   - The trader will use the new hardware security key to authenticate and access the OMS. IT support should be available to assist with any re-authentication issues.

### Failure Branch: Handling Exceptions

#### Scenario 1: Supervisor Unreachable
- **Action**: If the desk supervisor cannot be reached, the trader must attempt to contact an alternate supervisor or the department head for identity verification. Document all attempts and reasons for unavailability. The process cannot proceed without completing the two-person verification.

#### Scenario 2: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours
- **Action**: If the trader has open orders during market hours, the reissuance of the hardware security key must be postponed. Schedule the replacement for:
  - **After Market Close**: 4:00 PM ET or later.
  - **Before Market Open**: 9:30 AM ET or earlier the next trading day.
- **Communication**: Inform the trader of the delay and the scheduled time for the replacement. Ensure they are aware of the regulatory reasons behind the timing constraint.

### Conclusion
Replacing a lost hardware security key for trading-floor staff involves a stringent process designed to maintain security, compliance, and operational integrity. Adhering to the outlined steps and handling exceptions appropriately ensures minimal disruption to trading activities while upholding regulatory standards.

Due to SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations and live market risk, this procedure is not a standard credential reset. It requires dual-person identity verification and specific market-session windows to prevent order-state inconsistency.

## Prerequisites
Before initiating the reissuance procedure, you **must** verify the following conditions. Do not proceed to step 3 if any prerequisite fails.

1.  **Confirmed Loss:** The user has physically reported the loss of their FIDO2 key and has not attempted to authenticate via a backup method (e.g., backup key, mobile push notification, or biometric fallback).
2.  **Identity Verification (IT):** The helpdesk agent must verify the requester is the affected trader via a secondary channel (e.g., phone call + PIN sent to personal email).
3.  **Identity Verification (Supervisor):** The trader's Desk Supervisor must be reachable and willing to co-sign the reissuance request. This is a hard compliance requirement (SEC/FINRA).
4.  **OMS Session Status:** Verify the trader currently has **zero** open orders working in the OMS via the [Broker-Dealer's Monitoring Dashboard / Ticketing System integration].
5.  **Market Window:** Confirm current market status against the following ET times:
    *   **Pre-Open:** Before 9:30 AM ET
    *   **Post-Close:** After 4:00 PM ET

---

## Resolution Steps

### 1. Initial Triage & Documentation
1.1. Open a high-priority ticket in `[Ticketing System]` with the subject line: *OMS HSK Reissuance – [Trader Name]*.
1.2. Document the timestamp of the loss report, the trader's desk, and the specific key identifier (serial number or device ID).
1.3. Verify with the trader that their key is definitely lost and not merely locked or misplaced.

### 2. Market Status & Order Check
2.1. Log into `[Broker-Dealer's Internal Monitoring Tool]`.
2.2. Search for `[Trader Name]` or their desk ID.
2.3. **If the trader has ZERO open orders:** Proceed to Step 3 immediately.
2.4. **If the trader has open orders (see *Failure Branch B*):** Schedule reissuance for the next Market Close (4:00 PM ET) or Market Open (9:30 AM ET). Notify the trader that authentication will be suspended during the current session.
2.5. *Note:* Interrupting an OMS session with open orders mid-session risks the order landing in an inconsistent state (partial fills or ghost orders). **Do not force a session termination during active market hours.**

### 3. Compliance Verification (Two-Person Identity Verification)
3.1. **IT Verification:** Confirm that the current ticketing agent has personally verified the trader's identity (do not accept a text message from the trader as the sole proof).
3.2. **Supervisor Verification:** Contact the trader's Desk Supervisor via phone.
    *   Confirm the supervisor knows the trader is requesting a key replacement.
    *   Confirm the supervisor is aware of the specific loss event.
    *   Request verbal (or written) authorization from the supervisor.
3.3. **Record Keeping:** Once both parties are verified, log the timestamp and contact details of both the IT agent and the Supervisor in the ticket notes. This serves as the auditable event for SEC/FINRA.

### 4. De-Provision & Reissue
4.1. Work with [Broker-Dealer's OMS Vendor / Identity Provider] to de-provision the lost key ID from the OMS trust store.
4.2. Issue a new FIDO2 key to the trader.
4.3. Register the new key ID in the OMS trust store, linking it to the trader's existing account ID.

### 5. Authentication Test
5.1. Have the trader attempt to authenticate to the OMS using the new key.
5.2. Confirm the trader can log in and access their active workspace.
5.3. If the trader cannot authenticate, halt the process immediately and contact the OMS Vendor's support line for emergency credential rollback.

### 6. Final Closure
6.1. Close the ticket in `[Ticketing System]` with status `Resolved`.
6.2. Tag the ticket with `SEC-FINRA-AUDIT-LOG`.
6.3. Send a confirmation email to the trader and their Supervisor confirming the new key is active and the previous key is invalidated.

### Failure Branch B: Open Orders During Market Hours

**Action:**
1.  **HOLD.** Do not attempt to de-provision the key or terminate the session.
2.  **Notify Traders:** Inform the trader that the key replacement will not be completed until the market closes (4:00 PM ET) or opens (9:30 AM ET).
3.  **Schedule:** Set a reminder for the next 4:00 PM ET or 9:30 AM ET window.
4.  **Preparation:** At the scheduled time, verify one final time that all orders have been closed or cancelled by the trader before proceeding with Steps 3-5.
5.  **Communication:** If the trader is unable to close orders before the window, they must remain locked out until they can manually close their positions. **Do not force a session reset.**

## 5. Resolution Steps  

> **All steps must be performed in the order presented.**  
> Document each action in the ticket and in the CCT for audit purposes.

1. **Create the Service Ticket**  
   - Open a new incident: *Category → Access Management → Hardware Security Key – Replacement*.  
   - Attach the signed **KRR‑F01** (PDF) to the ticket.  
   - Set priority to **P2 – Business Critical** (trader lock‑out).

2. **Validate Two‑Person Verification**  
   - Confirm the Supervisor’s signature and the IT Security log entry in CCT.  
   - Record the verification timestamps in the ticket notes.  
   - If verification is missing, **go to Failure Branch 6.1**.

3. **Check OMS Order Status**  
   - Run `oms‑order‑status –trader <TraderID>` or view the OMS dashboard.  
   - If “Open Orders = 0”, proceed to Step 4.  
   - If open orders exist, **go to Failure Branch 6.2**.

4. **Confirm Timing Window**  
   - Verify current ET time.  
   - If the time is **outside market hours** (≤ 09:30 AM or ≥ 04:00 PM), continue.  
   - If within market hours **and open orders exist**, schedule the replacement for the next allowable window and **notify the trader** (see Step 9).  

5. **Retrieve a Replacement Key**  
   - Go to the **Secure Key Vault** (location per client SOP).  
   - Pull the next “Available – Unassigned” FIDO2 key.  
   - Record the **Key Serial Number** and **Vault Pull Log** in the ticket.

6. **Invalidate the Lost Key**  
   - Log into the **OMS Admin Console** with your privileged credentials.  
   - Navigate to *User Management → MFA Devices*.  
   - Locate the lost key (by serial number or last‑used timestamp) and select **Revoke / Delete**.  
   - Capture a screenshot of the revocation confirmation for audit.

7. **Enroll the New Key**  
   - Hand the replacement key to the trader (or arrange secure hand‑off).  
   - On the trader’s workstation, launch the **FIDO2 Enrollment Utility** (`fido2‑enroll`).  
   - Follow the on‑screen prompts to register the new key with the OMS.  
   - Verify successful enrollment by logging into OMS with the new key (do not place any orders).  
   - Capture the enrollment success screen and attach to the ticket.

8. **Update Documentation**  
   - In **CCT**, log the credential change event:  
     - Date/Time, Trader ID, Old Key Serial (revoked), New Key Serial, Verifiers (Supervisor & IT).  
   - Mark the **KRR‑F01** as “Completed” and file it per client record‑retention policy (7 years).

9. **Notify the Trader & Supervisor**  
   - Send a confirmation email (template “Key‑Reissue Completed”) to the trader, their Desk Supervisor, and IT Security Lead.  
   - Include: new key serial, next‑step instructions (e.g., backup key enrollment, password reset if applicable), and a reminder to report any issues within 30 minutes.

10. **Close the Service Ticket**  
    - Verify all required artifacts are attached (KRR‑F01, revocation screenshot, enrollment screenshot, CCT entry).  
    - Change ticket status to **Resolved – Completed** and add the closure note.

3.  **Order Status Verification:** You must verify the trader's current position in the OMS.

    *   Check the current time against ET market hours.

        *   Proceed to Step 5.

6.  **Register New Key:**

# KB Article: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

**Article ID:** MSP-TRD-SEC-042  
**Audience:** Helpdesk Tier 1 / Tier 2  
**Client:** Broker-Dealer [Client Name]  
**Last Updated:** [Current Date]  
**Severity:** High (Trader cannot execute orders)  

---

## 📝 Description
This article outlines the procedure for reissuing a FIDO2 hardware security key to a trading-floor staff member who has lost their device. **Note:** Loss of the key results in immediate lockout from the Order Management System (OMS). 

**Critical Constraints:**
*   **Compliance:** SEC/FINRA recordkeeping requires **documented two-person identity verification** involving the Trader's Desk Supervisor AND IT before any credential change. This is an auditable event.
*   **Order Integrity:** Reissuance **cannot** occur if the trader has open orders during market hours, as session interruption may cause orders to land in an inconsistent state.

---

## ✅ Prerequisites
Before initiating the ticket or contacting the trader, ensure you have the following:

1.  **OMS Order Status Access:** Permission to query the current order book for the specific trader to check for open orders.
2.  **Desk Supervisor Contact List:** Up-to-date contact information for the trader's assigned Desk Supervisor.
3.  **Audit Log Tool:** Access to the compliance audit logging system to record the reissuance event.
4.  **Market Hours Awareness:** Knowledge of current market time.
    *   *Market Hours:* 09:30 AM – 04:00 PM ET.
    *   *Pre-Market/Post-Market:* Outside these windows.

---

## 🛠️ Resolution Steps

### Step 1: Initial Triage & Security Lock
1.  Identify the affected trader by name and desk.
2.  **Immediate Security Action:** Once you have初步 verified the caller is indeed the trader (e.g., via badge number or known desk location), **immediately disable the lost FIDO2 key** in the OMS identity provider.
    *   *Reasoning:* The lost key is a potential security risk. Disabling it prevents unauthorized use while you work through verification and timing constraints.
3.  Inform the trader that their key has been disabled and you are proceeding with reissuance, but verify if there are immediate order risks before moving on.

### Step 2: Check for Open Orders
1.  Query the OMS for the trader's open order status.
2.  **Record Result:** Note the number of open orders and the current market time in your ticket notes.
3.  **Evaluate Timing:**
    *   **Scenario A:** Zero open orders. **Proceed to Step 3.**
    *   **Scenario B:** Open orders exist AND Current Time is within Market Hours (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM ET). **Execute Failure Branch A.**
    *   **Scenario C:** Open orders exist AND Current Time is outside Market Hours. **Proceed to Step 3.**

### Step 3: Two-Person Identity Verification
*Compliance Requirement: SEC/FINRA mandates joint verification by IT and the Desk Supervisor.*

1.  **Contact the Desk Supervisor:** Call (do not email) the Trader's Desk Supervisor using the approved contact list.
    *   *Script:* "This is IT regarding [Trader Name]. We have a lost security key request. We need your verification to proceed with the reissuance. Can you confirm [Trader Name] is on the floor, has lost their key, and authorizes IT to reissue credentials?"
2.  **IT Verification:** The Helpdesk Agent confirms their own identity and role to the Supervisor.
3.  **Supervisor Verification:** The Supervisor confirms the Trader's identity and the lost-key status.
4.  **Simultaneous Agreement:** Ensure both parties agree that the request is legitimate. If there is any discrepancy, stop and escalate.

### Step 4: Document Audit Trail
**⚠️ CRITICAL:** Do not skip this step. This is a FINRA/SEC auditable event.

1.  Open the **Audit Log Entry Form**.
2.  Record the following:
    *   Ticket Number.
    *   Trader Name and ID.
    *   Event: "Hardware Key Reissuance."
    *   **IT Representative:** Your Name/ID.
    *   **Desk Supervisor:** Name/ID and confirmation timestamp.
    *   **Verification Details:** Note that both parties verified identity and lost-key status.
3.  Save the log entry and attach the confirmation to the ticket.

### Step 5: Provision and Test New Key
1.  Provision the new FIDO2 hardware key in the OMS.
2.  Hand the key to the trader.
3.  Assist the trader in logging in to ensure the new key authenticates successfully.
4.  Confirm the trader can access the OMS and that no open orders (if any existed) are in an inconsistent state.
5.  Mark the ticket as **Resolved**.

---

## 🛑 Failure Branches

### Failure Branch A: Open Orders During Market Hours
**Trigger:** The trader has open orders AND the current time is between 09:30 AM and 04:00 PM ET.

**Action:**
1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key. Interrupting a session with open orders risks order inconsistency.
2.  **Assess Trader Ability to Close:** Ask the trader if they can manually cancel or modify their open orders to clear the OMS session immediately.
    *   *If Yes:* Instruct the trader to clear all orders. Once confirmed zero open orders, proceed to **Step 3**.
    *   *If No:* The reissuance must wait.
3.  **Schedule Resolution:**
    *   Instruct the trader that the new key will be issued after **04:00 PM ET**.
    *   Log this delay in the ticket as a "Compliance/Safety Hold."
    *   Set a reminder for the Helpdesk to re-engage the Supervisor and complete Steps 3–5 immediately after market close.
    *   *Note:* If the trader requires access before close for other reasons, this is a manual override requiring Escalation to Compliance; do not bypass this check.

### Failure Branch B: Supervisor Verification Failure
**Trigger:** The Desk Supervisor is unreachable, refuses to verify, or cannot confirm the trader's identity/lost-key status.

**Action:**
1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key. Lack of two-person verification violates compliance obligations.
2.  **Attempt Secondary Contact:** If the Supervisor is unreachable, attempt to contact their direct manager or the Head of Desk. Document all contact attempts.
3.  **Escalate:** If verification cannot be obtained within a reasonable timeframe (e.g., >15 minutes), escalate the ticket to the **MSP Security Lead** or **Client Compliance Liaison**.
    *   *Reason:* The request cannot proceed without the audit trail. The Compliance Liaison may have alternative verification workflows or authority to grant an exception, though this is rare.
4.  **Status:** Update ticket to "Blocked - Awaiting Verification" and notify the trader that the process is delayed due to compliance requirements.

---

## 📎 Notes & References
*   **Key Lifecycle:** All retired FIDO2 keys must be physically shredded and logged in the asset disposition register.
*   **Market Hours:** Market hours may shift for holidays or early closures. Check the [Client Calendar Link] before assuming standard hours.
*   **Audit Review:** Random audits may pull this ticket for review. Ensure the Audit Log entry matches the ticket notes exactly.

### 📋 Description

---

### ✅ Prerequisites
Before initiating this procedure, the helpdesk technician must verify the following:

1.  **Trader Identity:** Verified via standard MSP identity proofing (e.g., call back to registered number, badge scan at secure drop-off, or biometric verification if on-prem).  
2.  **Replacement Hardware:** A provisioned, inventory-tracked FIDO2 key is physically available.  
3.  **OMS Query Access:** Technician has access to the OMS Admin Console or Order Query Tool to check real-time order status.  
4.  **Supervisor Contact:** Current direct contact information for the Trader's Desk Supervisor is available.  
5.  **Audit Log Ready:** Ticket is tagged with `SEC-FINRA-AUDIT` and `SECURITY-KEY-REISSUE`.  

---

### 🚦 Failure Branches & Escalation Paths

> **READ BEFORE STARTING STEPS.** Check for these conditions immediately. If a failure condition exists, follow the branch protocol and **do not proceed** with reissuance.

#### 🔴 Branch A: Desk Supervisor Unreachable
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be contacted or fails to provide verbal/written confirmation of the trader's identity within 5 minutes.  
**Action:**  
1.  **Halt** the reissuance process.  
2.  Escalate immediately to the **Compliance Officer** and **Head of Trading**.  
3.  Document the attempt in the ticket: *"Supervisor unreachable. Escalated per Two-Person Rule exception policy."*  
4.  Do not reissue the key until the Compliance Officer provides explicit authorization.  

#### 🔴 Branch B: Open Orders During Active Market Hours
**Condition:** The trader has **one or more open orders** in the OMS, and the current time is between **9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET**.  
**Risk:** Reissuing the key forces a session reset. Interrupting an OMS session with open orders mid-session can cause the order to enter an **inconsistent state**, potentially resulting in execution failures, regulatory breaches, or financial loss.  
**Action:**  
1.  **Halt** the reissuance process.  
2.  Inform the Trader: *"Your key cannot be replaced until the market closes at 4:00 PM ET due to active orders. Please coordinate with your supervisor to manage risk exposure."*  
3.  Schedule the reissuance for **after 4:00 PM ET**.  
4.  *Exception:* If the trader has zero open orders, proceed immediately regardless of market hours.  

---

### 🔧 Resolution Steps

#### Step 1: Execute Two-Person Compliance Verification
1.  Contact the Trader's **Desk Supervisor** via the documented channel (phone or secure messaging).  
2.  State the request clearly: *"I am processing a lost key replacement for [Trader Name]. I require your verification of their identity to satisfy SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations."*  
3.  Obtain explicit confirmation from the Supervisor.  
4.  **Document Verification:**  
    *   In the ticket, record:  
        *   `Verified By IT: [Technician Name] at [Timestamp]`  
        *   `Verified By Supervisor: [Supervisor Name] at [Timestamp]`  
        *   `Method: [Phone/Secure Chat]`  
    *   *Note: Both signatures/confirmations are required for the audit trail.*  

#### Step 2: Check OMS Order State & Market Timing
1.  Query the OMS for the trader's account using the Order Query Tool.  
2.  Check for **Open Orders**.  
3.  Evaluate against current time (ET):  
    *   **Case A:** `Open Orders == 0` → **Proceed to Step 3.**  
    *   **Case B:** `Open Orders > 0` AND Time is `09:30 - 16:00 ET` → **STOP.** Follow **Branch B** above. Wait for market close.  
    *   **Case C:** `Open Orders > 0` AND Time is `Outside 09:30 - 16:00 ET` → **Proceed to Step 3.** (Safe to reissue pre-market or post-market).  

#### Step 3: Revoke Lost Key Credential
1.  Navigate to the **Identity Management / FIDO2 Admin Portal**.  
2.  Locate the user account and select **Revoke/Credential Management**.  
3.  Select the lost hardware key ID (or revoke all keys if the specific ID is unknown).  
4.  Click **Revoke Now**.  
5.  Verify the status shows `Revoked` or `Disabled`.  
    *   *This ensures the lost key cannot be used maliciously.*  

#### Step 4: Provision New Key
1.  In the FIDO2 Admin Portal, select **Register New Key** for the user.  
2.  Assign the new FIDO2 key to the account.  
3.  Set the **User Presence Requirement** to `Required`.  
4.  Save changes.  

#### Step 5: Handover and Validation
1.  Physically hand the new key to the Trader.  
2.  Require the Trader to authenticate to the OMS test environment (if available) or production immediately.  
3.  Confirm successful login.  
4.  Ask the Trader to verify their order status matches expectations.  

#### Step 6: Final Documentation & Closure
1.  Update Ticket:  
    *   `Action Taken: Key Replaced.`  
    *   `Old Key ID: [ID] (Revoked).`  
    *   `New Key ID: [ID].`  
    *   `Compliance Verified: Yes (Two-Person Rule Met).`  
    *   `Order State Check: [Zero Orders / Post-Market Reissue].`  
2.  Attach the **Key Issuance Form** to the ticket for the audit repository.  
3.  Close ticket with status `Resolved - Compliance Verified`.

## ⚠️ Compliance & Risk Warning
**SEC/FINRA Recordkeeping Obligation:** OMS access changes via hardware key reissuance are auditable events. Failure to document the two-person verification process may result in regulatory penalties.  
**System Risk:** Reissuing a key while the trader has open orders in the OMS can result in session interruption, order state corruption, and financial loss. **NEVER** bypass the open-order check during market hours.

---

## Description
Trading-floor staff utilize physical FIDO2 hardware security keys to authenticate to the Order Management System (OMS). Loss of this key results in immediate lockout. This article outlines the procedure for replacing the key, strictly adhering to the client's compliance requirements and OMS operational constraints.

---

## Prerequisites
Before initiating the reissuance process, the helpdesk technician must verify the following conditions are met. **Do not proceed** until all prerequisites are confirmed and documented.

1.  **Requester Identity:** The trader's identity has been verified via out-of-band authentication or trusted channel.
2.  **Key Details:** The model/serial number of the lost key is identified (if known), and a replacement key is prepared.
3.  **Compliance Sign-off (Two-Person Verification):**
    *   **Desk Supervisor:** The trader's assigned desk supervisor must provide explicit authorization for the credential change.
    *   **IT Team:** The helping technician (representing IT) must document authorization to perform the change.
    *   *Note:* Both verifications must be logged in the ticket with timestamps and specific acknowledgment of the auditable event.
4.  **OMS State Validation:**
    *   **Scenario A (No Open Orders):** The trader currently has **zero** active orders in the OMS.
    *   **Scenario B (Open Orders):** The trader has open orders. Reissuance is permitted **only** if the current time is:
        *   **After Market Close:** 4:00 PM ET, **OR**
        *   **Before Market Open:** Before 9:30 AM ET.

---

## Resolution Steps

### 1. Validate Request and Gather Information
*   Confirm with the trader that the key is genuinely lost/stolen and cannot be located.
*   Verify the trader's full name and desk assignment match the OMS user profile.
*   Ask the trader if they currently have any active trades or open orders in the OMS.

### 2. Execute Compliance Verification (Two-Person Check)
*   **Contact Desk Supervisor:**
    *   Reach out to the trader's desk supervisor.
    *   Confirm the loss and request authorization for key reissuance.
    *   **Document:** Add a ticket comment containing:
        > `COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION: Supervisor [Supervisor Name/ID] has authorized key reissuance for trader [Trader Name] at [Timestamp]. Reason: Lost FIDO2 Key.`
*   **IT Verification:**
    *   **Document:** Add a ticket comment containing:
        > `IT AUTHORIZATION: Technician [Your ID] has verified prerequisites and authorizes credential reissuance per compliance protocol at [Timestamp].`

### 3. Check OMS Open Order Status
*   Access the OMS admin panel or use the `check_trader_orders` CLI tool/script.
*   Run: `get_trader_open_orders --user <TRADER_ID>`
*   **Interpret Results:**
    *   If `count == 0`: Proceed to **Step 4**.
    *   If `count > 0`: Proceed to **Failure Branch: Open Orders Detected**.

### 4. Provision and Reissue Key
*   *Only execute this step if Prerequisite 4 is met and No Open Orders exist.*
*   Log into the OMS Provisioning Console.
*   Navigate to **Identity & Access > FIDO2 Provisioning**.
*   Select the trader's account and select **Reissue Hardware Key**.
*   Select the prepared replacement key device (scan serial/UID).

*   **Confirm Transaction:** Review the audit log entry generated and ensure it captures the two-person verification notes.

### 5. Handoff and Validation
*   Securely deliver the new hardware key to the trader.
*   Instruct the trader to:
    1.  Power on the new FIDO2 key.
    2.  Log into the OMS using the new key.
    3.  Perform a test authentication.
*   **Post-Check:** Verify the trader reports successful login.
*   Close ticket only after successful authentication is confirmed.

### Branch A: Desk Supervisor is Unreachable or Refuses Authorization
*   **Symptom:** Supervisor cannot be contacted within the SLA window, or authorization is denied.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **Do NOT** proceed with reissuance. Compliance mandates two-person verification; IT-only approval is invalid for OMS access changes.
    2.  Escalate to the Trader's Team Manager or Compliance Officer via the designated **MSP Broker-Dealer Escalation Line**.
    3.  Update ticket status to `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_APPROVAL` and notify the trader of the delay.
    4.  *Note:* In cases of theft suspected, report to the client's Security/Fraud team immediately.

### Branch B: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours
*   **Symptom:** `get_trader_open_orders` returns `count > 0` AND current time is between 9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET.
*   **Risk:** Reissuance will force an OMS session logout. Orders in progress may land in an inconsistent state, resulting in failed executions or unauthorized position changes.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **HALT** the reissuance process.
    2.  Inform the trader: *"Reissuance cannot be performed while you have open orders during market hours. This prevents order state corruption. We will schedule this immediately after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET."*
    3.  **Schedule Ticket:** Set a follow-up reminder for 4:00 PM ET or 9:30 AM ET depending on which is sooner.
    4.  *Workaround Check:* Ask the trader if they can transfer open orders to a backup user (if allowed by client policy) to clear their session. **Do not attempt this without explicit Client Manager approval.**
    5.  Once the time window is safe, return to **Step 3** to re-verify order count.

### Branch C: Identity Discrepancy
*   **Symptom:** Requester cannot pass identity verification, or details do not match the OMS profile.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Deny request.
    2.  Escalate to IT Security Team to investigate potential social engineering or account takeover attempt.
    3.  Alert the client's Security desk.

## KB Article: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

| Field | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Article ID** | MSP-OMS-KEY-001 |
| **Title** | Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff |
| **Client** | [Broker-Dealer Client Name] |
| **Severity** | P1 (Trader Blocked) |
| **Category** | Identity & Access Management / OMS |
| **Affected System** | FIDO2 Identity Provider, Order Management System (OMS) |

### Summary
This article outlines the procedure for reissuing a FIDO2 hardware security key to trading-floor personnel when the physical token is lost, stolen, or compromised. Loss of the key prevents the trader from authenticating to the OMS, resulting in a total halt of trading activity.

**⚠️ CRITICAL WARNINGS:**
1.  **COMPLIANCE RISK:** OMS access changes are **auditable events** under SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations. No key may be reissued without documented **two-person verification**. Proceeding without verification constitutes a policy violation.
2.  **ORDER STATE RISK:** Interrupting an OMS session with open orders mid-session can place orders in an inconsistent state. Reissuance involving session termination is prohibited during market hours if open orders exist.

---

### Prerequisites

Verify **all** items below before initiating the reissuance workflow. If any prerequisite fails, follow the **Failure Branches** defined at the end of this article.

- [ ] **Trader Identity Confirmed:** Trader presents valid firm ID badge and government-issued photo ID.
- [ ] **Desk Supervisor Available:** The trader's designated desk supervisor is present (in-person or via verified secure call) and ready to provide explicit authorization.
- [ ] **IT Technician Present:** A qualified MSP technician is present to perform the technical reissuance and co-sign the audit log.
- [ ] **Replacement Asset Available:** A spare FIDO2 key (correct model) has been pulled from stock and its asset tag verified against the replacement inventory sheet.
- [ ] **Operational Status Check:**
    - [ ] OMS query shows **Zero Open Orders** for this trader; **OR**
    - [ ] Current time is **before 09:30 AM ET** or **after 04:00 PM ET**.
    - *Note: If orders exist and time is within market hours, reissuance must be deferred. Do not attempt to force-logout.*

---

### Resolution Steps

#### 1. Validate Trader Identity
Confirm the trader's identity using the firm's standard protocol. Record the trader's name, desk, and employee ID in the ticket.

#### 2. Assess Operational Impact
Query the OMS backend to verify the trader's current session state:
- **Action:** Run `OMS-STATUS --user=<ID>`.
- **If Open Orders exist AND Market is Open (09:30–16:00 ET):** Stop. Inform the trader that reissuance will be scheduled immediately post-close (target: 16:05 ET). Proceed only with **Step 3** to log the request for deferred processing.
- **If Zero Open Orders OR Market is Closed:** Proceed to Step 3.

#### 3. Execute Two-Person Verification
**This step requires simultaneous involvement of IT and the Desk Supervisor.**

- **IT Action:** Request the Desk Supervisor to verbally confirm the following:
  > *"I verify that [Trader Name] has lost their hardware key, that this is not a coercion scenario, and I authorize the immediate reissuance of a replacement key."*
- **Supervisor Action:** Supervisor provides verbal confirmation and agrees to sign the audit log.
- **IT Action:** Technician confirms authorization received.

#### 4. Document Auditable Event
**Strict FINRA/SEC Requirement.** Populate the Credential Change Log:
- **Fields:** Trader ID, Date/Time, Reason (Lost Key), IT Tech Name, Supervisor Name, Ticket ID.
- **Attestation:** Both the IT Technician and the Desk Supervisor must countersign the log entry (physical signature or digital co-sign based on firm tooling) **before** the key is revoked.
- *If the supervisor cannot sign immediately, do not proceed. Escalate to Compliance.*

#### 5. Revoke Compromised Key
In the FIDO2 Identity Provider console:
- Locate the lost key's credential ID.
- Select **Revoke / Disable**.
- Confirm the trader's session is terminated (verify via OMS that the trader is logged out).
- *Note: Since Step 2 confirmed zero open orders or off-hours, session termination risk is mitigated.*

#### 6. Provision Replacement Key
- **Hardware:** Initialize the replacement FIDO2 key. Affix the new asset tag sticker.
- **Enrollment:** Assign the new key to the trader's account in the FIDO2 IdP.
- **Policy:** Ensure the new key inherits the same privilege scopes as the previous configuration.

#### 7. Handover and Validation
- Hand the new key to the trader.
- **Validation:** Instruct the trader to authenticate to the OMS using the new key.
- **Confirm:** Verify the trader successfully logs in and can execute a test action (e.g., view position screen) without errors.
- **Update Asset Record:** Log the new key's serial number to the trader's asset file. Update the ticket with "Resolved."

---

### Failure Branches

Use these branches if a prerequisite cannot be satisfied.

#### ❌ FAILURE: Supervisor Unreachable
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be contacted via approved channels, refuses to verify, or is unavailable to sign the audit log.
**Action:**
1.  **STOP.** Do not reissue the key under any circumstances. Bypassing two-person verification violates compliance policy.
2.  Inform the trader that the request is paused pending supervisor verification.
3.  **Escalate** to the MSP Security Lead and flag for the firm's **Compliance Officer**.
4.  The Compliance Officer may authorize alternative verification methods if permitted by the firm's written policies, but IT may never self-verify.

#### ❌ FAILURE: Open Orders During Market Hours
**Condition:** Trader has open orders, and the current time is between 09:30 AM ET and 04:00 PM ET.
**Action:**
1.  **DO NOT REVOKE** the lost key. Revoking the key will terminate the OMS session. If the trader has active orders, this interruption risks the order landing in an inconsistent state (e.g., partially filled, lost status, or duplicate submission).
2.  Advise the trader: *"We cannot reissue the key until market close. Please contact your desk supervisor to arrange coverage or manual trading protocols if required by firm policy."*
3.  Log the request as **Deferred**. Schedule the reissuance for 16:05 ET (5 minutes post-close to allow order settlement buffers).
4.  Set a reminder to execute Steps 3–7 immediately after 16:00 ET.
5.  *Do not force the trader to close orders manually to bypass this check unless explicitly instructed by the Desk Supervisor and confirmed safe by the Trader.*

#### ❌ FAILURE: Supervisor Refuses Authorization
**Condition:** Supervisor is reachable but denies the reissuance request.
**Action:**
1.  **STOP.**
2.  Document the refusal in the ticket, noting the Supervisor's name and reason for refusal (if provided).
3.  **Escalate** to the firm's Compliance Department and the MSP Account Manager.
4.  This may indicate a dispute, potential coercion, or policy disagreement that requires higher-level adjudication.

---

### Post-Resolution Checklist
- [ ] Ticket closed with "Resolved" status.
- [ ] Audit log signed by IT and Supervisor; scanned/archived per retention policy.
- [ ] Old key status set to "Revoked" in FIDO2 IdP.
- [ ] New key asset tag recorded in trader's profile.
- [ ] Trader confirmed login success.
- [ ] If deferred case: Ticket reopened and actioned post-close.
