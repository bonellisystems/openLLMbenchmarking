# KB-2024-001: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

**Last Updated:** March 15, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal Helpdesk Procedure  
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2024

---

## Overview

This article provides step-by-step instructions for replacing lost FIDO2 hardware security keys used by trading-floor staff to authenticate to the order-management system (OMS). Loss of a hardware security key results in complete lockout from the OMS, requiring immediate credential reissuance following strict compliance protocols.

## Prerequisites

Before initiating the hardware security key replacement process, verify the following conditions:

1. **Traders must be unable to access the OMS** due to loss of their hardware security key
2. **Current market hours** - verify whether it is before 9:30 AM ET, after 4:00 PM ET, or during trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)
3. **Supervisor availability** - confirm that the trader's desk supervisor is reachable and available for verification
4. **IT staff availability** - confirm that an IT representative is available to process the reissuance
5. **No open orders in OMS** - verify that the trader has zero open orders in the system, or that the current time falls within acceptable reissuance windows

## Resolution Steps

### Step 1: Initial Verification
Contact the affected trader to confirm:
- The hardware security key is indeed lost (not misplaced)
- They cannot access the OMS system
- They have no open orders in the OMS

### Step 2: Market Hours Verification
Check the current time (ET):
- If **before 9:30 AM ET** or **after 4:00 PM ET** - proceed with immediate reissuance
- If **during trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)** - proceed with caution and verify open orders

### Step 3: Supervisor Contact and Verification
Contact the trader's desk supervisor to verify:
- The supervisor is available and can confirm the trader's identity
- The supervisor has witnessed the loss of the security key
- The supervisor agrees to the reissuance process

### Step 4: IT Representative Coordination
Coordinate with an available IT representative to:
- Verify that both the supervisor and IT have completed their identity verification steps
- Document the credential replacement in the compliance system
- Process the hardware security key reissuance

### Step 5: Reissuance and Documentation
Complete the hardware security key reissuance by:
- Creating a new hardware security key for the trader
- Recording the reissuance in the firm's audit trail system
- Notifying the trader that their new key is ready for use

## Failure Branches

### Branch A: Supervisor Unavailable or Unable to Verify
**Condition:** Supervisor cannot be reached or refuses to participate in verification process

**Action Required:**
1. Document the supervisor unavailability in the helpdesk ticket
2. Attempt to contact the supervisor's backup contact or manager
3. If supervisor remains unreachable after 30 minutes of attempts:
   - Escalate to the Compliance Department for exception processing
   - Document the incident in the Compliance Tracking System
   - Notify the trader that the process is on hold pending supervisor availability

### Branch B: Trader Has Open Orders During Trading Hours
**Condition:** Trader has active orders in the OMS during trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)

**Action Required:**
1. Inform the trader that reissuance cannot be completed during active trading hours due to order integrity risks
2. Schedule the reissuance for the next available window:
   - **Before market open** (9:30 AM ET) on the following business day
   - **After market close** (4:00 PM ET) on the same business day
3. Document this delay in the helpdesk ticket and Compliance Tracking System
4. Notify the trader of the scheduled reissuance window and provide temporary written instructions for trading during the delay

### Branch C: Multiple Traders Affected
**Condition:** Multiple traders in the same desk area have lost keys simultaneously

**Action Required:**
1. Process each trader individually following standard procedure
2. Document all reissuances in the Compliance Tracking System
3. Consider implementing a bulk reissuance process during non-trading hours
4. Notify the desk supervisor of the increased credential activity for compliance purposes

- Two-person verification is mandatory for all reissuances and cannot be bypassed under any circumstances
- The firm's SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations require detailed documentation of all OMS access changes
- Any exception to standard procedures must be documented and approved by the Compliance Department

# Knowledge Base Article  
**Title:** Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading‑Floor Staff  

**Author:** MSP Help Desk – Trading‑Floor Support Team  
**Last Updated:** 18‑Jul‑2026  
**Affected System:** Order‑Management System (OMS) – FIDO2 Hardware Security Key authentication  

---

## 1. Purpose

This article provides the step‑by‑step procedure for helpdesk staff to re‑issue a lost FIDO2 hardware security key to a trader while ensuring compliance with SEC/FINRA record‑keeping requirements and minimizing the risk of order‑state corruption.

---

## 2. Scope

- **Audience:** MSP helpdesk agents, IT security staff, and desk supervisors.  
- **Use‑case:** A trader on the trading floor reports a lost hardware key.  
- **Exclusions:** Re‑issuance of keys for non‑trading staff, key recovery via software backup, or key replacement for non‑OMS systems.

---

## 3. Prerequisites  

Before initiating the re‑issuance process, verify the following **must‑passes**:

| # | Requirement | Verification Method | Notes |
|---|-------------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Trader’s identity** (must be the same person who lost the key). | • Confirm trader’s full name, employee ID, and desk number.<br>• Verify in the HR/Personnel system. | Use the latest employee directory. |
| 2 | **Supervisor’s availability and willingness** to sign off. | • Contact desk supervisor via phone or instant‑messaging.<br>• Obtain verbal confirmation and written signature on the *Hardware Key Re‑issue Form* (see Appendix A). | If supervisor cannot be reached, proceed to Failure Branch #1. |
| 3 | **IT Security sign‑off**. | • IT Security lead must review the request and provide a signed checkbox on the form. | Use the IT Security sign‑off template. |
| 4 | **OMS session status** – no open orders at time of re‑issuance. | • Check the trader’s current OMS session in the *OMS Dashboard*.<br>• Verify that there are zero “Open” or “Pending” orders. | If open orders exist during market hours, proceed to Failure Branch #2. |
| 5 | **Compliance audit log**. | • Verify that the request will be logged in the *Compliance Audit System* (CAS). | The audit log must capture the supervisor, IT sign‑off, and timestamps. |

> **Important:** All prerequisites must be satisfied **before** any key is issued. Failure to meet any prerequisite is an audit‑critical error.

---

## 4. Failure Branches  

### 4.1 Supervisor Unreachable / Unavailable  

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Attempt to contact the trader’s desk supervisor via phone, email, and instant‑messaging (maximum of **3 attempts**). |
| 2 | If the supervisor is still unreachable after 3 attempts, notify the *Compliance Officer* and request a **temporary override**. |
| 3 | The Compliance Officer must issue a written approval (via email) and provide a signed “Supervisor Override” note. |
| 4 | Record the override note in the *Compliance Audit System* and proceed with re‑issuance. |
| 5 | **Do NOT** issue the key until the override is documented. |

### 4.2 Open Orders During Market Hours  

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | If the trader’s OMS session shows any open or pending orders **between 9:30 AM and 4:00 PM ET**, do **not** proceed with re‑issuance. |
| 2 | Inform the trader to **hold** the re‑issuance until after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET the next trading day. |
| 3 | Schedule a **post‑market re‑issuance window** (e.g., 4:30 PM – 5:00 PM ET) and document the scheduled time in the *Compliance Audit System*. |
| 4 | If the trader has a **critical open order** that cannot be delayed, contact the *Trade Management* team immediately to move or cancel the order *before* proceeding. |
| 5 | Once the window has passed and the OMS session shows **zero open orders**, resume steps 1–3 of the standard re‑issuance procedure. |

> **Tip:** Use the *OMS Order State Viewer* to confirm that all orders are marked “Completed” or “Cancelled” before proceeding.

---

## 5. Resolution Steps (Standard Path)

1. **Log In to the MSP Helpdesk System**  
   - Open the *Hardware Key Re‑issue Module* and create a new ticket.  
   - Enter the trader’s name, employee ID, desk number, and the date/time the key was reported lost.

2. **Verify Trader Identity**  
   - Cross‑check the trader’s details against the HR system.  
   - If discrepancies arise, halt the process and notify the Compliance Officer.

3. **Obtain Supervisor Sign‑off**  
   - Call the desk supervisor.  
   - Once reached, ask them to sign the *Hardware Key Re‑issue Form* (digital signature or handwritten if necessary).  
   - Store the signed form in the *Compliance Audit System*.

4. **Obtain IT Security Sign‑off**  
   - IT Security lead reviews the request.  
   - Sign the same form (digital).  
   - Attach the signature to the ticket.

5. **Check OMS Session for Open Orders**  
   - Open the *OMS Dashboard* for the trader.  
   - Verify that the “Open Orders” count is **zero**.  
   - If open orders exist during market hours, follow Failure Branch #2.  

6. **Create a New FIDO2 Hardware Key**  
   - Log into the MSP’s *Key Management Portal*.  
   - Generate a new key for the trader’s account.  
   - Record the key’s serial number, issuance date, and assigned trader in the portal.

7. **Deliver the Key**  
   - Coordinate with the **Physical Delivery Team** to hand the key to the trader in person.  
   - Have the trader confirm receipt and test login to the OMS.  
   - Capture a signed “Key Receipt Confirmation” on the form.

8. **Update the OMS User Profile**  
   - In the OMS administration console, replace the old key with the new key’s serial number.  
   - Disable the old key to prevent reuse.

9. **Audit Logging**  
   - Log every step in the *Compliance Audit System*:  
     - Supervisor sign‑off timestamp.  
     - IT Security sign‑off timestamp.  
     - OMS session status.  
     - Key issuance timestamp.  
     - Delivery confirmation timestamp.

10. **Close the Ticket**  
    - Mark the helpdesk ticket as “Resolved – Key Issued”.  
    - Attach all relevant documents (forms, audit logs, delivery confirmation).  
    - Notify the trader that the re‑issuance is complete and remind them of the new key’s use policy.

### **Description**
Trading-floor staff use physical FIDO2 hardware security keys to authenticate to the OMS. Loss or damage of the key results in immediate loss of access. This article outlines the strict procedure for reissuing keys, governed by SEC/FINRA audit requirements and OMS session stability constraints.

**⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING:** Reissuing a key while a session has open orders active risks corrupting the order state in the OMS, potentially leading to financial loss or regulatory breaches. **Never reissue a key if open orders exist during market hours.**

---

### **Prerequisites**
Before attempting any resolution, verify the following conditions. If any are not met, refer to the **Failure Branches** below.

1.  **Valid Request:** The ticket must originate from a verified trader requesting a replacement due to loss/damage.
2.  **Two-Person Identity Verification:**
    *   The **Desk Supervisor** for the trader must be available and verified.
    *   The **IT Representative** handling the ticket must be present/verified.
    *   *Note:* Both parties must acknowledge the reissuance as an auditable event.
3.  **OMS Session Status Check:**
    *   Query the OMS dashboard to confirm the trader currently has **zero open orders**.
4.  **Market Hours Check:**
    *   Determine current ET time relative to trading windows.
    *   Valid windows: Before **9:30 AM ET** (pre-open) OR After **4:00 PM ET** (post-close).

---

### **Resolution Steps**

#### **Phase 1: Verification & Compliance**

1.  **Verify Trader Identity:** Confirm the requester is the account holder of the lost key using standard authentication methods.
2.  **Execute Two-Person Verification:**
    *   Contact the Trader's Desk Supervisor. Confirm they are aware of the key loss and authorize the reissue.
    *   **Action:** Log the Supervisor's approval in the ticket notes and attach a screenshot or transcript if required by client policy.
    *   **Action:** Verify IT representative ID. Ensure both the Supervisor and IT are present/acknowledging the audit event.
    *   *Audit Note:* Record this interaction as the justification for the OMS access change to satisfy SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations.
3.  **Check Open Orders:**
    *   Log in to the OMS monitoring tool and query `[TRADER_ID]`.
    *   Confirm the `Open Order Count` is **0**.
4.  **Check Market Status:**
    *   Verify current time. Ensure the system is not in active trading hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET) unless open orders are zero.

#### **Phase 2: Reissuance**

5.  **Provision New Key:**
    *   If prerequisites are met, proceed to the Key Management Portal.
    *   Revoke the lost key certificate.
    *   Provision a new FIDO2 key binding to the trader's account.
6.  **Validate Access:**
    *   Ask the trader to authenticate with the new key immediately.
    *   Verify successful login and OMS access.
7.  **Final Audit Documentation:**
    *   Mark the ticket as resolved.
    *   Add a final summary note stating: *"Key reissued on [Date] at [Time]. Two-party verification completed by [Supervisor Name] and [IT Name]. No open orders present. OMS access change logged for audit compliance."*

---

### **Failure Branches**

#### **Branch A: Supervisor Unreachable or Uncooperative**
*   **Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be verified, is unreachable during the request window, or refuses to authorize the reissue.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **STOP:** Do not proceed. The two-person verification requirement is a hard constraint for SEC/FINRA compliance.
    2.  **Escalate:** Escalate the ticket to the **Trading Manager** or **Compliance Officer**.
    3.  **Communicate:** Inform the trader that the key replacement is blocked until the Supervisor authorizes the change.
    4.  **Workaround:** If the trader is locked out and the Supervisor is unavailable, the trader may be required to cancel any pending trades via alternative channels (e.g., voice confirm to broker) and wait for the Supervisor to return for key reissue. *Consult Client Policy on "Emergency Access Procedures."*

#### **Branch B: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours**
*   **Condition:** The trader has **≥1 open order** AND the current time is between **9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET**.
*   **Risk:** Interrupting the OMS session to reissue the key will cause the session to drop while orders are active, risking an **inconsistent order state** (e.g., partial fills, duplicate submissions, or orphaned orders).
*   **Action:**
    1.  **STOP:** Do not reissue the key.
    2.  **Instruct Trader:** Inform the trader that the key cannot be replaced until market close or pre-open.
    3.  **Options:**
        *   **Option 1:** Ask the trader to cancel all open orders manually via voice or secondary channel if possible. *If orders are successfully canceled and confirmed to OMS, proceed with reissue.*
        *   **Option 2:** Schedule the reissuance for **after 4:00 PM ET** or **before 9:30 AM ET**.
    4.  **Ticket Update:** Update the ticket status to `Pending: Market Hours Conflict` with a note: *"Reissue delayed due to active open orders. Rescheduled for [Date/Time]."*

---

### **Notes & References**
*   **Audit Trail:** All OMS access changes must remain auditable. Failure to document the two-person verification may result in a regulatory citation.
*   **FIDO2 Binding:** Ensure the new key is bound to the specific user account and not a shared profile.
*   **Client Policy:** Refer to *Client Policy Doc #SEC-OMS-2023* for details on emergency access exceptions.
*   **Timezone:** All times are **Eastern Time (ET)**. Ensure your system accounts for daylight saving transitions.

## ⚠️ Critical Warnings

3.  **TIME ZONES:** All trading windows are defined in **Eastern Time (ET)**.

## Prerequisites

Before initiating this procedure, verify **all** of the following:

*   **P1. Inventory:** You have a verified, spare FIDO2 hardware key from the client's secure key inventory.
*   **P2. Identity:** You are speaking directly with the affected trader (audio/video), and you can confirm their desk assignment and employee ID.
*   **P3. Supervisor Contact:** You have a working phone number for the trader's **Desk Supervisor**. (Chat/email is insufficient for this step; voice verification is required).
*   **P4. Timing Assessment:** You have checked the trader's current OMS session status (see *Resolution Step 1*).

## Resolution Steps

### Step 1: Assess Open Orders & Determine Window
**Action:** Query the OMS backend for the trader's active order count.

*   **Result A: Zero Open Orders.**
    *   Proceed to **Step 2**.
*   **Result B: One or more Open Orders.**
    *   Check current time against trading windows:
        *   *Market Open Window:* 09:30 AM – 04:00 PM ET.
        *   *Safe Windows:* Before 09:30 AM ET or After 04:00 PM ET.
    *   **If within Market Hours:** **STOP.** This triggers a **Failure Branch** (see *Branch B*). You must wait for the next safe window.
    *   **If outside Market Hours:** Proceed to **Step 2**.

### Step 2: Initiate Two-Person Verification
**Action:** The trader must remain on the line while you contact the Desk Supervisor.

1.  Call the **Desk Supervisor** directly.
2.  State your name, MSP role, and the request: *"I am processing a lost-key replacement for Trader [Name] on [Desk]. Do you authorize the reissuance of their OMS hardware credential?"*
3.  **Supervisor must explicitly confirm:**
    *   They have verified the trader's identity.
    *   They acknowledge the key is lost/stolen.
    *   They authorize the reissuance.
4.  While Supervisor confirms, request the **Trader** verbally confirm:
    *   *"Trader, please confirm you have lost your key and understand this will reissue your access."*

### Step 3: Execute Reissuance
**Action:** Perform the technical reissuance only after both parties have verbally confirmed.

1.  Deprovision the lost key ID in the FIDO2 policy (record this action in the audit log).
2.  Provision the replacement key against the trader's OMS account.
3.  Instruct the trader to register the new key via the OMS client portal.
4.  Confirm with the trader that they can successfully authenticate and the OMS loads.

### Step 4: Secure the Lost Key
**Action:** Instruct the trader to take the following physical security action:

1.  If the key was a physical token, the trader must report the loss to their Desk Supervisor immediately for internal incident tracking.
2.  Advise the trader to check if the key was returned to a desk drawer or left in a vehicle; if found, it must be surrendered to IT immediately to prevent unauthorized use.

### Step 5: Document the Audit Trail
**Action:** Complete the **Credential Change Audit Log** before closing the ticket. This log satisfies SEC/FINRA requirements.

## Failure Branches

### Branch A: Desk Supervisor is Unreachable
**Trigger:** The Desk Supervisor does not answer, is unavailable for >15 minutes, or cannot verify the trader's identity.

1.  **DO NOT** proceed with reissuance.
2.  Inform the trader: *"I cannot complete the request until your Desk Supervisor can verify the loss. We will retry in 15 minutes."*
3.  If the Supervisor remains unreachable after two attempts:
    *   **Escalate** to the MSP On-Call Manager.
    *   The On-Call Manager may authorize reissuance based on secondary compliance channels, per the client's *Emergency Credential Recovery Policy*.
    *   Document the escalation and the Manager's authorization in the Audit Log.

### Branch B: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours
**Trigger:** Step 1 reveals open orders and it is currently between 09:30 AM and 04:00 PM ET.

1.  **DO NOT** attempt to reissue the key. Interrupting the session risks order inconsistency and regulatory breach.
2.  Inform the Trader and Supervisor:
    > *"We cannot reissue your key right now because you have open orders in the OMS. Reissuance must wait until after market close at 04:00 PM ET or before market open at 09:30 AM ET to protect order integrity."*
3.  **Action Required:**
    *   Set a **reminder** to reissue the key at **04:01 PM ET** (or **09:29 AM ET** the following day if after hours).
    *   If the Supervisor indicates the open orders are at risk and need immediate closure, advise the Supervisor to attempt order management via **secondary authorized channels** (if the client has a sanctioned backup workflow). **Never** use the lost key path.
4.  Log the ticket status as **"Pending Timing Window"** with the scheduled execution time.

### Branch C: Trader Cannot Be Reached
**Trigger:** The person claiming to be the trader cannot answer knowledge-based verification or refuses to stay on the line during Supervisor verification.

1.  **DO NOT** proceed.
2.  Log the ticket as **"Identity Verification Failed."**
3.  Notify the Desk Supervisor that the requestor may not be the affected trader.
4.  The Desk Supervisor must verify the loss through internal personnel channels and re-initiate the request with the trader present.

Before attempting to reissue a security key, you **must** verify the following four conditions. Do not proceed if any condition is unmet.

    *   In the ticket notes, paste the timestamps and names of both the **Desk Supervisor** and the **IT/Compliance Manager** who approved the request.
    *   Attach screenshots/logs of the old key being revoked and the new key being registered.

    2.  Update the ticket status to **"Pending Approval."**

# KB Article: Replacing a Lost FIDO2 Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

**Article ID:** KB-0042  
**Classification:** Internal — Helpdesk & Floor Operations  
**Last Updated:** 2023-10-15  
**Priority:** Critical (Financial Impact)  

---

## Summary
Trading-floor personnel authenticate to the Order Management System (OMS) exclusively via a physical FIDO2 Hardware Security Key (HSM). The loss of this key immediately locks the trader out of the OMS, preventing order entry, modification, or cancellation. This article details the procedure for reissuing a lost credential while satisfying SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations and maintaining the integrity of the order book.

**Warning:** Do **not** bypass the two-person verification protocol. Any credential reissuance performed without documented sign-off from both the Desk Supervisor and IT Security constitutes a compliance violation.

---

## Prerequisites

Before initiating the reissuance workflow, the Helpdesk Agent must verify the following conditions:

1.  **Confirmation of Loss:** Verify with the trader that the hardware key is confirmed lost or destroyed (do not treat as a "misplaced" key until a physical search is completed).
2.  **OMS Session Status:** Check the OMS backend console to determine if the affected trader has any **open orders** currently active in the session.
3.  **Market State:** Confirm the current time against the firm's trading hours (9:30 AM ET – 4:00 PM ET).
4.  **Supervisor Identification:** Identify the trader's current Desk Supervisor of Record.
5.  **Key Inventory:** Confirm that a replacement FIDO2 key is available in the secure key vault (do not issue a key that has not been security-verified).

---

## Resolution Steps

### Phase 1: Triage and Market State Check

**Step 1:** Log a High-Priority ticket and notify the Floor Operations Lead.
**Step 2:** Check the OMS console for open orders associated with the affected trader's account.
**Step 3:** Determine the appropriate execution path:
*   **Path A (No Open Orders):** If the trader has zero open orders, proceed immediately to Phase 2 (Reissuance).
*   **Path B (Open Orders):** If the trader has one or more open orders, **stop.** Proceed to the Failure Branch: *Open Orders Mid-Session*.

### Phase 2: Two-Person Identity Verification (Compliance Requirement)

*Applicable to Path A or after a delayed reissuance request has been approved via Path B.*

**Step 4:** Contact the trader's **Desk Supervisor**.
*   Verbally confirm the loss event with the Supervisor.
*   Require the Supervisor to verbally confirm they approve the credential reset.
*   **Document the call** (date, time, supervisor name, line number) in the ticket notes.

**Step 5:** Contact the **IT Security Officer** (or the Helpdesk Lead acting as the second verifier).
*   Confirm that IT is aware of the reissuance request.
*   Require the IT verifier to confirm they approve the generation of a new FIDO2 key for this user.
*   **Document this second call** in the ticket notes.

**Step 6:** If either verification fails (see Failure Branch: *Supervisor Unreachable*), **do not proceed.**

### Phase 3: Key Reissuance

**Step 7:** Upon successful two-person verification, generate the new FIDO2 Hardware Security Key from the secure vault.
**Step 8:** Provision the new key to the trader via secure courier or secure drop-box.
**Step 9:** Immediately **revoke/void** the lost key in the OMS credential store.
*   *Note:* Revoke the key immediately upon verification. Do not wait for the trader to confirm receipt of the new key.
**Step 10:** Instruct the trader to authenticate with the new key. Verify successful login to the OMS.

### Phase 4: Audit Closure

**Step 11:** Update the ticket with the following mandatory audit data:
*   Date/Time of reissuance.
*   Date/Time of Supervisor verification.
*   Date/Time of IT verification.
*   Status of the revoked key (Voided).
*   Status of the new key (Active).
**Step 12:** Close the ticket. Ensure the ticket is tagged with `FINRA_AUDIT` and `SEC_RECORDKEEPING` for the monthly compliance export.

---

## Explicit Failure Branches

### Failure Branch 1: Supervisor is Unreachable or Refuses to Verify
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be reached by phone or is unable to confirm the loss event within 15 minutes of the initial ticket creation.

**Action Required:**
1.  **Do not** initiate a reissuance.
2.  **Escalate** to the Floor Operations Manager and the Compliance Officer immediately.
3.  The Compliance Officer will determine if an emergency override is authorized (e.g., if the trader is physically incapacitated).
4.  If a Compliance Officer override is granted, a **third-party witness** (e.g., Security Guard or Regional Manager) must be present for the credential handoff to replace the Supervisor signature.
5.  Document the override authorization in the ticket notes. Without this, the reissuance is non-compliant.

### Failure Branch 2: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours
**Condition:** The trader has open orders in the OMS, and the current time is between 9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET.

**Action Required:**
1.  **Hold the ticket.** Do not provision a new key.
2.  **Do not** instruct the trader to close or cancel their open orders, as this constitutes a trading decision, not an IT support action.
3.  **Notify the trader:** Inform them that the new key will be ready for pickup after the next market boundary.
    *   **If currently 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET:** The reissuance must be performed **after 4:00 PM ET** (Market Close) or **before 9:30 AM ET** (Market Open).
    *   **If currently 4:00 PM – 9:30 AM ET:** The reissuance can be performed **before 9:30 AM ET** (Market Open).
4.  **Update the ticket:** Set the ticket status to `PENDING_MARKET_BOUNDARY`. Set a reminder for the Helpdesk team to check the ticket status at the next market boundary.
5.  **Risk Rationale:** Interrupting an OMS session with open orders creates a risk of the order landing in an inconsistent state (e.g., partially filled, orphaned, or duplicate). This could result in financial loss or regulatory scrutiny regarding order integrity.

*   **Escalation Path:** If the Helpdesk Lead is also unreachable, the next escalation is the **Compliance Officer** followed by the **VP of Operations**.

## Overview
Traders authenticate to the Order Management System (OMS) using a physical FIDO2 hardware security key. If this key is lost, the trader is locked out of the OMS entirely. Reissuing a key requires strict adherence to **SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations**.

**Critical Constraints:**
1.  **Compliance:** Two-person identity verification is mandatory (Desk Supervisor + IT Support) before reissuance.
2.  **Operational Safety:** Key reissuance is blocked if the trader has open orders. It must occur only when the OMS is idle (Market Close: 4:00 PM ET or Market Open: 9:30 AM ET) to prevent inconsistent state errors.

---

## Prerequisites
Before attempting to reset credentials, the following must be verified:

1.  **Trader Identity:** Confirm the trader's full name, employee ID, and desk location.
2.  **Open Order Status:** Query the OMS for the specific trader ID to confirm **zero** active/open orders.
3.  **Market Hours Check:** Verify current time relative to market hours (9:30 AM ET – 4:00 PM ET).
4.  **Supervisor Availability:** Ensure the trader's Desk Supervisor is reachable for dual-verification.
5.  **Audit Tool Access:** Ensure you have access to the Compliance Ticketing System to log the event.

---

## Resolution Steps

### Step 1: Verify Open Order Status
Log into the OMS administrative console. Search for the trader's ID.
*   **Action:** Check the "Open Orders" tab.
*   **Decision:**
    *   If **Open Orders Exist:** Proceed to **Failure Branch A**.
    *   If **No Open Orders:** Proceed to Step 2.

### Step 2: Confirm Market Hours Window
Check the current time against the Market Schedule.
*   **Allowed Times:** Before 9:30 AM ET OR After 4:00 PM ET.
*   **Action:**
    *   If **Inside Market Hours (9:30-4:00 ET):** Proceed to **Failure Branch B** (Even if no orders exist, do not reissue mid-session).
    *   If **Outside Market Hours:** Proceed to Step 3.

### Step 3: Initiate Dual-Verification (Compliance)
Contact the trader’s Desk Supervisor via phone or verified messaging channel.
*   **Script:** *"This is [Your Name] from MSP Support. We are processing a hardware key replacement for Trader [Name]. I need to verify your identity as the Desk Supervisor to authorize this compliance event."*
*   **Action:**
    1.  Supervisor confirms trader identity.
    2.  Supervisor confirms they authorize the key replacement.
    3.  **Record:** Note Supervisor's Name and Timestamp.
*   **Failure:** If Supervisor is unavailable, **STOP**. Proceed to **Failure Branch C**.

### Step 4: Provision New Key
1.  Log into the OMS Admin Console.
2.  Select the Trader Profile.
3.  Navigate to **Authentication > Hardware Keys**.
4.  Select **Deactivate Old Key** (to invalidate the lost token).
5.  Select **Provision New Key** for the new physical device.
6.  Assign the new key ID to the trader profile.

### Step 5: Test and Document
1.  Hand the new key to the trader.
2.  Have the trader log into the OMS to confirm access is restored.
3.  **Audit Log:** Immediately create a ticket/entry in the Compliance System documenting:
    *   Trader Name
    *   Supervisor Name
    *   Timestamp
    *   Reason for Key Loss
    *   Verification of No Open Orders (Screenshot required).

---

## Failure Branches

### Branch A: Trader Has Open Orders (During Market Hours)
**Condition:** The OMS query shows active orders while the market is open (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET).

**Procedure:**
1.  **DO NOT** proceed with key reissuance.
2.  Inform the trader: *"I cannot reissue the key while you have open orders in the system. This is a compliance and risk control requirement."*
3.  **Schedule Action:** Set a reminder for the next safe window (Market Close or Pre-Market).
4.  **Notify Supervisor:** Send an email to the Desk Supervisor stating: *"Access restoration scheduled for [Date/Time] pending market close."*
5.  **Ticket Status:** Mark the ticket as **On Hold - Compliance/Market Window**.

### Branch B: No Open Orders BUT Inside Market Hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET)
**Condition:** The trader has no orders, but it is currently trading time.

**Procedure:**
1.  **DO NOT** reissue the key. Interrupting an active OMS session during trading hours risks order state inconsistency.
2.  Inform the trader: *"Even with no open orders, we must wait for the market close to ensure session integrity."*
3.  **Schedule Action:** Set a reminder for the next safe window (After 4:00 PM ET).
4.  **Ticket Status:** Mark the ticket as **On Hold - Session Integrity**.

### Branch C: Desk Supervisor Unreachable
**Condition:** The Desk Supervisor cannot be contacted to verify identity within 15 minutes.

**Procedure:**
1.  **STOP** all troubleshooting immediately.
2.  Escalate to the **Compliance Team Lead** or **Head of Trading**.
3.  **DO NOT** attempt to bypass the two-person verification rule.
4.  **Ticket Status:** Mark ticket as **Blocked - Compliance Approval**.
5.  **Documentation:** Log the attempt and the reason for blockage in the Compliance Ticketing System.

*   **Audit Trail:** If the ticket is not logged with the Supervisor's confirmation, the firm is out of compliance. This takes precedence over ticket closure time.

**Knowledge Base Article**  
**Title:** Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading‑Floor Staff  
**Audience:** MSP Helpdesk / Service Desk – Trading‑Floor Support Team  
**Version:** 1.0 – 2025‑11‑03  

---  

## 1. Purpose  
When a trader loses the physical FIDO2 security key that authenticates access to the Order‑Management System (OMS), the trader is locked out of the platform. This procedure explains how to **re‑issue** a new key while staying compliant with the firm’s SEC/FINRA record‑keeping obligations and without jeopardising any open orders.

---  

## 2. Scope  
- Applies to **all broker‑dealer traders** who use a hardware‑based FIDO2 key for OMS authentication.  
- Must be performed only by **authorized MSP support staff** who have completed the “Hardware‑Key Lifecycle” training.  
- Excludes end‑user self‑service actions (e.g., ordering a replacement key).  

---  

## 3. Prerequisites  

| # | Requirement | How to Verify |
|---|-------------|----------------|
| **P‑1** | **Two‑person identity verification** – the trader’s **desk supervisor** *and* a designated **IT representative** must explicitly approve the re‑issuance. | Obtain written (email or ticket comment) approval from both parties before any credential work begins. |
| **P‑2** | **No open orders** in the OMS at the time of re‑issuance (unless it is a scheduled off‑hours window). | Run the “Open‑Order Inventory” report for the trader’s account. Confirm the result is **0** open orders. |
| **P‑3** | **Current key is truly lost or unusable** – confirm the trader has reported the loss in the ticket and that the key cannot be located in a secure location (e.g., desk drawer, locker). | Review the incident ticket; check the “Key‑Loss Confirmation” field. |
| **P‑4** | **Approved time window** – if the trader currently has open orders, re‑issuance may only occur **after market close (4:00 PM ET) or before market open (9:30 AM ET)**. | Verify the market schedule; confirm the current time is outside active‑trading hours or that all open orders have been cancelled/closed. |
| **P‑5** | **Re‑issuance queue is available** – a replacement FIDO2 token must be physically stocked in the “Security‑Key Distribution” cabinet. | Check inventory system; confirm at least one spare key is present and not already allocated. |
| **P‑6** | **Secure transport** – the new key must be transferred to the trader in a **tamper‑evident envelope** with a unique serial number recorded in the asset‑management database. | Ensure the envelope seal is intact and the serial number is entered into the “Hardware‑Key Tracker.” |

*All prerequisites must be satisfied before proceeding to Step 1. If any prerequisite cannot be met, move to the **Failure Branch** section below.*

---  

## 4. Resolution Flow  

### Step 1 – Confirm Two‑Person Approval  
1. Retrieve the approval email/comments from the trader’s supervisor and the IT approver.  
2. Log the approval in the ticket (e.g., “Approved by John Doe (Supervisor) – john.doe@company.com; Approved by SARA Lee (IT) – s.lee@msp.com”).  

### Step 2 – Validate Open‑Order Status  
1. Run the OMS query: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM Orders WHERE TraderID = <TraderID> AND Status = 'Open'`.  
2. If **> 0**, verify that the market is currently **closed** (i.e., after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET).  
   - **If market is open** → **Failure Branch – Open Orders During Market Hours** (see § 6).  
   - **If market is closed** → continue to Step 3.  

### Step 3 – Retrieve a Replacement Key  
1. Pull a new FIDO2 token from the **Secure Distribution Cabinet**.  
2. Record the token’s **serial number** in the “Hardware‑Key Tracker” (include date, custodian, and trader ID).  
3. Place the token in a **tamper‑evident envelope** labeled with the trader’s name and ticket number.  

### Step 4 – Schedule Delivery / Activation  
1. Coordinate with the trader’s desk to arrange a **secure hand‑off** (e.g., courier, on‑site locker, or in‑person drop‑off).  
2. Ensure the hand‑off occurs **outside of active trading windows** (unless Step 2 confirmed a safe off‑hours window).  
3. Upon receipt, the trader must **register the new key** in the OMS (follow OMS “Add New Security Key” guide).  

### Step 5 – Verify Successful Activation  
1. Log into the OMS using the **new key** to confirm authentication succeeds.  
2. Run a quick “Credential‑Sync Check” to ensure the new key is reflected in the **User‑Device Inventory** report.  
3. Update the ticket status to **“Resolved – Key Re‑issued”** and close the incident.  

---  

## 5. Documentation & Auditing  

- Attach all approval emails, the Open‑Order report screenshot, and the **Hardware‑Key Tracker** entry to the ticket.  
- Retain the tamper‑evident envelope (sealed) for **minimum 7 years** as required by SEC/FINRA record‑keeping.  
- Log the re‑issuance event in the **Change‑Management System** with a unique change request ID.  

---  

## 6. Failure Branches  

### 6.1 Supervisor or IT Approver Unreachable  
**Symptoms:** No written approval can be obtained after a 30‑minute effort (phone, email, chat).  

**Action:**  
1. **Escalate** to the Service Desk Manager and the client’s **Trading‑Desk Supervisor** via the designated escalation channel (e.g., “TRADING‑ESCALATE” Teams channel).  
2. Do **not** proceed with re‑issuance until explicit approval is received.  
3. Document the attempted contact attempts in the ticket (time, method, outcome).  

### 6.2 Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours  
**Symptoms:** The Open‑Order Inventory query returns **> 0** open orders and the current time falls within the market session (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET).  

**Action:**  
1. **Notify** the trader that re‑issuance cannot occur until the market closes or all open orders are cancelled/filled.  
2. Offer the trader one of the following options:  
   - **Cancel/Replace** the open orders manually (if allowed) and re‑run the inventory check.  
   - **Schedule** the re‑issuance for the next safe window (after 4:00 PM ET or before 9:30 AM ET).  
3. Record the decision and any trader‑initiated actions in the ticket.  

### 6.3 No Replacement Key Available  
**Symptoms:** Inventory check shows zero spare tokens.  

**Action:**  
1. Open an **Urgent Asset‑Replenishment Request** with the Security‑Operations team.  
2. Temporarily hold the trader’s access using **temporary OTP credentials** (if policy permits) until a key is available.  
3. Do **not** issue a key from a non‑controlled source.

## 7. Glossary  

| Term | Definition |

**Title:** Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff  
**Audience:** MSP Helpdesk (Tier 1/2), On-Site Engineers  
**Client:** [Broker-Dealer Client Name]  
**Risk Level:** **CRITICAL**  
**Category:** Identity & Access Management / Trading Floor Support  

---

### 📋 Overview
This article guides support staff through the secure replacement of a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for a trader. **Strict adherence to compliance and technical constraints is mandatory.**

**Key Risks:**
*   **Compliance Block:** Reissuance without documented two-person verification violates SEC/FINRA recordkeeping obligations.
*   **Operational Risk:** Interrupting an OMS session with open orders can cause order state inconsistency and potential financial loss.

**Prerequisites:**
*   [ ] **OMS Admin Access:** You have elevated privileges in the Order Management System (OMS) to query open orders and manage credentials.
*   [ ] **Inventory:** A spare, pre-provisioned FIDO2 key is available in the secure hardware locker.
*   [ ] **Contact List:** You have the direct contact information for the Trader's Desk Supervisor and the Secondary Supervisor.
*   [ ] **Audit Tool:** You have access to the audit logging system to record the credential change.

---

### 🚀 Resolution Steps

#### Step 1: Verify Trader Identity
*   Confirm the trader's identity via biometric badge scan or manager verification on the trading floor.
*   **Action:** Create a ticket tagged `OMS-CRITICAL` and `KEY-REISSUE`.
*   **Note:** Do not proceed if the trader cannot be positively identified.

#### Step 2: Assess OMS Session State & Market Hours
*   Query the OMS for the trader's session.
*   Check for `open_orders` and current `session_status`.
*   **Check Market Hours:** Verify current time against **09:30 AM ET** and **04:00 PM ET**.

> **🛑 DECISION POINT:**
> *   **Case A:** `open_orders == 0` AND (Time < 09:30 AM ET OR Time > 04:00 PM ET) **OR** Market is closed.
>     *   ✅ **Proceed to Step 3.**
> *   **Case B:** `open_orders > 0` AND 09:30 AM ET ≤ Time ≤ 04:00 PM ET.
>     *   🛑 **STOP.** Go to **Failure Branch: Open Orders During Market Hours**.
> *   **Case C:** `open_orders == 0` AND Market is Open.
>     *   ✅ **Proceed to Step 3.**

#### Step 3: Execute Two-Person Identity Verification
Compliance requires documented verification by **both** the Desk Supervisor and IT before any action.

1.  **Supervisor Verification:**
    *   Contact the Desk Supervisor directly (phone or secure video).
    *   Request authorization: *"Confirming identity of Trader [Name]. Verifying lost key incident and authorizing reissuance per compliance policy."*
    *   **Record:** Supervisor Name, Verification Time, and Method (e.g., "Verbal via secure line, recorded per policy").
2.  **IT Verification:**
    *   A second IT engineer (or you, if policy allows independent audit logging) must verify the request in the audit tool.
    *   **Record:** IT Agent ID, Verification Time, and Audit Log Entry ID.

> **🛑 DECISION POINT:**
> *   **Case A:** Supervisor contacts you and provides authorization.
>     *   ✅ **Proceed to Step 4.**
> *   **Case B:** Supervisor is unreachable after two attempts or refuses authorization.
>     *   🛑 **STOP.** Go to **Failure Branch: Supervisor Unreachable**.

#### Step 4: Document Auditable Event
*   Log the credential change event immediately in the audit system.
*   **Required Fields:**
    *   Ticket ID.
    *   Reason: `Lost Hardware Key`.
    *   Supervisor Name & Auth Timestamp.
    *   IT Verifier Name & Timestamp.
    *   Old Key Fingerprint (if retrievable) vs. New Key Fingerprint.
*   **Note:** This log satisfies the SEC/FINRA requirement for auditable access changes. Do not proceed if the log fails to write.

#### Step 5: Reissue Hardware Key
*   Deactivate the lost key in the OMS (if the session is still active).
*   Physically hand the new FIDO2 key to the trader.
*   Instruct the trader to insert the key and test authentication immediately.
*   **Validate:** Ensure the trader successfully logs in and the OMS dashboard loads without errors.

#### Step 6: Close & Notify
*   Update the ticket with the completion time and audit log reference.
*   Notify the Desk Supervisor that access has been restored.
*   Remind the trader of key handling procedures.
*   **Escalation Check:** If this is a repeat loss for the same trader, flag the ticket for Compliance review.

---

### ⚠️ Explicit Failure Branches

#### ❌ Failure Branch: Open Orders During Market Hours
**Scenario:** The trader has active orders working in the OMS and the market is currently open (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM ET).

*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** attempt to reissue or deactivate the key.
    2.  **Inform Trader:** "Your key cannot be replaced while you have open orders. Interrupting your session risks order state errors. Please close or manage your orders."
    3.  **Notify Supervisor:** Alert the Desk Supervisor that the trader is locked out due to open orders and advise them to manage the workload.
    4.  **Ticket Management:**
        *   Set Ticket Status to `HOLD - WAIT FOR MARKET CLOSE`.
        *   Add comment: *"Reissuance blocked. Trader has open orders. Market hours active. Will proceed after 04:00 PM ET or upon order clearance."*
    5.  **Re-evaluation:**
        *   If the trader clears orders mid-session, check OMS again. If `open_orders == 0`, proceed to Step 3.
        *   If orders persist until close, resume workflow immediately after 04:00 PM ET.

#### ❌ Failure Branch: Supervisor Unreachable / Authorization Denied
**Scenario:** You cannot reach the Desk Supervisor, or the Supervisor declines to authorize the reissuance.

*   **Action:**
    1.  **DO NOT** proceed with reissuance. This is a hard compliance block.
    2.  **Inform Trader:** "Access replacement is paused pending supervisor verification."
    3.  **Escalate:**
        *   Contact the **Desk Head** or **Secondary Supervisor** immediately.
        *   If both are unreachable, escalate to the **MSP Account Lead** and **Client Compliance Officer** via the emergency escalation channel.
    4.  **Ticket Management:**
        *   Set Ticket Status to `BLOCKED - COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION`.
        *   Add comment: *"Reissuance blocked. Supervisor [Name] unreachable/unresponsive. Escalated to [Name/Title] at [Time]."*
    5.  **Resolution:**
        *   Wait for explicit authorization from a higher authority or the Supervisor.
        *   If the Supervisor becomes available, log the authorization and proceed to Step 3.
        *   If the Supervisor is unavailable for >30 minutes, require sign-off from the Compliance Officer before proceeding.

---

### 📝 Notes for Helpdesk
*   **Never** bypass the two-person verification, even if the trader is the Firm's CEO or high-priority desk. SEC/FINRA records are immutable and audited.
*   If a trader claims they "already spoke" to the supervisor, you must verify this yourself. Do not rely on trader testimony.
*   Ensure the **audit log** is generated *before* the key is physically handed over. The timestamp of the log entry serves as proof of authorization.
*   **Market Hours:** Be aware of early closures, holidays, or pre-open auctions which may shift the 09:30/04:00 boundaries. Consult the client's internal trading calendar if uncertain.

## Description
This article details the procedure for replacing a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for Trading-Floor staff accessing the Order Management System (OMS).

**WARNING:** Trading staff are completely locked out of the OMS without their key. This process involves strict regulatory controls and market-risk dependencies. **Never** bypass dual-verification or order-state checks. Failure to follow this procedure may result in regulatory violations, financial loss due to inconsistent order states, or breach of client trust.

---

## Prerequisites
*Before initiating this process, verify the following conditions are met. If any prerequisite fails, refer to the **Failure Branches** section.*

1.  **Replacement Hardware:** A provisioned FIDO2 security key is physically available and ready for deployment.
2.  **Dual-Control Availability:**
    *   The Trader's **Desk Supervisor** is reachable and available to verify identity.
    *   An **IT Representative** (MSP or Client-side) is available to act as the second control/witness.
3.  **Trader Presence:** The trader must be physically present to present government-issued ID and receive the new key.
4.  **OMS Risk Assessment:**
    *   You have queried the OMS to confirm the trader has **zero open orders**.
    *   **OR** The current time is outside market hours (before 9:30 AM ET or after 4:00 PM ET).
5.  **Ticket Documentation:** A service ticket has been created with the description `SEC-SENSITIVE: OMS Key Replacement`.

---

## Resolution Steps

### Phase 1: Identity Verification & Dual Control
*Regulatory Requirement: SEC/FINRA recordkeeping mandates documented two-person verification for OMS credential changes.*

1.  **Contact the Trader:** Confirm the key is lost (not merely misplaced). Ask the trader to remain at their desk.
2.  **Contact Desk Supervisor:**
    *   Call the trader's designated Desk Supervisor.
    *   Verify the supervisor's identity via the client's internal directory.
    *   Request explicit authorization for key replacement and identity verification.
3.  **Execute Dual-Verification:**
    *   Have the **Desk Supervisor** and **IT Representative** meet at the trader's workstation (or via secure verified video call if remote protocol allows).
    *   Both parties must visually verify the trader's government-issued photo ID against the client's employee records.
    *   Both parties must verbally confirm: *"I verify the identity of [Trader Name] and authorize the reissuance of OMS credentials."*
4.  **Log Verification:**
    *   Record names, times, and confirmation statements in the ticket.
    *   Tag the ticket with `#SEC-AUDIT-VERIFIED`.

### Phase 2: OMS Risk Assessment
*Operational Requirement: Interrupting a session with open orders can cause inconsistent order states.*

5.  **Check OMS Order State:**
    *   Query the OMS backend or dashboard for the trader's user ID.
    *   Check for **Open Orders**.
6.  **Evaluate Timing:**
    *   Check current time against market hours: **9:30 AM ET – 4:00 PM ET**.

> **DECISION POINT:**
> *   If **No Open Orders** → Proceed to Step 7.
> *   If **Open Orders Exist** AND Time is **Outside** Market Hours → Proceed to Step 7.
> *   If **Open Orders Exist** AND Time is **Inside** Market Hours → **STOP. Go to FAILURE BRANCH A.**

### Phase 3: Key Reissuance Execution
7.  **Revoke Lost Key:**
    *   In the OMS Identity Provider, revoke the lost FIDO2 key credential.
    *   *Note:* This action is irreversible. Ensure you are in a safe window per Phase 2.
8.  **Provision New Key:**
    *   Assign the replacement FIDO2 key to the trader's OMS account.
    *   Configure the key to the specific user profile; do not use a generic/service account.
9.  **Physical Handoff:**
    *   Hand the new key to the trader.
    *   Verify the key is not damaged.
10. **Authentication Test:**
    *   Ask the trader to authenticate to the OMS immediately.
    *   Confirm successful login and that the session reflects the correct user profile.
11. **Post-Change Validation:**
    *   Monitor the OMS for 5 minutes to ensure no latency or session errors.
    *   Have the Desk Supervisor sign off in the ticket that the trader is operational.

### Phase 4: Audit & Closure
12. **Final Documentation:**
    *   Attach copies of ID verification logs.
    *   Record the serial number of the new key and the timestamp of revocation.
    *   Add note: `Reissuance completed. Dual verification by [Supervisor] and [IT Rep]. OMS state confirmed clean. SEC/FINRA audit trail complete.`
13. **Close Ticket:**
    *   Mark as Resolved.
    *   Alert the Client Compliance Officer if required by the client's internal SLA for audit notifications.

---

## Failure Branches

### FAILURE BRANCH A: Open Orders Detected During Market Hours
**Condition:** OMS shows open orders AND current time is between 9:30 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET.

1.  **DO NOT REVOKE THE KEY.** Interrupting the session now risks the order landing in an inconsistent state, potentially causing financial loss or regulatory reporting errors.
2.  **Notify Trader:** Inform the trader the replacement cannot proceed until the market closes.
3.  **Schedule Hold:**
    *   Update the ticket status to `HOLD - MARKET RISK`.
    *   Set a reminder/alert for **4:05 PM ET** (Post-Close).
    *   Instruct the trader to remain logged in if possible, or advise them not to attempt re-authentication until cleared.
4.  **Resume Process:**
    *   At 4:05 PM ET, re-verify that all orders have closed or been cancelled.
    *   Once confirmed, proceed to **Phase 3, Step 7**.

### FAILURE BRANCH B: Supervisor Unreachable or Refusal
**Condition:** Desk Supervisor cannot be contacted, cannot verify identity, or denies authorization.

1.  **STOP IMMEDIATELY.**
2.  **Escalate:**
    *   Escalate to the **Client CISO** or **Compliance Officer**.
    *   State clearly: `Dual-control verification failed. Supervisor unreachable/refused. Process halted pending Compliance override.`
3.  **No Workarounds:**
    *   Do not proceed based on verbal authorization from the trader alone.
    *   Do not proceed based on IT-only verification.
    *   Reissuance is strictly prohibited without documented two-person verification.
4.  **Document:** Log the escalation details and wait for explicit instructions from Compliance before taking any further action.

## Title: Replacing a Lost Hardware Security Key for Trading-Floor Staff

### Overview
This article outlines the procedure for replacing a lost FIDO2 hardware security key for trading-floor staff at our broker-dealer client. Losing a hardware security key locks the trader out of the Order-Management System (OMS), necessitating a swift yet compliant replacement process. Due to regulatory compliance (SEC/FINRA) and system integrity considerations, specific steps and verifications are mandatory before reissuance.

### Prerequisites
Before initiating the replacement process, the following must be verified:

1. **Identity Verification**: Confirmation from both the trader's desk supervisor **and** an IT representative that the request is legitimate. This two-person verification is a compliance requirement for any credential reissuance.
2. **Open Orders Check**: Ensure the trader has **zero open orders** in the OMS. If there are open orders, the reissuance must be scheduled post-market close (4:00 PM ET) or pre-market open (9:30 AM ET) to prevent inconsistent order states.
3. **Availability of Documentation**: All necessary documentation for the audit trail, including the trader's identification, supervisor's approval, and IT authorization, must be ready.

### Resolution Steps

1. **Initiate Verification Process**
   - The trader contacts their desk supervisor to initiate the verification process. The supervisor acknowledges the request and confirms the trader's identity.
   - Simultaneously, the trader or supervisor contacts the IT department to inform them of the lost key and the intention to replace it.

2. **Two-Person Identity Verification**
   - Both the desk supervisor and an IT representative must independently verify the trader's identity through established secure channels (e.g., internal secure messaging, phone call with predefined security questions).
   - Document this verification process, noting the date, time, and participants involved, for audit purposes.

3. **Check for Open Orders**
   - The IT department checks the OMS for any open orders under the trader's account.
   - **If there are no open orders**, proceed to step 4.
   - **If there are open orders**, proceed to the failure branch below.

4. **Reissue Hardware Security Key**
   - Upon successful verification and confirmation of zero open orders, the IT department reissues a new FIDO2 hardware security key to the trader.
   - The trader is guided through the setup process for the new key, ensuring it is correctly registered with the OMS.
   - Update the audit trail with the details of the reissuance, including the new key's serial number and the trader's acknowledgment of receipt and setup.

5. **Post-Reissuance Verification**
   - The trader logs into the OMS using the new hardware security key to confirm functionality.
   - The IT department and the desk supervisor are notified of the successful login to close the incident.

### Failure Branches

#### Failure Branch 1: Supervisor Unreachable
- **Action**: If the desk supervisor cannot be reached within a reasonable timeframe (e.g., 1 hour), the IT department must escalate the issue to the Compliance department for alternative verification methods. Document all attempts to contact the supervisor.

#### Failure Branch 2: Trader Has Open Orders During Market Hours
- **Action**: If the trader has open orders during market hours, the reissuance process is paused. The trader is informed of the need to wait until after market close (4:00 PM ET) or before market open (9:30 AM ET) for the key replacement.
- **Follow-Up**: The IT department schedules the reissuance for the next available compliant window. The trader is advised to monitor their open orders and contact IT once all orders are closed or the market hours window is reached.

### Conclusion
Replacing a lost hardware security key for trading-floor staff requires a careful balance between swift action and strict adherence to compliance and system integrity protocols. By following the outlined steps and addressing any failure branches appropriately, the helpdesk team can ensure a secure and compliant reissuance process.
