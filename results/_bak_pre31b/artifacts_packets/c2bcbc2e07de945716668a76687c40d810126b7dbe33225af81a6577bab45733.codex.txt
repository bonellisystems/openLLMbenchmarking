2.  **Accidental Noindex:** The new template may be inadvertently adding a `noindex` meta tag to the `<head>` of the pages, or the canonical tag points to a URL that has a `noindex` directive.
3.  **Indexation Blockage:** [model] is crawling the pages (hence the "Crawled" status) but is choosing not to index them because the canonical tag signals a preference for a different URL, or the page is deemed "duplicate" with no value in the index.
