# Panel Judge -- Battery 1 Business Task Scoring

## Role

You are one of three independent judges (Claude, Codex, Gemini) scoring a set
of blinded answers to a single MSP-realistic business task. You do not know
which model produced which answer -- every answer below is labeled only by a
letter. Do not try to guess model identity from writing style. Score only
what is on the page against the anchors below.

## Anchors

The following anchors define what a 0, 3, 5, 7, and 10 look like for this
business unit. Use them as the scoring scale for every answer.

# SEO -- scoring anchors (0-10)

Scale: integers 0-10. Anchors define 0/3/5/7/10; use 1-2/4/6/8-9 for answers between anchors.

## 10 -- Frontier
Technical recommendations are correct for the stated platform (canonical tags, redirects, schema, robots directives used properly), keyword strategy is matched to actual search intent, and every recommendation is prioritized by expected impact versus effort. No black-hat tactic is suggested, and the answer is calibrated about ranking-factor uncertainty rather than asserting exact algorithm weights as fact.
Example character: a prioritized fix list starting with the highest-impact technical issue, each item tied to a specific, correctly-described mechanism.

## 7 -- Strong
The technical guidance is sound and usable for the stated platform, and keyword direction matches intent. It is missing an explicit effort-versus-impact prioritization, or one recommendation is generic rather than platform-specific, but nothing here is technically wrong.
Example character: correct on-page and technical fixes listed without ranking them by which matters most.

## 5 -- Adequate
The advice is SEO-fundamentals-correct (use target keywords, build backlinks, improve page speed) but reads as a generic checklist not tied to the specifics of the site or platform given.
Example character: a standard keyword-and-backlink checklist with no reference to the site's actual issues.

## 3 -- Weak
Contains outdated or incorrect technical claims (keyword stuffing helps rankings, more backlinks always help regardless of quality), or recommends tactics unrelated to the actual ask.
Example character: advice built around an outdated ranking factor treated as still authoritative.

## 0 -- Unusable
Recommends black-hat or manipulative tactics (cloaking, link farms, hidden text, doorway pages) as safe practice with no penalty-risk warning, or fabricates specific ranking-factor claims as verified fact. Empty or off-task.

## Unit-specific red flags (deduct hard)
- Black-hat tactics (cloaking, PBNs, hidden text, keyword stuffing) recommended without a penalty-risk warning.
- Fabricated or overconfident claims about exact Google ranking-factor weights.
- Technical errors in canonical, redirect, or robots guidance that would deindex pages.
- Ignoring a stated platform or CMS constraint.

## Unit-specific excellence markers
- Recommendations prioritized by effort versus expected impact.
- Correct technical implementation detail for the stated platform (canonical, redirects, schema, robots).
- Keyword strategy matched to actual search intent, not just volume.
- Honest flag of algorithm-update or ranking-factor uncertainty.

This Local SEO plan is designed to close the competitive gap between the clinic and the top three local competitors by focusing on authority (reviews), relevance (profile completeness), and trust (NAP consistency).

### **Phase 1: Foundational Trust (NAP Consistency)**
*Priority: High | Timeline: Immediate (Week 1)*

Search engines use "citations" to verify a business is real and reliable. Inconsistencies create "data friction," which can lower rankings.

1.  **Immediate Correction:**
    *   **Yelp:** Log in and update the old address to match the GBP and website exactly.
    *   **Healthgrades:** Update the old phone number to match the current clinic line.
2.  **Deep Audit:** Perform a manual search for "[Clinic Name] [City]" to identify other rogue directories (Yellow Pages, Bing Places, Apple Maps) containing old information.
3.  **Standardization:** Ensure the clinic uses the exact same format across all platforms (e.g., if the website uses "St." instead of "Street," use "St." everywhere).

### **Phase 2: GBP Optimization (Profile Completeness)**
*Priority: High | Timeline: Weeks 1–3*

A 40% complete profile tells [model] the business is inactive. We will optimize for "Relevance" so the clinic shows up for specific services.

1.  **Information Completion:**
    *   **Service Menu:** Populate the "Services" section with specific keywords: *Wellness Exams, Vaccinations, Dental Cleaning, Spay/Neuter, Urgent Care, and Senior Pet Care.*
    *   **Hours of Operation:** Input all standard hours and specifically add **Holiday Hours** for the next 12 months to prevent customer frustration.
    *   **Attributes:** Select relevant attributes (e.g., "Appointment required," "Wheelchair accessible").
2.  **Visual Trust Building:**
    *   **The "Rule of 10":** Upload at least 10 new high-resolution photos:
        *   3 Exterior shots (to help clients find the building).
        *   3 Interior shots (waiting room and exam rooms to reduce anxiety).
        *   4 "Happy Patient" shots (staff interacting with pets to build emotional connection).
3.  **Q&A Implementation:** Use the "Ask a Question" feature to post and answer 3-5 frequently asked questions (e.g., *"Do you offer emergency services after hours?"*).

### **Phase 3: Reputation Engine (Review Generation)**
*Priority: Ongoing | Timeline: Months 1–6*

To move from a 4.1 to a 4.6, we need a high volume of *recent* 5-star reviews to dilute the older lower ratings.

1.  **The "Post-Visit" SMS/Email Workflow:**
    *   Integrate a review request into the clinic’s workflow. 24 hours after an appointment, send an automated, personalized text or email: *"Hi [Pet Parent Name], thank you for trusting [Clinic Name] with [Pet Name]! We'd love to hear about your experience: [[model] Review Link]."*
2.  **In-Clinic "Frictionless" Capture:**
    *   **QR Code Signage:** Place small, branded signs at the checkout desk that say: *"Loving [Clinic Name]? Scan to tell us how we did!"* with a direct link to the GBP review window.
3.  **Staff Incentive Program:**
    *   Create a monthly "Review Bonus." The staff member mentioned by name in a new 5-star review receives a small reward ($10 gift card or lunch). This encourages staff to ask for reviews during moments of high client satisfaction.
4.  **Review Management:**
    *   **Respond to 100% of reviews:** Respond to every new review (positive or negative) within 48 hours. For negative reviews, respond professionally: *"We strive for better care; please contact us at [Phone] so we can address this."*
