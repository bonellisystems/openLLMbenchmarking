### 1. Bottleneck Driver Identification
**Actual Bottleneck:** **Weekly Batch Ingestion via Shared Inbox.**

*   **Analysis:** While the 30% missing-document bounce rate adds significant rework, the **weekly inbox check frequency** is the structural bottleneck.
    *   The weekly check imposes a deterministic, mandatory delay on **100%** of applications. A packet cleared on Tuesday faces a 6-day wait; the average wait is ~3.6 days.
    *   The bounce rate is probabilistic (30% occurrence) and adds ~1.5 days on average ($0.3 \times 5$ days).
    *   The weekly check also likely delays *scheduling* itself (e.g., a packet arriving Thursday sits until the next Monday for scheduling), compounding the delay beyond just the "first look" time.
    *   **Conclusion:** The handoff mechanism creates a systemic "pull" delay that affects all volume, whereas the missing document issue is an intermittent error. Fixing the ingestion cadence yields a larger, guaranteed reduction in cycle time than fixing the bounce rate alone.

### 2. Redesigned Handoff Mechanism
**Solution: Shared "Handoff Drop-Box" + Pre-Handoff Quality Gate**

Since no new software or headcount is available, the redesign leverages existing digital infrastructure (e.g., SharePoint/Network Drive or Outlook Shared Folder) and process changes to eliminate the email delay and enforce quality.

*   **Mechanism:**
    1.  **Replace Email with Shared Drop-Box:** Planning uploads the complete packet (PDF + Site Survey) to a designated shared folder accessible by Building.
    2.  **Automated Alert:** Existing tools are configured to send an automatic email alert to Building staff when a new file is uploaded, enabling **Daily Triage** rather than weekly checks.
    3.  **Pre-Handoff Quality Gate:** Planning must complete a mandatory **Digital Checklist** (Word/Excel template) prior to upload. The checklist requires a specific field: *"Site Survey Attached? [Yes/No]."*

*   **Why this works:**
    *   **Eliminates Wait:** Daily alerts and folder access reduce the "wait to open" from ~3.6 days to ~0.5 days.
    *   **Eliminates Bounces:** The checklist forces Planning to verify the site survey before the packet enters the Building queue, reducing bounces to near zero.
    *   **Zero Cost:** Uses existing file storage, email alerts, and document templates; requires no new licenses or hiring.
